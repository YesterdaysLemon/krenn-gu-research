## Why r=+-1 collapse: the reconstruction mechanism

At r=1 the D_23-weighted three-row subconfiguration of planes
U_0,U_1,U_2 becomes a pure P_3-type compression: all its mixed
weighted 3x3 permanents vanish on Phi=0, only the all-beta one (=4)
survives.  The single-1-word own-extension coefficients ARE these
permanents — that is exactly why three of the four elimination
denominators carry (r-1) — and the surviving all-beta permanent turns
the y_3 extension slot into a universal pure-reconstruction kernel
direction.  The same mechanism at r=-1 kills a whole alpha/beta slot
pair.  This is the component-8 analogue of the six-dimensional
component's equal-weight collapse
(P5_H22_SIX_DIMENSIONAL_EQUAL_WEIGHT_BINARY_OBSTRUCTION.md) and of the
q=1 ubiquitous-kernel story of
P5_H31_SIX_DIMENSIONAL_COMPONENT_GENERIC_OBSTRUCTION.md.
