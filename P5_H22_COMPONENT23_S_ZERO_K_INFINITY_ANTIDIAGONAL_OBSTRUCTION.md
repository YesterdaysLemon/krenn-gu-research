
## Exact antidiagonal geometry

Put

```text
A=X0+X1,   C=X0-X1,   B=X2+X3,   D=X2-X3.
```

The finite corner rows restrict on (1) to

```text
alpha=(A,D,B+rD,B-rD),
beta =(B,B,C,C).                                   (2)
```

All sixteen restricted tensor coefficients vanish except

```text
T1111=-4.                                          (3)
```

In edge order `01,02,03,12,13,23`, the generic pair profile is

```text
(3,3,3,3,3,4).                                    (4)
```

The nonzero edge-`23` four-minors are, up to sign,

```text
16 r(r^2+1),                                       (5)
```

and their exact gcd is the same polynomial.  Thus the only rank drop on the
punctured divisor is

```text
r^2=-1,
```

where the profile is `(3,3,3,3,3,3)`.  The proof below localizes only at
`r`, so it covers these two all-rank-three points exactly.

Every local plane in (2) has rank two.  Once `alpha_i` is fixed, every
genuine marked basis is represented by

```text
marked_i=beta_i+h_i alpha_i.                       (6)
```

The omitted projective marking is proportional to `alpha_i` and is not a
basis.

## Complete shared-binary obstruction

Let `x=(x0,...,x3;y0,...,y3)` be the common fifth-coordinate extension.
For each of the labelled contractions `D01,D23`, expand all sixteen binary
permanent coefficients and retain the fourteen mixed equations.  Write the
four pure diagonals as

```text
(A01,A23,B01,B23).                                 (7)
```

Because the inherited nonzero coefficient is the all-beta word, genuine
weighted-`H22` incidence requires

```text
B01 B23 !=0,       and at least one of A01,A23 is nonzero.   (8)
```

Split (8) into its two alpha charts.  In chart `j=01,23`, normalize

```text
Aj=1,
```

invert `B01 B23`, and impose the exact localization `u r-1=0`.  For finite
weight `[lambda:1]`, the ideal generated by the twenty-eight mixed
equations and these three chart equations has reduced standard basis

```text
<1>                                                (9)
```

in both alpha charts.  The same is true at projective weight `[1:0]`.
Consequently there is no genuine shared-binary incidence, so no ternary
weighted-`H22` lift can exist.

## Opposite-normalization audit

The no-import audit reverses the projective normalization rather than
replaying the same chart equations.  It sets

```text
B01=1,
```

inverts `B23`, and separately inverts `A01` or `A23`, again with `r`
inverted.  All four resulting finite/projective standard bases are exactly
`<1>`.  Thus (9) is not an artifact of selecting an alpha diagonal as the
projective scale.

No factor `r^2+1`, `lambda-1`, or `lambda+1` is inverted in either replay.
The rank-drop points and finite endpoint weights are therefore part of the
theorem, not generic specializations.

## Nonredundancy

On `r!=0`, the antidiagonal is disjoint from the coordinate axes and from
the diagonal `r=t`.  It meets `rt=1` only at the retained points
`r^2=-1`.  Thus this theorem closes a genuine finite-interior divisor rather
than restating an axis, diagonal, or `rt=1` result.
