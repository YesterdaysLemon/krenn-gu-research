## Universal kernel and diagonal obstruction

Let \(M\) be the fourteen-by-eight finite-\(D_{23}\) mixed matrix.  At
\(\rho=-1\), direct exact multiplication gives

\[
 M k=0,
 \qquad
 k=(-1/D,0,0,0,(1-h_0)/D,0,0,1)^T.
\]

On the line \(x=\tau k\), both endpoint coefficients vanish:

\[
 A(\tau k)=B(\tau k)=0.
\]

Consequently, wherever \(M\) has rank seven, its kernel is exactly \(Kk\)
and neither endpoint normalization is possible.
