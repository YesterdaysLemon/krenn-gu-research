# Face-specific quotient descent and a sharp common-edge lift

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero overlap theorem with a sharp symbolic boundary.**
For the prescribed faces

```text
F_01=1234a,       F_02=1235b,       F_12=1345b,
```

the smaller terminal-incidence quotients glue by a canonical
Mayer--Vietoris exact sequence.  The projections of each physical core edge
also agree in every union quotient.  Consequently, whenever two projected
face equations are nonzero, their forced rank-one edge lines must either
both die in the union quotient or descend to the same projective line.

This is the coordinate-free gluing invariant supplied by the face overlaps.
It does not by itself obstruct the formal three-face data.  An explicit
rational seven-mode incidence system has zero overlap defect, is sharp in
each of the three binary face quotients, attains the earlier three-high-rank
global bound, and admits one common family of core-edge tensors satisfying
every nonzero union-line compatibility.

Thus face-span descent and common-edge projective lines alone cannot improve
the existing quotient rank bound or prove nonrealizability.  Residual
five-row permanents, lower degrees, or the unprojected face equations are
still required.  The sharp lift below is not asserted to realize the three
full prescribed response tensors.

No graph family, support, colour word, alignment, or parameter space is
enumerated.

## 1. The three smaller quotient systems

Work over a characteristic-zero field `K` containing `rho`, with
`rho^2=21`.  At core mode `i`, let `V_i^*` be the physical local covector
space and let

```text
U_i(S)=span{R_(i,p): p in S},
Q_i(S)=V_i^*/U_i(S).                                  (1)
```

For the three prescribed faces, exact terminal Wick inversion gives

```text
Phi_F01=(1+43rho/21)D_0-6D_1,
Phi_F02=rho D_0+2(1+rho)/7 D_2,
Phi_F12=rho D_1+2(1+rho)/7 D_2.                       (2)
```

All six displayed coefficients are nonzero.  If `q_i^F` denotes the map to
`Q_i(F)`, projecting the physical one-edge expansion at two core modes
`i,j` kills every core edge except `A_ij`.  Hence, for the colour pair
`cd` belonging to `F_cd`,

```text
dim span{
 q_i^F(epsilon_i^c) tensor q_j^F(epsilon_j^c),
 q_i^F(epsilon_i^d) tensor q_j^F(epsilon_j^d)
} <= 1.                                               (3)
```

Unlike the full-terminal quotient, the three instances of (3) live in
different spaces.  Their legal comparison is the descent construction
below.

## 2. Terminal-span Mayer--Vietoris sequence

Fix two terminal sets `F,G`.  Write

```text
O=F intersect G,          H=F union G,
X_i(F,G)=(U_i(F) intersect U_i(G))/U_i(O).            (4)
```

The space `X_i(F,G)` is the overlap defect: it records coincidences between
the two incidence spans that are not generated by shared terminals.

### Theorem 1 (face-quotient descent)

For every physical incidence system and every core mode there is a canonical
exact sequence

```text
0 -> X_i(F,G) -> Q_i(O)
   -> Q_i(F) direct_sum Q_i(G) -> Q_i(H) -> 0.        (5)
```

The middle maps are

```text
[v]_O -> ([v]_F,[v]_G),
([x]_F,[y]_G) -> [x-y]_H.                             (6)
```

In particular, if `X_i(F,G)=0`, then `Q_i(O)` is exactly the fibre product
of `Q_i(F)` and `Q_i(G)` over `Q_i(H)`.

Proof.  The kernel of the first map in (6) is
`(U_i(F) intersect U_i(G))/U_i(O)`.  A pair `([x]_F,[y]_G)` maps to zero
exactly when `x-y=u_F+u_G`.  Then `z=x-u_F=y+u_G` maps to that pair.  The
last map is surjective because every class `[x]_H` is the image of
`([x]_F,[0]_G)`.

For the three faces in (2), the overlap/union table is

```text
F_01,F_02:       O=123,        H=P,
F_01,F_12:       O=134,        H=P,
F_02,F_12:       O=135b,       H=P minus {a}.          (7)
```

This exact sequence is valid over any field.  Characteristic zero enters the
application only through the nonzero coefficients in (2).

## 3. The common-edge projective gluing condition

Fix a core pair `e={i,j}`.  Let

```text
B_e^F=(q_i^F tensor q_j^F)(A_ij),
t_e^(F,c)=q_i^F(epsilon_i^c) tensor q_j^F(epsilon_j^c). (8)
```

Let `P_e^F` be the five-row terminal permanent tensor on the complementary
core modes.  The projected physical equation is

```text
B_e^F tensor P_e^F
 =lambda_c^F t_e^(F,c) tensor E_bar(e)^c
  +lambda_d^F t_e^(F,d) tensor E_bar(e)^d.             (9)
```

The two residual pure tensors on the right are independent.  Therefore, if
the right side of (9) is nonzero, then

```text
L_e^F=span{t_e^(F,c),t_e^(F,d)}
```

is a line and `B_e^F` is a nonzero generator of that line.

For `H=F union G`, let `s_e^(F,H)` be the tensor product of the two natural
quotient maps.  Because all face projections come from the same physical
edge tensor,

```text
s_e^(F,H)(B_e^F)
 = (q_i^H tensor q_j^H)(A_ij)
 = s_e^(G,H)(B_e^G).                                  (10)
```

### Corollary 2 (union-line test)

If the projected right sides for `F` and `G` are both nonzero, then the
images of `L_e^F` and `L_e^G` in
`Q_i(H) tensor Q_j(H)` are either both zero or are the same nonzero line.

This is intrinsic: it uses only the incidence-span diagram, the colour
axes, and the fact that one physical `A_ij` lies behind all faces.  It does
not choose bases in any quotient.

## 4. A zero-defect sharp incidence lift

The union-line test is consistent with the sharp quotient boundary.  Take
`V_i^*=E_i=<e_i^0,e_i^1,e_i^2>` for seven modes `i=0,...,6`.  All incidence
covectors are zero except

```text
R_(0,5)=e_0^2,
R_(1,4)=e_1^1,
R_(2,2)=e_2^0,
R_(d,1)=e_d^1,   R_(d,3)=e_d^2       for d=3,4,5,6.  (11)
```

The resulting face kernels `K_i^F=E_i intersect U_i(F)` are

```text
mode       K^F01          K^F02          K^F12          K^P
0          0              <e^2>          <e^2>          <e^2>
1          <e^1>          0              <e^1>          <e^1>
2          <e^0>          <e^0>          0              <e^0>
3,4,5,6    <e^1,e^2>      <e^1,e^2>      <e^1,e^2>      <e^1,e^2>. (12)
```

Every incidence vector shared by two or three face spans in (11) is attached
to an actual terminal in their set-theoretic overlap.  Consequently

```text
U_i(F) intersect U_i(G)=U_i(F intersect G),
intersection_(F in {F_01,F_02,F_12}) U_i(F)=U_i(13).  (13)
```

for all three face pairs and every mode.  All 21 pairwise overlap defects in
(5), as well as all seven triple-overlap defects, vanish; this model has
exact, not merely lax, descent.

For `F_01`, only mode 0 has rank two on the colour plane `<e^0,e^1>`.
For `F_02`, only mode 1 has rank two on `<e^0,e^2>`.  For `F_12`, only mode
2 has rank two on `<e^1,e^2>`.  Thus every binary face condition (3) is
sharp.  In the full-terminal quotients the ranks are

```text
(2,2,2,1,1,1,1),                                     (14)
```

with the three rank-two supports `01,02,12`.  Hence the earlier tricolour
at-most-three theorem is also attained.

## 5. One common edge family passes every union-line test

The lift can retain the common physical edge tensors rather than specifying
three unrelated face lines.  Put

```text
A_01=e^0 tensor e^0,
A_02=e^1 tensor e^1,
A_12=e^2 tensor e^2,                                  (15)

A_0d=A_1d=e^0 tensor e^0                 (3<=d<=6),
A_dd'=e^0 tensor e^0                     (3<=d<d'<=6),
A_2d=0                                    (3<=d<=6).  (16)
```

For every core pair and every face with nonzero projected diagonal side,
the projection of (15)--(16) is a nonzero generator of `L_e^F`.  Whenever
two faces are simultaneously nonzero, their line images in the union
quotient are nonzero and equal.  There are seventeen such face-pair/core-pair
comparisons; the primary and independent replays check all of them exactly.

This is stronger than three unrelated abstract quotient models:

- the quotients come from seven labelled physical incidence covectors;
- every pairwise overlap square and the triple overlap have zero defect;
- all face lines are projections of the same `A_ij` at each core pair;
- the facewise and full-terminal rank bounds are simultaneously sharp.

### Theorem 3 (sharpness of face-overlap descent)

The exact-sequence constraints (5), the facewise rank-one laws (3), and the
common-edge union-line conditions (10) do not improve the full-quotient
bound of three high-rank modes and do not contradict the three prescribed
colour-pair patterns.

Proof.  Equations (11)--(16) are an explicit simultaneous lift attaining all
four properties above.

## 6. Exact boundary

Proved:

- the universal face-span exact sequence (5);
- the coordinate-free common-edge identity (10) and union-line test;
- zero-defect descent for an explicit seven-mode incidence system;
- simultaneous sharpness of all three facewise binary bounds;
- simultaneous sharpness of the global three-high-rank bound;
- a common core-edge tensor family passing every active union-line test.

Not proved:

- that (11)--(16) realizes the residual permanent tensors in (9);
- that it realizes any full prescribed face response in (2);
- compatibility with degrees one or three or with the complete ledger;
- that no stronger invariant using the five-row permanents exists;
- the `P_7 -> Delta_3` restriction or the global Krenn--Gu conjecture.

The conclusion is therefore precise:

```text
face-span Mayer--Vietoris descent:     EXACT;
common-edge union-line test:           EXACT;
zero-defect sharp lift:                EXISTS;
overlap constraints alone obstruct:   NO;
full physical three-face lift:         UNKNOWN.       (17)
```

The next viable overlap object must retain `P_e^F` in (9), rather than only
its rank-one edge line.  Equivalently, it must glue the residual permanental
