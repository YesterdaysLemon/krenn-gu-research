## Status

word.**  Continue the six-parameter degree-five family in
`P7_221_ALIGNED_CORE_DEGREE5_AFFINE_COMPLETION.md`.  That family satisfies all
twenty prescribed degree-five mixed-zero faces for the alignment

```text

(z_1,z_2) -> (ell,h_3),
(z_3,z_4) -> (h_5,h_a),
(z_5,z_6) -> (f_2,h_4)                                (1)
```


the three faces `124,125,12a` already generate the unit ideal.

This excludes the aligned completion (1)--(2) with the fixed pure scalar
certificates.  It is not an obstruction to other alignments, other mixed
words, or other scalar principal-hafnian realizations of the formal ledger.

## 1. The degree-five parameter space

Put `rho^2=21` and abbreviate




For a surviving terminal triple `S`, the Wick-deconvolved response is


## 2. Three normalized face equations

Write

```text

```


```text

lambda_12a=-(1380+624rho/7)/(2rho+105).                (9)
```

Every multiplier in (9) is nonzero in `Q(rho)`.  Dividing by them gives the


The contradiction is already visible: `f_124=f_125=0` forces
`q=delta` and `p=0`, whereas `f_12a=0` would then require

## 3. Exact unit-ideal certificate

More strongly, the normalized polynomials obey

```text

```

The three relevant field elements are nonzero because their quadratic norms
are

```text

Norm(alpha)=521/21,
Norm(C)=2364964/49=4*591241/49.                        (12)
```

Multiplying (11) by `C/(delta^2 alpha)` yields the explicit

```text

```

Thus the ideal generated by the 35 degree-three responses contains one.
It has no zero over `Q(rho)` or over any field extension, including the
algebraic closure.

## 4. Why these mixed coefficients must vanish

For the mixed core word (2), let `F_S` denote the full frozen-terminal
cofactor coefficient and `Phi_S` its no-terminal-edge Wick quotient.  Every
prescribed cofactor tensor is a sum of pure blocker-colour tensors, so every
mixed coefficient `F_S` is zero.

For `|S|=3`, the exact inverse Wick relation

```text
Phi=E_(-M)F                                             (14)
```

uses only mixed coefficients `F_T` with `T subset S` of sizes three and one.
Their complementary deletion sets have sizes four and six, all prescribed
by the formal ledger.  Hence the required tensor realization would have

```text
Phi_124=Phi_125=Phi_12a=0.                             (15)
```

The corresponding deletion faces are

```text
P\124=35ab,        P\125=34ab,        P\12a=345b.    (16)
```

Equations (13)--(16) are incompatible.

### Theorem 1 (single-alignment degree-three obstruction)

No choice of the six free parameters (4), over `Q(sqrt(21))` or its
algebraic closure, extends the exact degree-five family (5) through all
degree-three mixed-zero equations for the alignment (1) and word (2).

The exact degree-one point found previously does not alter this conclusion:
the degree-three unit certificate holds on the entire degree-five family,

## 5. Sharp scope

The theorem proves only:

```text
fixed alignment (1), word (2), degree-five faces: REALIZED;
same alignment and word, degree-three faces:       EXCLUDED;
solutions over algebraic closure:                  NONE.      (17)
```

It does not prove that every alignment or mixed word is obstructed, that all
scalar realizations of the ledger have an analogous circuit, or that the
formal 2+2+1 ledger is globally impossible.  Tangent-jet compatibility, a
universal tensor `P_7` obstruction, and the Krenn--Gu conjecture remain
unresolved.
