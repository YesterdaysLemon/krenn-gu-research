# P7 primitive Boolean squares have a dual triangle and star normal form

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero reduction of the last full-support pinned
branch.**  Work in

```text
A=K[z_0,...,z_7]/(z_0^2,...,z_7^2),
ell=z_0+...+z_7,
Q=sum_(i<j) b_ij z_i z_j,                 (1)
```

over a characteristic-zero field.  Assume every `b_ij` is nonzero.  The
remaining P7 pinned condition

```text
ell Q^2=0                                             (2)
```

has the following sharper exact form.  Put

```text
r_i=sum_(j!=i) b_ij.                                  (3)
```

Then (2) is equivalent to the 56 **dual triangle equations**

```text
b_ij r_k+b_ik r_j+b_jk r_i
 =2(b_ij b_ik+b_ij b_jk+b_ik b_jk)       (i,j,k distinct).   (4)
```

At least one `r_i` is nonzero on the edge torus.  After naming such a vertex
`0`, every off-star edge obeys

```text
[r_0-2(b_0j+b_0k)] b_jk
 =2b_0j b_0k-b_0j r_k-b_0k r_j.                      (5)
```

Thus, away from the 21 displayed denominator divisors, all 21 off-star
edges are rational functions of the seven star edges and eight row sums.
After common projective scaling this is a 14-parameter star/row-sum chart,
rather than the original `P^27`.  The row-sum closure equations and the 35
triangles not through `0` still have to be imposed; the parameter count is
a birational ambient reduction, not a dimension claim about the solution
locus.

On an exceptional divisor the corresponding numerator must vanish as well.
The exceptional branch is therefore explicit, not discarded.  In addition,
every solution satisfies the complementary-hafnian law

```text
haf B[U]=haf B[V minus U]                  (|U|=4).    (6)
```

The reciprocal-rank-one stratum is empty on the torus: there are no nonzero
`u_i` with `1/b_ij=u_i u_j` satisfying (2).

This note does **not** prove that the full primitive Boolean-square torus is
empty and does not construct a torus point.  It replaces the 56 five-set
equations by a local row-sum system, proves that a nonzero-row star chart
always exists, gives its generic rational reconstruction and exact boundary,
and eliminates one natural reciprocal-rank-one stratum.  P7 full-support
rank drop remains **UNKNOWN**, and global Krenn--Gu remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 1. Boolean `sl_2` duality at the middle level

Let `U` be multiplication by `ell` and let `D` be its transpose in the
square-free monomial bases:

```text
U:A_4 -> A_5,      D:A_4 -> A_3,
(Df)_R=sum_(v notin R) f_(R union {v}).               (7)
```

The Boolean algebra carries the standard characteristic-zero `sl_2`
action generated by `U`, `D`, and their degree commutator.  At middle degree
four, both maps kill exactly the trivial `sl_2` summand, which as an `S_8`
module is the Specht module of shape `(4,4)`.  Hence

```text
ker(U:A_4->A_5)=ker(D:A_4->A_3),
dim ker U=70-56=14.                                  (8)
```

This Boolean/zero-square `sl_2` viewpoint is part of the established
representation-theoretic interface; see Philip Feinsilver,
[Representations of sl(2) in the Boolean lattice, and the Hamming and
Johnson schemes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0368).  The citation supplies
terminology and context only.  Equality (8), including the exact middle
kernel used here, is proved in the next paragraph and independently replayed
below.

This can also be seen from the Boolean flag decomposition: on every flag
quotient indexed by `j<4`, the raising and lowering scalars are nonzero;
on the `j=4` quotient they both vanish.  This is an equality of
characteristic-zero subspaces, not a finite-field rank observation.

Write

```text
H_U=haf B[U]
   =b_ab b_cd+b_ac b_bd+b_ad b_bc       (U={a,b,c,d}). (9)
```

The coefficient of `z_U` in `Q^2` is `2H_U`.  Therefore (2) says `UH=0`.
By (8), it is equivalent to `DH=0`.

## 2. Derivation of the triangle equations

For a fixed triple `{i,j,k}`, expand its down equation:

```text
0=sum_(l notin {i,j,k}) H_{ijkl}
 =b_ij sum_l b_kl+b_ik sum_l b_jl+b_jk sum_l b_il
 =b_ij(r_k-b_ik-b_jk)
  +b_ik(r_j-b_ij-b_jk)
  +b_jk(r_i-b_ij-b_ik).                              (10)
```

Collecting the pair products gives exactly (4).  Conversely, (4) for all
triples is `DH=0`, so (8) gives `UH=0`, hence (2).  Thus no information is
lost in passing from five-sets to triangles.

The same middle Specht module is fixed by four-set complementation.  Indeed,
a spanning `(4,4)` polytabloid has the form

```text
product_(a,b in a tableau column) (z_a-z_b).          (11)
```

Complementing a monomial reverses the choice in each of four columns and
introduces the sign `(-1)^4=1`.  Schur irreducibility, or the polytabloid
spanning set directly, shows that complementation is the identity on (8).
Applying this to `H` proves (6).

## 3. A nonzero row sum must exist

Suppose, for contradiction, that `r_i=0` for all eight vertices.  Since all
edges are nonzero, divide (4) by `2b_ij b_ik b_jk` and put
`c_ij=1/b_ij`.  Every triangle then gives

```text
c_ij+c_ik+c_jk=0.                                    (12)
```

This is the two-subset/three-subset inclusion map
`W_(2,3)(8)c=0`.  It is injective in characteristic zero.  For completeness,
if `R` is the unsigned vertex-edge incidence matrix of `K_8`, then

```text
W_(2,3)^T W_(2,3)=4I+R^T R,
R R^T=6I+J.                                          (13)
```

The Gram eigenvalues are `18` once, `10` seven times, and `4` twenty times.
They are all nonzero, so (12) forces `c=0`, contradicting `c_ij!=0`.
Consequently some `r_i` is nonzero on every hypothetical torus solution.

## 4. The star/row-sum chart and its boundary

Relabel a vertex with nonzero row sum as `0` and put `a_j=b_0j`.  Equation
(4) for the triangle `{0,j,k}` is precisely (5).  Define

```text
Delta_jk=r_0-2(a_j+a_k),
N_jk=2a_j a_k-a_j r_k-a_k r_j.                       (14)
```

On the open set `Delta_jk!=0` for every `j<k` in `{1,...,7}`,

```text
b_jk=N_jk/Delta_jk.                                  (15)
```

The coordinates `(r_0,...,r_7,a_1,...,a_7)` number 15, and common scaling
removes one, giving the stated projective 14-parameter ambient chart.  Its
exact closure system consists of

```text
r_0=sum_j a_j,
r_j=a_j+sum_(k!=0,j) N_jk/Delta_jk,                  (16)
```

together with (4) for the 35 triples among `{1,...,7}` and the requirements
that all reconstructed edges be nonzero.  These equations can have
dependencies; no dimension or nonemptiness assertion is inferred from
their raw count.

If `Delta_jk=0`, equation (5) forces `N_jk=0`:

```text
r_0=2(a_j+a_k),       a_j r_k+a_k r_j=2a_j a_k.      (17)
```

The edge `b_jk` is then controlled only by the remaining row-sum and triangle
equations.  Formula (17) is the exact coordinate-boundary branch that must
be retained in any future elimination.

## 5. Reciprocal rank one is impossible

Assume `c_ij=1/b_ij=u_i u_j` with every `u_i` nonzero.  Dividing (4) by the
three edge products gives

```text
r_i c_ij c_ik+r_j c_ij c_jk+r_k c_ik c_jk
 =2(c_ij+c_ik+c_jk).                                 (18)
```

After substituting `c_ij=u_i u_j` and dividing by `u_i u_j u_k`, this is

```text
t_i+t_j+t_k=0,       t_i=r_i u_i-2/u_i,              (19)
```

for every triple.  Subtracting two triples shows all `t_i` are equal, and
then characteristic zero gives `t_i=0`.  Put `v_i=1/u_i` and
`S=sum_i v_i`.  Since `b_ij=v_i v_j`, equations (3) and (19) give

```text
v_i(S-v_i)=2v_i^2,       so S=3v_i for every i.       (20)
```

Thus all `v_i=S/3`.  Summing over eight vertices gives `S=8S/3`, hence
`S=0` and then every `v_i=0`, a contradiction.  This excludes the entire
reciprocal-rank-one torus stratum without specializing parameters.
