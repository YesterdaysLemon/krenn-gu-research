# Strict two-endpoint companions synchronize the top face, but only on the shore line

## Status

**Exact conditional characteristic-zero theorem and legal-response
counterboundary.**  In the all-axis root branch, impose the strict
two-endpoint companion hypothesis: differentiated roots can use tangent-only
root--root edges or the two residual endpoints `q_0,q_1`, but no blocker,
frozen-root, or additional nonroot companion channel.

Under this hypothesis, the singleton equations force the two endpoint forms
at every root to be a tangent basis.  It follows that the residual-absent and
residual-present companion forms of **every root pair are independent**.
Consequently the marked-shore theorem really does expose a synchronized
pair of top responses

```text
m_W=haf(B[W]),             z_W=haf(G[Q union W])      (1)
```

with the same nonzero shore scalar.

This is not the synchronization required by the full corrected-pair
determinant theorem.  The marked shore is enforced by restricting every
port in `W` to a common root-null subspace, generically a line.  The legal
observation therefore gives only the top response on those shore-null
directions.  It does not expose a `3 x 3` mixed-colour pair block or a
partition-closed deletion cube.

An exact `h!=0` family of legal two-residual responses has fixed
`(m_W,z_W,h)` and fixed corrected top value `z_W-hm_W`, while a lower direct
pair and a lower corrected pair vary reciprocally.  Thus even perfect
two-selector recovery of the top face cannot license the subtraction and
determinant argument at a hidden lower pair.

The full `T_Q-hT_0` determinant remains **CONDITIONAL**.  No unconditional
`P_7` exclusion or Krenn--Gu theorem is claimed.

## 1. Strict two-endpoint tangent companions

Let the root tangent covector at root `i` be a coordinate axis

```text
a_i=e_(alpha_i)^*,          S_i=ker a_i.              (2)
```

The two residual endpoints induce tangent forms

```text
p_i=B_(i,q_0)(-,z_0)|_(S_i),
q_i=B_(i,q_1)(-,z_1)|_(S_i).                          (3)
```

Assume:

1. differentiated root--blocker edges vanish on `S_i`;
2. a root--root tangent block is effective only when both of its roots are
   varied;
3. no frozen root or nonroot vertex other than `q_0,q_1` is an effective
   tangent companion; and
4. all singleton and pair root jets equal their exact GHZ values.

These are the strict common-companion assumptions.  They are stronger than
the general lower-jet theorem, which permits additional deletion classes.

### Lemma 1 (endpoint forms are bases)

For every root `i`, the two forms `p_i,q_i` are linearly independent in
`S_i^*`.

Proof.  The singleton GHZ derivative on `S_i` has root-flattening rank two:
the two target coordinate forms whose colours differ from `alpha_i` form a
basis of `S_i^*`.  Under the strict hypothesis, the graph singleton
derivative has root-side span contained in `span{p_i,q_i}`.  Equality with
the rank-two target forces that span to have dimension two.

## 2. Every root pair has two independent distinguished companions

For distinct roots `i,j`, write the tangent root--root form as `L_ij`.  The
two even-parity companion forms are

```text
g_empty=L_ij,
g_Q=h L_ij+R_ij,
R_ij=p_i tensor q_j+q_i tensor p_j.                  (4)
```

In the ordered bases `(p_i,q_i)` and `(p_j,q_j)`, the matrix of `R_ij` is

```text
J=[0 1]
  [1 0],                                              (5)
```

so `R_ij` has bilinear rank two.

### Theorem 2 (strict companion independence)

For every all-axis root pair, `g_empty` and `g_Q` are linearly independent.

Proof.  Suppose they were dependent.  Equation (4) would make `L_ij`
proportional to the nonzero rank-two form `R_ij`; this also covers
`L_ij=0`, because then the companion line is generated by `R_ij`.

If `alpha_i=alpha_j`, the GHZ pair derivative has a two-dimensional
root-side span, generated by the two surviving pure-colour products.  One
companion line cannot equal it.

If `alpha_i!=alpha_j`, only the third colour survives.  The GHZ root-side
span is then generated by one decomposable, bilinear-rank-one product.  It
cannot equal a line generated by the rank-two form `R_ij`.  Both cases are
contradictions.

The theorem uses both the strict topology and the exact singleton values.
Without the singleton equations, the endpoint forms need not be bases;
with additional companion classes, the target rank need not be carried by
the distinguished empty/`Q` pair.

## 3. What the marked shore now exposes

Use the canonical blocker profile

```text
012, 01,01, 02,02, 12,12.                            (6)
```

The marked permanental Laplace theorem supplies a root triple `J`, a
three-blocker shore `D` containing the triple blocker, and its complementary
root pair `I=R minus J`, such that

```text
f=per H[J,D] !=0,          W=B minus D,       |W|=4. (7)
```

Every blocker in `W` is double type.  The pair jet is

```text
T_I=g_empty tensor C_I+g_Q tensor C_(I union Q).      (8)
```

By Theorem 2, dual legal root probes select the two displayed cofactors.
After forcing `J` onto `D`, they give

```text
f z_W,                    f m_W,                     (9)
```

respectively.  Both values use the same shore matching, the same physical
port graph, and the same nonzero scalar `f`.  Therefore

```text
f(z_W-hm_W)                                             (10)
```

is a legal synchronized **top-face** observation.

This removes the companion-rank proviso from the clean-window theorem only
inside the strict all-axis two-endpoint model.  In the general P7 lower-jet
problem, other companion columns remain allowed and Theorem 2 does not
apply.

## 4. The shore-line limitation

The shore is forced by restricting every `w in W` to

```text
L_w=intersection_(j in J) ker r_(j,w),                (11)
```

where `r_(j,w)` is the root--blocker row at `w`.  Double type gives
`L_w!=0`, but no current theorem gives `L_w=V_w`.  On the exact rank-two
double-type stratum,

```text
dim L_w=1.                                            (12)
```

Accordingly, (9) exposes the restrictions

```text
m_W|_(tensor_(w in W)L_w),
z_W|_(tensor_(w in W)L_w),                            (13)
```

or their values at chosen nonzero shore vectors.  If an open port is moved
outside `L_w`, a root in `J` may match that port and the common forced shore
is lost.

For two line-restricted ports, the restriction map from a `3 x 3` bilinear
block to its shore value has rank one and an eight-dimensional kernel.
Thus (13) cannot recover the six off-diagonal mixed-colour entries, or even
one full corrected pair matrix.  Three diagonal pure charts do not repair
this: the mixed-pair determinant is a minimal nine-coordinate circuit, and
the diagonal-chart interpolation theorem makes the three diagonal shadows
independent.

Hence top synchronization and full pair synchronization are different
observability statements.

## 5. Exact `h!=0` top-face fibre

Work in the commuting square-zero port algebra on
`W={1,2,3,4}`.  Fix `h!=0` and let `a!=0`.  Define

```text
M_a=1+a x_3 x_4,
Phi_a=h+a^(-1)x_1x_2,
Z_a=M_a Phi_a
   =h+h a x_3x_4+a^(-1)x_1x_2+x_1x_2x_3x_4.         (14)
```

This is an honest two-residual response.  Install exactly the scalar edges

```text
B_34=a,
B_(q_0,q_1)=h,
B_(q_0,1)=1,
B_(q_1,2)=a^(-1),                                    (15)
```

and set every other edge to zero.  The port-only Wick response is `M_a`.
With both residuals present, they either pair together, giving `h`, or use
ports `1,2`, giving `a^(-1)x_1x_2`; multiplication by `M_a` proves (14).

For every `a`,

```text
m_empty=1,          z_empty=h,
m_1234=0,           z_1234=1,
n_1234=z_1234-hm_1234=1,                              (16)
```

while

```text
m_34=a,             n_12=z_12-hm_12=a^(-1).          (17)
```

Thus all synchronized top data in (16) are constant, but a lower direct
pair and a lower corrected pair vary.  Their product is the visible top
insertion `m_34 n_12=1`.

The scalar construction embeds into three-dimensional physical ports by
placing its edge forms on chosen shore lines and extending by zero.  It is a
legal graph-response countermodel to top-face inference, not a full coloured
P7 or GHZ construction.

## 6. General observation-kernel warning

Outside the strict model, even the distinguished companion columns need not
be independently observable.  If a rank-one pair jet has

```text
g_empty=g_Q=g,                                        (18)
```

then only `C_I+C_(I union Q)` is visible, and

```text
(C_I,C_(I union Q))
 ->(C_I+S,C_(I union Q)-S)                            (19)
```

is an exact invisible deformation.  If the target root rank is two, an
additional companion column may carry the second direction while (19)
remains invisible.  Consequently a general lower-frame rank theorem does
not by itself select these two particular deletion classes.

## 7. Exact sufficient hypotheses still missing

The full corrected-pair determinant would become legal if one proved all of
the following for one common chart.

1. **Private companion selectors.**  In the observation matrix containing
   every effective companion class, the coordinate rows selecting `C_I` and
   `C_(I union Q)` both lie in the legal row space.
2. **Full-dimensional shore preservation.**  After one common nonzero shore,
   the two selected responses remain multilinear on a chart containing
   three independent directions at each of two open blocker modes.  Merely
   knowing a nonzero shore line is insufficient.
3. **Synchronized physical data.**  The two depths retain the same root
   cofactors `F_uv` and direct blocks `B_uv`, so subtraction really gives
   `W_uv-hB_uv=D_uv` term by term.
4. **Residual-null complement.**  The other blocker modes admit common-null
   contractions killing every competing corrected pair.
5. **Nonresonant target.**  The three corrected target diagonal entries are
   nonzero after the same contraction.

Under these hypotheses, the synchronized two-depth polar-selector theorem
applies and the selected `3 x 3` corrected block has rank at most two while
the target contraction has rank three.

Current results establish strict companion independence and the same scalar
top shore, which are portions of items 1 and 3.  They do not establish item
2, partition closure, or a mixed-colour left inverse.  Therefore the
determinant remains conditional.
