# Fixed-order P3 restrictions

This package owns two exact restriction results for the order-three tensor
over `C`: the decomposable-restriction classification and the zero-hyperplane
product theorem, together with their existing primary verifiers and audits.

They are shared structural antecedents used by P4 and P5 work.  Colocation
here does not make either result arbitrary-order, assign it to one downstream
consumer, prove a P4/P5 case cover, or resolve the global conjecture.  The
global Krenn--Gu status remains **UNRESOLVED**.
