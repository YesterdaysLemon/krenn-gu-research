# Complete marked-fibre obstruction on the internal `E=0` divisor

## Status

This is an exact characteristic-zero obstruction.

The coupled three-plane toric base of the known pure rank-two
component has one divisor inside the preferred component chart:
`E=0`, with toric normal `(-1,0,0)`.  Its pure slice is a secant line,
so it has two reduced pure directions.  The earlier canonical-section
theorem excluded one marking.

The complete marked-basis fibre is now excluded.  Both pure
directions, all three all-rank distinguished coordinates `q=0,2,3`,
both projective charts of the first-plane fibre, every kernel-row
shift, and every binary `Delta_2` extension direction are obstructed.

Together with the finite-family, genuine toric-boundary, nonzero
component-chart divisor, and first-plane-infinity theorems, this closes
the complete marked fibre of the known pure-compression component.
A second irreducible pure-compression component has since been proved;
only one rational marked fibre on it is currently excluded.  Its
generic and boundary fibres, any further component, and the separate
`H22` branch remain unresolved.  This is not a global solution of the
prize problem.

## Toric-Segre reduction

The three monomial plane maps of
[`P4_PURE_RANK_TWO_COMPONENT_TORIC_BOUNDARY.md`](../../../p4/classifications/pair-geometry/pure-rank-two/boundaries/P4_PURE_RANK_TWO_COMPONENT_TORIC_BOUNDARY.md)
restrict at `E=0` to a secant line of the Segre variety.  In the
canonical slice coordinates its two pure directions are

```text
(-1,-1),       (1,-1).
```

For both directions, deletion has rank two in all last-three-plane
factors exactly for

```text
q=0,2,3.
```

The first plane is a projective line over that base point.  If
`k0,k1` span its kernel fibre and `p` is the selected pure lift, use

```text
finite chart:
  alpha0=k0+r k1,
  beta0=p+s k1+t0 alpha0;

infinity chart:
  alpha0=k1,
  beta0=p+s k0+t0 alpha0.
```

The remaining three planes have all markings

```text
beta_i=beta_i^0+t_i alpha_i.
```

Thus the parameters `(r,s,t0,t1,t2,t3)` cover the full marked plane
bundle; no marked basis is fixed by hand.

## Exact binary projections

For each direction, distinguished coordinate, and first-plane chart,
form the fourteen mixed binary equations in an eight-entry extension
vector `z`.  Saturate by the two diagonal coefficients

```text
d_A(z)d_B(z)!=0
```

and eliminate `z`.  The twelve characteristic-zero projection ideals
are:

```text
direction (-1,-1)

q=0 finite:    <t3-1,t2,t1 r,t0 t1>
q=0 infinity:  <s,t3,t2,t1,t0>

q=2 finite:    <t3-1,t2,t1(s+1),t1 r,t0 t1>
q=2 infinity:  <t2,t1,s(t3-1),t0(t3-1)>

q=3 finite:    <t2,t1(s+1),t1 r,t1(t3-1),t0 t1,
                 (t3-1)(t0 r+s+1)>
q=3 infinity:  <t3,t2,t1,s(t0+1)>

direction (1,-1)

q=0 finite:    <t1,t2 s,t2 r,t2 t3,t0 t2,
                 t3(t0 r+s)>
q=0 infinity:  <t3-1,t2,t1,t0 s>

q=2 finite:    <t3,t1,t2 s,t2 r,t0 t2>
q=2 infinity:  <t2,t1,t3 s,t3(t0-1)>

q=3 finite:    <t3,t1,t2 r,t0 t2>
q=3 infinity:  <s,t3-1,t2,t1,t0-1>.
```

Their squarefree component decompositions have 24 components.

## Residual atlas

The 24 projection components split into 29 rational kernel charts.
The extra charts isolate the only kernel-pivot divisors:

```text
s=0, t3=0, t3=1, t0=-1, t0=-2.
```

One chart is a projection-closure artifact: one diagonal coefficient
vanishes identically, so it contains no binary `Delta_2` extension.

On every ordinary chart, write an arbitrary extension in an exact
basis of `ker M_q`.  For mode `i` and a selected four-row minor
`Delta_i`, form

```text
(entry of the pure distinguished column) *
Delta_i/(d_A d_B).
```

Rows are numbered `000,001,...,111` as `0,...,7`.  It suffices to use
the ten row sets

```text
0137, 0147, 0157, 0237, 0247,
0267, 0357, 0367, 0457, 0467
```

in the four modes.  On each chart, the ideal generated by the nonzero
residual products becomes the unit ideal after inverting `d_A`,
`d_B`, and the chart parameters.  These are exact no-common-zero
computations over `Q`, not samples.

One coupled component,

```text
direction=(-1,-1), q=3, finite chart:
t1=t2=0, s=-1-t0 r,
```

is more efficient as a direct selected-minor calculation.  The four
certificates

```text
(3;0237), (2;0457), (3;0267), (1;0137)
```

make the saturated characteristic-zero obstruction ideal equal to
`<1>`.

Therefore every genuine binary extension has an injective neighbouring
one-marked map with a transverse pure coordinate.  As in the previous
marked-fibre theorems, the third target row must then vanish on the
neighbouring hyperplane and on the remaining pure coordinate.  It
vanishes globally, contradicting target rank three.
