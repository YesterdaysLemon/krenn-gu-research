# First/second-component provenance: disputed ownership

Status: **NEUTRAL COLOCATION; SCIENTIFIC DISAGREEMENT UNADJUDICATED**

This is a narrow, neutral colocation package for the H31 artifacts whose
first/second-component provenance cannot honestly be assigned to one of the
existing component-owned packages during root exit.  It records the
disagreement; it does not adjudicate it.

The package contains exactly two evidence units:

- `marked-basis-fibre-classification/`: the exact characteristic-zero
  marked-basis classification/obstruction, its primary verifier, modular
  audit, and directly owned proof-producing elimination generator; and
- `diagonal-quadric-outer-boundary/`: the exact characteristic-zero
  diagonal-quadric outer-boundary theorem, primary verifier, and independent
  audit.

The local scopes are not themselves ambiguous.  The marked-basis theorem is
an exact first/known-finite-chart result, while its scope remainder records the
second complete fibre as open.  The outer-boundary theorem is exact on the
second-component outer boundary and, together with separately owned
normalized-affine results, records the presently known second component as
closed.  The disagreement is therefore in the cross-document remainder and
provenance account, substantially inherited from commit `60a885ca`; recency
does not adjudicate it.

All seven artifacts retain their committed mathematical status, scope,
assumptions, provenance, and asymmetric primary/audit roles.  Colocation does
not make either evidence unit a premise of the other, does not decide whether
historical first/second-component labels should control broader ownership,
and does not reconcile complete-closure language across marked-basis, toric,
high-coordinate, root navigation, and synthesis records.

The marked-basis elimination generator is proof-producing support for its
primary, not an independent route.  Its audit is modular QA and is reused by
descendant audits.  The outer-boundary audit imports the component-point audit
and supplies finite-field QA; it is not the characteristic-zero proof.  These
evidence qualifications remain unchanged by colocation.

Those broader records remain external dependencies in their actual owners;
an import, link, or historical hash is an evidence/provenance edge, not a
membership claim.  In particular, the P4 decomposable/pure-rank-two and
diagonal-quadric packages; marked-basis-open-branch; toric, internal-`E=0`,
component-fibre-infinity, and chart-boundary units; diagonal-quadric
point/curve/`E`/`H0`/pure-direction/normalization families; H22 first-rank-two
consumers; and the high-coordinate/frontier, root README, alternative map,
boundary atlas, Delta3 ledger, and migration reports are not members of this
neutral package.

This directory is neither an archive nor a catch-all.  It changes no
mathematics and supplies no missing component-exhaustiveness argument.  The
global Krenn--Gu conjecture remains **UNRESOLVED**.
