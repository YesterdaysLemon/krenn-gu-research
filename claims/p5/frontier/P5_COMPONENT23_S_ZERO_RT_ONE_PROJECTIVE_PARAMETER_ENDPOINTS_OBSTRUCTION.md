# Component twenty-three `s=0, rt=1` projective-parameter endpoint obstructions

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero normalized-endpoint theorem.**  The two
projective parameter endpoints of component twenty-three's finite-`k` face

```text
s=0,       rt=1
```

have empty complete affine-marked `H31` fibre and empty complete homogeneous
weighted-`H22` fibre in the fixed normalized contraction order.  The proof
includes every finite `k`, the further rank drop at `k=0`, every marked basis,
every source-coordinate insertion, every finite weight, and projective weight.
Everything is over `Q`; no finite-field calculation is used.

This is a theorem only for the displayed normalized endpoint families.  It
does not cover arbitrary ambient/source bases, arbitrary contraction order,
other projective component charts, the local-to-global reduction, or the
global Krenn--Gu conjecture, which remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## Exact endpoint families

Put

```text
A=X0+X1,   C=X0-X1,   B=X2+X3,   D=X2-X3.
```

On the affine part of the face the rows are

```text
alpha=(A,A+kD,B+rD,B+D/r),
beta =(B,B,   C,    C).
```

At `r=infinity,t=0`, rescale `alpha2` by `1/r`; at
`t=infinity,r=0`, rescale `alpha3` by `1/t`.  The exact endpoints are

```text
E_r: alpha=(A,A+kD,D,B),   beta=(B,B,C,C),
E_t: alpha=(A,A+kD,B,D),   beta=(B,B,C,C).          (1)
```

Thus `E_t` is obtained from `E_r` by the explicit mode permutation
`2<->3`.  The verifiers nevertheless reconstruct both families separately.
All sixteen pure coefficients vanish except

```text
T1111=-4.                                           (2)
```

In edge order `01,02,03,12,13,23`, the pair profiles are

```text
E_r, k!=0: (3,2,3,3,4,3),    E_r, k=0: (3,2,3,2,3,3),
E_t, k!=0: (3,3,2,4,3,3),    E_t, k=0: (3,3,2,3,2,3).       (3)
```

Every displayed plane has rank two.  Replacing one `beta_i` by the omitted
projective marking `alpha_i` makes the inherited pure coefficient zero.
Consequently every genuine marked basis is represented by

```text
beta_i -> beta_i+h_i alpha_i.                       (4)
```

## Complete marked `H31` fibre

For source-coordinate deletion `d`, let `M_d(h)` be the fourteen binary
mixed equations in the eight extension entries.  Normalize the alpha
diagonal and invert the beta diagonal.  Exact elimination gives

```text
              d=0,1                         d=2,3
E_r:     <h0,h1,h2>                  <h0,h1,h2 h3>
E_t:     <h0,h1,h3>                  <h0,h1,h2 h3>.          (5)
```

Both inclusions are checked, so these coordinate marking lines are the
complete binary survivor set.

### Insertions `d=0,1`

On the surviving line write its marking coordinate as `p`.  The mixed
matrix has rank six and nullity two.  With kernel parameters `c0,c1`, its
diagonals are

```text
A_d=2 c0 k,
B_0=-2(c0 p+2c1),       B_1=+2(c0 p+2c1).          (6)
```

Thus a genuine binary extension already forces `k!=0`.  For `E_r`, the
mode-zero pure-plus-neighbour stack has, on rows `01279`, determinant

```text
d=0: +16 c0^2 k^4(c0 p+2c1),
d=1: -16 c0^2 k^4(c0 p+2c1).                      (7)
```

For `E_t` use rows `0127,10`; the two signs in (7) reverse.  These
determinants are nonzero on the genuine diagonal open, forcing the third
local target row to vanish.

### Insertions `d=2,3`, punctured marking lines

On either punctured component of (5), write the nonzero marking coordinate
as `p`.  The mixed matrix has rank four and nullity four.  In the exact
kernel coordinates used by the verifiers its diagonals, up to a harmless
sign depending on endpoint, insertion, and marked mode, are

```text
A_d = +/- 2(c0 p-c3)/p,       B_d=-2(c1+c2).       (8)
```

For `k!=0`, the ideal of all maximal minors of the mode-zero
pure-plus-neighbour stack, saturated by

```text
k p (c0 p-c3)(c1+c2),
```

is the unit ideal.  At `k=0`, the ideal generated by the maximal minors of
all four such stacks, saturated by

```text
p (c0 p-c3)(c1+c2),
```

is again the unit ideal.  These eight endpoint/insertion/marking checks are
performed independently.  Hence at least one required third row is forced
to zero on every genuine punctured branch, including `k=0`.

### The common marking intersection

At `h=0`, the mixed kernel for `d=2,3` has parameters `c0,...,c3` and

```text
A_2=+/-2(c0-c1),      A_3=2(c0+c1),
B_2=B_3=-2(c2+c3).                                  (9)
```

For `k!=0`, the saturated maximal-minor ideal of the mode-zero stack is the
unit ideal.  At `k=0`, the mode-zero and mode-one stacks have rank exactly
four on the genuine open.  Their polynomial kernel generators are,
respectively,

```text
E_r,d=2: (0,0, 1,-1,c3), (0,0, 1,-1,c2),
E_r,d=3: (0,0,-1, 1,c3), (0,0,-1, 1,c2),
E_t,d=2: (0,0, 1,-1,c3), (0,0, 1,-1,c2),
E_t,d=3: (0,0,-1, 1,c3), (0,0,-1, 1,c2).          (10)
```

The exact rank-four statement is certified by saturating their four-minor
ideals by `A_d B_d`; the displayed nonzero generators give the matching
upper bound.  Rank three at modes zero and one therefore forces nonzero
multiples of the two rows in (10).  On the original pure hyperplane,

```text
per(gamma0,gamma1,beta2,beta3)=4                  (11)
```

for every one of the four cases.  This forbidden two-third-row coefficient
cannot vanish.  Equations (5)--(11) close the complete marked `H31` fibre.

## Complete homogeneous weighted `H22` fibre

Use the common extension for the labelled contractions `D01` and `D23`.
The inherited pure coefficient is the all-beta word, so genuineness requires

```text
B01 B23 !=0,       and at least one of A01,A23 is nonzero.   (12)
```

For each endpoint, each alpha-normalization chart, and both the finite and
projective weight charts, exact elimination gives the complete shared-binary
projection

```text
E_r: <h0,h1,h2>,             E_t: <h0,h1,h3>.      (13)
```

Thus there is a genuine binary survivor line; it must not be erased by a
false mixed-module claim.  Write its nonzero coordinate as `p` and scale the
unique mixed-kernel extension to

```text
E_r: z=(0,0,0,-1;0,1,0,0),
E_t: z=(0,0,-1,0;0,1,0,0).                         (14)
```

The combined mixed matrix has rank seven.  In diagonal order
`(A01,A23,B01,B23)`, the finite-weight values are

```text
(2k(lambda+1), -2(lambda-1),
 2p(lambda-1), -2(lambda+1)),                      (15)
```

and the projective-weight values are

```text
(2k,-2,2p,-2).                                     (16)
```

Hence finite genuineness is exactly `p(lambda-1)(lambda+1)!=0`, while
projective genuineness is exactly `p!=0`; `k` remains arbitrary.

These binary incidences do not extend to ternary local maps.  Form the
sixteen one-third-row equations obtained from both contractions.  At mode
one the rows `0678,14` for `E_r` and `0578,13` for `E_t` give determinant

```text
-8 p(lambda-1)^3(lambda+1)^2                      (17)
```

at finite weight, and determinant `-8p` at projective weight.  They are
nonzero on exactly the genuine opens above, so the third row in mode one is
zero.  This contradicts the rank-three local map required by a ternary
lift.  Thus the complete homogeneous weighted-`H22` fibre is empty, even
though its necessary shared-binary incidence is not.
