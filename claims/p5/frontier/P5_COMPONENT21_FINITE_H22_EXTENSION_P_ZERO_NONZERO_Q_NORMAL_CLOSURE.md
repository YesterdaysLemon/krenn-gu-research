## Rank-drop locus

Let `M(q,kappa,ell,lambda)` be the stacked `32 x 8` extension map on (1).
For `ell*(ell^2-1)!=0`, put

```text
A=(ell+1)lambda-ell+1,
B=(ell+1)lambda+ell-1.                              (3)
```

Three exact `8 x 8` minors are

```text
rows (11,14,15,16,18,19,20,24):
 256*ell*q^3*(lambda-1)^5*(lambda+1)^2*(ell^2-1)*A,

rows (10,11,16,18,19,20,23,24):
-256*ell*q^2*(lambda-1)^5*(lambda+1)^2*(ell^2-1)*B,

rows (10,11,15,16,18,20,22,24):
 256*kappa*q^3*(lambda-1)^6*A*B.                   (4)
```

The first two cover every `lambda!=+/-1` because `B-A=2(ell-1)`;
at `lambda=-1`, the last covers `kappa!=0`.

At `ell=0`, use instead

```text
256*q^3*(lambda-1)^6*(lambda+1)^2,
-256*kappa^2*q^2*(lambda-1)^8.                     (5)
```

At `ell=epsilon`, `epsilon=+1,-1`, the minors

```text
-256*q^2*(lambda-1)^5*(lambda+1)^3,

epsilon=+1:  1024*lambda^2*kappa*q^3*(lambda-1)^6,
epsilon=-1: -1024*kappa*q^3*(lambda-1)^6           (6)
```

give the same cover.  Thus rank drop is confined to (2).

At `lambda=1` the universal kernel vector is

```text
Z=(0,0,0,0,-q,0,1,0).                              (7)
```

For `ell!=+/-1` the extension rank is seven.  Fixed rank-seven minors are

```text
-16384*ell*q^5*(ell^2-1)  if ell!=0,
-16384*q^5                 if ell=0.                (8)
```

At `ell=epsilon` the rank is six and the complete kernel plane is spanned by
`Z` and

```text
V_epsilon=(0,-epsilon,-epsilon,0,-epsilon,-1,0,1); (9)
```

a fixed rank-six minor is `-4096*epsilon*q^4`.

At `kappa=0,lambda=-1`, the complete kernel line is generated by

```text
ell!=0: W=(0,-1/ell,0,0,1/ell,0,0,1),
ell=0:  W_0=(0,-1,0,0,1,0,0,0).                   (10)
```

Fixed rank-seven minors are `16384*ell^3*q^3` for nonzero `ell` and
`-16384*q^2` at `ell=0`.  This proves that (2) is exact.

## The `lambda=1` line away from `ell=+/-1`

The complete normal along `[Z]` has the same column image as

```text
N_+=[M | partial_lambda(MZ)].                       (11)
```


The complete mixed kernel is spanned by

```text
e_b0,
-e_a0+ell e_a2+e_b1,

```

On these vectors the four diagonal rows
`D01(0000),D01(1111),D23(0000),D23(1111)` are

```text
(0,0,0,4),
(0,4q,0,4(kappa*q-ell)),

```

In particular `D23(0000)` vanishes throughout the mixed-free normal.

## The `ell=+/-1`, `lambda=1` kernel plane

Write a nonzero leading direction as

```text
H=X Z+Y V_epsilon.                                 (15)
```

The complete normal retains all thirteen subordinate-extension and
parameter-tangent columns.  If `Y!=0`, its mixed rank is six: rows
`(10,11,20,23,25,29)` and columns `(0,1,2,3,11,12)` give

```text
-4096*epsilon*Y^2*q^4.                             (16)
```

For the remaining direction `[Z]`, rows `(10,11,18,23,29)` and columns
`(0,1,2,3,12)` have determinant `512*q^3`, so the mixed rank is five.
In both cases explicit complete mixed-kernel bases have zero
`D01(0000)` and zero `D23(0000)` rows.  Thus no projective direction in
the kernel plane has both `D23` diagonals.

## The `kappa=0,lambda=-1` normals

For nonzero `ell`, the `p,q` derivatives vanish and the `ell` derivative is
absorbed by the extension columns.  The reduced complete normal is

```text
N_-=[M | partial_kappa(MW) | partial_lambda(MW)].   (17)
```

On `ell*(ell^2-1)!=0`, a fixed mixed `8 x 8` minor is

```text
32768*q^2*(ell^2-1)/ell.                           (18)
```

At `ell=0` the corresponding minor is `32768*q^2`; at each unit endpoint a
different fixed minor is again `32768*q^2`.  The complete mixed kernel is
always the span of `e_a0` and the appropriate vector in (10).  Their
diagonal images are

```text
(0,0,4,0), (0,0,0,0).                              (19)
```

Hence only `D23(0000)` can survive on these normals.

A genuine shared weighted-`H22` contraction requires the `D01` pure
diagonal and both opposite `D23` diagonals after all mixed coefficients
vanish.  Equations (14), (16), and (19) always remove one required `D23`
diagonal, so every complete first normal above is empty.

Finite marking changes act by invertible triangular transformations on the
binary coefficient rows and extension columns.  Their derivative on a
leading zero vanishes, while their derivative on the kernel direction is
absorbed by subordinate extensions.  No finite marking tangent is omitted.
