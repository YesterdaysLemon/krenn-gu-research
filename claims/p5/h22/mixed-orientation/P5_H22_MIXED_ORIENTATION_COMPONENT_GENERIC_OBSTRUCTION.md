# Generic weighted `H22` obstruction on the mixed-orientation component

## Status

This is an exact characteristic-zero obstruction on the generic
diagonal-source orbit of the mixed-orientation pure-compression
component proved in
[`P4_MIXED_ORIENTATION_PURE_COMPONENT.md`](../../../p4/components/mixed-orientation/P4_MIXED_ORIENTATION_PURE_COMPONENT.md).

For the two weighted diagonal-hyperplane pencils required by `H22`:

1. the `01` mixed-coefficient matrix has full column rank for every
   marked basis, so it has no binary extension at all; and
2. every genuine binary survivor of the `23` pencil has a rank-four
   one-marked contraction and therefore cannot extend to a ternary
   local map.

Thus a relevant pure binary plane cannot be generic on this component
in a hypothetical `H22` restriction.

This does **not** close special parameter or slope divisors, the
component's projective boundary, the other pure components, component
exhaustiveness, all of `H22`, or the global prize problem.

## Weighted diagonal pencils

Use the normalized `H22` contractions

```text
v_0=e_0+e_1,        v_1=e_2+e_3,        v_2=e_4.
```

After putting the pure `P_4` plane configuration into canonical form,
the residual diagonal source torus changes the two neighboring source
bases to

```text
D_01^r(u)=(r u_0+u_1,u_2,u_3,u_4),
D_23^r(u)=(u_0,u_1,r u_2+u_3,u_4).                 (1)
```

The slope `r` belongs in the function field; fixing it in advance
would only prove a special-orbit statement.

## The `01` pencil: a projective-kernel obstruction

The statement needed here is stronger than a saturation by the two
diagonals:

```text
ker M_01(t)=0 for every t in Kbar^4.                 (6)
```

Because (5) is homogeneous in `z`, a nonzero kernel direction would
give a point of `P^7`.  The following hierarchical affine cover proves
that this projective incidence is empty.

First normalize `x_2=1` and `x_3=1` in turn.  In each chart the exact
ideal generated by the fourteen equations is the unit ideal.  Any
remaining projective solution would therefore satisfy

```text
x_2=x_3=0.
```

On that linear subspace, normalize `y_2=1` and `y_3=1` in turn.  Both
restricted ideals are again unit, so a remaining solution would lie in

```text
x_2=x_3=y_2=y_3=0.                                  (7)
```

Finally cover the residual `P^3` by the four charts

```text
x_0=1, x_1=1, y_0=1, y_1=1.
```

All four restricted ideals are unit.  These eight exact Gröbner
certificates cover every possible nonzero `z`, proving (6).  In
particular, the `01` pencil has no binary neighbor for any marking.

This is a projective determinantal argument, not an enumeration of
markings or extension directions.

## The `23` pencil: three marking closures

For the `23` direction, impose the fourteen mixed equations, normalize
`A(t)z=1`, invert `B(t)z`, and eliminate `(z,w)`.  Five low-degree
members of the resulting exact marking ideal are

```text
t_1 t_3,
(t_0-1)t_3,
t_1((d+q)t_2-pq),
(t_0-1)(t_2+d-p),
(t_0-1)t_1.                                        (8)
```

Equations (8) place every genuine binary marking in the union of three
closed sheets:

```text
A: t_0=1, t_1=0;

B: t_0=1, t_3=0, (d+q)t_2=pq;

C: t_1=0, t_3=0, t_2=p-d.                          (9)
```

Indeed, if `t_0=1`, then either `t_1=0` and the point lies on `A`, or
`t_1!=0` and (8) forces the equations of `B`.  If `t_0!=1`, equations
(8) force the equations of `C`.  Equality with the full projected
scheme is unnecessary: the three closures in (9) already cover its
points.

## Two-minor ternary obstruction

For a ternary `H22` lift, the mode-three one-marked contraction through
the other three binary planes must have rank at most three.  Let
`N_3(z)` be its `8 x 4` coefficient matrix.  On each closure in (9),
adjoin the two minors

```text
det (N_3)_{0267},       det (N_3)_{0467}
```

to the fourteen mixed equations and saturate by
`(A(t)z)(B(t)z)`.  Exact calculation over `K` gives

```text
<M_23(t)z, equations of A,
  det (N_3)_{0267}, det (N_3)_{0467},
  w(A(t)z)(B(t)z)-1> = (1),

<M_23(t)z, equations of B,
  det (N_3)_{0267}, det (N_3)_{0467},
  w(A(t)z)(B(t)z)-1> = (1),

<M_23(t)z, equations of C,
  det (N_3)_{0267}, det (N_3)_{0467},
  w(A(t)z)(B(t)z)-1> = (1).                        (10)
```

Consequently at least one displayed minor is nonzero on every genuine
binary extension.  The one-marked map has rank four, whereas a local
map to three target coordinates has rank at most three.  No `23`
binary survivor lifts to `H22`.

Combining (6) and (10) closes both weighted diagonal directions at the
generic point of the mixed-orientation component.
