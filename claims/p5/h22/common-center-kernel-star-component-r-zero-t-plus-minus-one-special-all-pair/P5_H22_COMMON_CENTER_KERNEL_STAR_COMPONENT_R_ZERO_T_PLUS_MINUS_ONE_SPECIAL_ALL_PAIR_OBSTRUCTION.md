
```text

```

Thus edge `13` has dropped from its generic rank four to rank three, but no
pair has dropped below rank three.  Both points remain in the all-pair locus.
The pure tensor still has only its all-beta coefficient nonzero:

```text
T_0000=0,             T_1111=-4.                  (2)
```

Mark every row by `beta_i -> beta_i+h_i alpha_i`.  For the same homogeneous
weight and the same eight extension entries, form both labelled contractions

The rank change in (1) does not change this labelled shared-extension
formulation: `D01` and `D23` remain the two complementary contractions for

## Finite endpoints and projective weight

At `lambda=0`, the minor on rows `0,1,3,4,7,8,9,14` specializes to

```text
t= 1: -256*h3,
t=-1:  256*h3*(3-4*h3).                           (8)
```

The remaining row modules are full:

```text
t= 1: h3=0,
t=-1: h3=0 or h3=3/4.                             (9)
```

The other endpoint modules are identical at the two parameter points.  In
the diagonal order `(A01,B01,A23,B23)`, exact bidirectional module reductions
give

| weight | mixed-row module | diagonal membership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lambda=1` | `<e1,e2,e3,e4,e6,e7,e8>` | `(yes,yes,yes,no)` |
| `lambda=-1` | `<e1,e2,e3,e4,e5,e6,e8>` | `(yes,no,yes,yes)` |
| projective | `R^8` | all four |

Thus `B01` vanishes on every mixed kernel at `lambda=1`, while `B23`
vanishes at `lambda=-1`; either conclusion contradicts the required two
nonzero beta diagonals.  At `lambda=0` and projective weight, the full module
leaves no nonzero shared extension.  Together with (7), this closes both
homogeneous weight charts.
