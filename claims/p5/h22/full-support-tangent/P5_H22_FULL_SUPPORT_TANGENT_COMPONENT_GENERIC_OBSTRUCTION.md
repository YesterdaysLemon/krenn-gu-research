# Generic `H22` exclusion for the full-support tangent component

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero generic-fibre theorem.** The complete weighted
`H22` fibre over the generic point of pure-`P_4` component fourteen is
empty.

The proof works in the two-parameter source-torus quotient `C(p,q)` from
[`P5_H31_FULL_SUPPORT_TANGENT_COMPONENT_GENERIC_OBSTRUCTION.md`](../../h31/full-support-tangent/P5_H31_FULL_SUPPORT_TANGENT_COMPONENT_GENERIC_OBSTRUCTION.md).
It eliminates both finite weighted slopes, retains the slope-marking
relation before projection, checks the exceptional slope `r=-1`, and treats
both points at infinity.  Special parameter/projective component boundaries,
component fifteen, pure-`P_4` exhaustiveness, and the global Krenn--Gu
conjecture remain open.

## Two weighted Fitting images

Put

```text
P=p+q,       S=P+1,       A=(p-q)(P-1).             (1)
```

Use the pure bases `(alpha_i,beta_i)` of the preceding `H31` theorem and
write every marked basis as

```text
beta_i(h)=beta_i+h_i alpha_i.                        (2)
```

The two finite weighted contractions are

```text
D01^r(x,e)=(r*x0+x1,x2,x3,e),
D23^s(x,e)=(x0,x1,s*x2+x3,e).                       (3)
```

For either direction, impose the fourteen mixed binary equations, normalize
one binary diagonal to one, invert the other, and eliminate the extension
vector and the finite slope.  The exact projected closure ideals are

```text
J_01^fin=<A*h3-q,h2>,
J_23^fin=<h2,h0*h1>.                                (4)
```

Consequently every common finite-slope marking lies in

```text
A*h3=q,       h2=0,       h0*h1=0.                  (5)
```

The verifier proves both ideals in both directions and proves their sum is
exactly the ideal in (5).  Keeping the `D01` slope instead of eliminating it
gives one further exact equation:

```text
r*h0*h1+S*(1-rP)*(h0+h1)+r*S^2=0.                  (6)
```

On the first branch of (5), equations (5)--(6) therefore give

```text
(h0,h1,h2,h3)=
(r*S/(rP-1),0,0,q/A).                              (7)
```

The other branch is obtained by swapping the two equal tangent planes
`U0=U1`.

## A one-dimensional extension kernel

Away from `r=-1`, the mixed kernel over the branch (7) is generated by

```text
z=1/((p-1)(r+1)) *
  (1-rP,1-rP,S(r-1),0,0,-rS,rP-1,(p-1)(r+1)).      (8)
```

The two required diagonals on this generator are

```text
A_01=2r(p-q)P(P-1)(rP-1)/((p-1)(r+1)),
B_01=-2((P-1)r-1).                                  (9)
```

Take the mode-zero marked minors with row sets `0147` and `0457`.  Clear the
common entry denominator

```text
C=(p-1)(p-q)(P-1)(r+1).
```

Over the univariate polynomial ring `C(p,q)[r]`, exact determinant and gcd
calculation gives

```text
gcd(det(C*N_0147),det(C*N_0457))
  = unit * r*(rP-1)*((P-1)r-1)*(r+1)^2.             (10)
```

The first three factors are exactly the slope-dependent zero factors in
`A_01B_01`.  The last is the denominator-clearing artifact.  Hence on

```text
r!=-1,       A_01B_01!=0,                           (11)
```

the two marked minors cannot vanish together.  At least one marked map has
rank four, contradicting an `H22` lift.

The verifier checks (8) against all fourteen mixed equations before taking
the determinants.  It also checks the full marked mode swap

```text
U0<->U1,       (z0 z1)(z4 z5),                      (12)
```

so the second branch of (5) needs no new calculation.

## The exceptional and infinite slopes

At `r=-1`, branch (7) has `h0=1`.  The exact normalized binary incidence is
the unit ideal: equivalently, the all-active diagonal vanishes on the whole
mixed kernel.  Thus the only pole of (8) is not a genuine neighbour.

At `D01` slope infinity, the contraction is

```text
(x0,x2,x3,e).
```

This is literally the marked `H31` problem obtained by deleting source
coordinate one.  Its two marking points and marked maps agree entry by
entry with the first `H31` sheet of component fourteen (and its `U0/U1`
partner).  That exact binary-resultant certificate therefore excludes the
infinite `D01` slope.

Finally, the `D23` infinity projection has

```text
P*(p-q+1)*h3=q,                                     (13)
```

whereas every finite or infinite `D01` marking has `A*h3=q`.  Cross
multiplication differs by the nonzero function-field element `2p`; the two
infinity/finite cross-ideals are unit ideals.  No projective slope pair has
been omitted.

## Consequence and proof boundary

Every finite common marking is killed by (10), with `r=-1` killed at binary
level; the `D01` point at infinity is the already-excluded `H31` sheet; and
the `D23` point at infinity is disjoint.  Therefore

```text
generic H22 fibre(component 14)=empty.              (14)
```

Together with the preceding `H31` theorem, both fifth-coordinate partition
types are generically excluded over component fourteen.  Component fifteen
is now the only one of the seventeen known pure-`P_4` component orbits whose
generic `H31` and `H22` fibres both remain open.
