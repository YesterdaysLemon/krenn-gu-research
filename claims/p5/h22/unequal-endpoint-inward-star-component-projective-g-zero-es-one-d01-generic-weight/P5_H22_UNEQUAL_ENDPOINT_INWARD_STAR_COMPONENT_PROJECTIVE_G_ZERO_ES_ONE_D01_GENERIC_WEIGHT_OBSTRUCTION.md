## Polynomial marking module

Mark

```text

```

and append the eight free extension coordinates.  For finite `D01`, contract

```text
(x,e) -> (lambda*x0+x1,x2,x3,e).                  (4)
```

The sixteen binary coefficient rows are elements of an eight-dimensional
extension module.  Exact standard-basis reduction over

```text
Q(s,k,lambda)[h0,h1,h2,h3]
```

puts the all-`alpha` diagonal row in the module generated by the fourteen
mixed rows.  Therefore every extension whose mixed coefficients vanish also
has zero all-`alpha` diagonal and cannot be a genuine binary neighbour.  The
marking variables are polynomial variables, not coefficient-field units;
the conclusion covers every marking at the generic ordinary weight.

At weight infinity the contraction is

```text

```

and the same exact membership holds over `Q(s,k)[h0,h1,h2,h3]`.

The function-field use of `lambda` in (4) is deliberate.  A separate attempt
to promote the membership to
`Q(s,k)[lambda,h0,h1,h2,h3]` returned non-membership.  It supplies no
uniform special-weight theorem, so all finite special weights remain in the
boundary ledger.
