# Principal four-hafnians generically recover every edge up to finitely many branches

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero generic tomography theorem and sharp singular-fibre
boundary.**  Let `A=(a_ij)` be a symmetric zero-diagonal weighted graph on
`n>=6` named vertices, and record every principal four-vertex hafnian

```text
h_ijkl=a_ij a_kl+a_ik a_jl+a_il a_jk.                 (1)
```

The polynomial map from the `binom(n,2)` edge weights to the
`binom(n,4)` values in (1) is generically finite onto its image.  More
precisely, at the all-one graph its Jacobian is the `2`-subset versus
`4`-subset inclusion matrix and has full column rank.  Hence some
`binom(n,2)` named four-hafnians form an etale local coordinate system there,
and the full edge field is a finite algebraic extension of the field
generated by all four-hafnians.

This supplies the first exact nonlinear route around the `P_7` mixed-root
depth ceiling.  In the five-root/two-residual cell there are nine nonroots.
The maximal depth-five cofactors are precisely the principal hafnians on the
complementary four-sets.  If all 126 of those cofactors are individually and
legally exposed from one hypothetical witness, and its nonroot graph lies in
the full-Jacobian open set, then all 36 nonroot edges -- including the direct
blocker pair absent from every linear mixed-root ledger -- have only finitely
many algebraic possibilities.

The qualifications are essential.  The map is not globally injective:
`A` and `-A` have the same four-hafnians.  Worse, the one-edge line has every
four-hafnian equal to zero while its single edge varies freely.  This is the
same physical shallow-tower escape that defeats universal pair recovery.
Thus the new theorem converts the missing pair problem into three exact
obligations:

1. legally label the whole depth-five four-cofactor deck;
2. force the deck off the Jacobian rank-drop locus, or stratify that locus;
3. select the target-compatible finite algebraic branch.

None of those obligations is presently proved for GHZ.  This note neither
enumerates graph supports nor claims a `P_7` obstruction.  The global
Krenn--Gu conjecture remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 1. The four-hafnian response map

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero field, let `V` be a named `n`-set, and put

```text
E=K^{binom(V,2)}.
```

For `A in E` and a four-set `I={i,j,k,l}`, define

```text
H_I(A)=haf A[I]
      =a_ij a_kl+a_ik a_jl+a_il a_jk.                 (2)
```

Collect these polynomials into

```text
H_4:E -> K^{binom(V,4)},       A |-> (H_I(A))_I.       (3)
```

Write `1_E` for the graph with every edge weight one.  Differentiating (2)
with respect to an edge `e` gives the weight of the complementary edge when
`e subset I`, and zero otherwise.  Consequently

```text
(dH_4)_(1_E)[I,e] = 1 if e subset I, and 0 otherwise. (4)
```

This is exactly the transpose-convention version of the classical inclusion
matrix `W_(2,4)(n)`.  Inclusion matrices have a much broader integral
diagonal-form theory; see Ghorbani, Khosrovshahi, Maysoori, and
Mohammad-Noori, [*Inclusion Matrices and Chains*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.3144).
The special full-rank statement needed here has the following short
self-contained proof.

## 2. Symbolic kernel proof

### Theorem 1 (full tangent rank)

For `n>=6`, the matrix in (4) has column rank `binom(n,2)` over every field
whose characteristic does not divide six.

### Proof

Let edge scalars `x_e` lie in its kernel.  Then every four-set `I` satisfies

```text
sum_(e subset I, |e|=2) x_e=0.                         (5)
```

Fix distinct vertices `a,b`, and let `T` be any three-set disjoint from
them.  Subtract (5) for `{a} union T` and `{b} union T`.  The three edges
inside `T` cancel, leaving

```text
sum_(t in T) (x_at-x_bt)=0.                            (6)
```

There are at least four vertices outside `{a,b}`.  If `u,v` are two of
them, choose two more, `r,s`, and subtract (6) for `{u,r,s}` and
`{v,r,s}`.  Thus

```text
x_au-x_bu=x_av-x_bv.                                   (7)
```

All differences in (6) are therefore one scalar `c`, and (6) gives
`3c=0`; hence `c=0`.  We have proved

```text
x_at=x_bt                                               (8)
```

for every distinct triple `a,b,t`.  Edges sharing a vertex have equal
weights, and the line graph of the complete graph is connected, so every
`x_e` is one common scalar `d`.  Equation (5) now reads `6d=0`, whence
`d=0`.  The kernel is zero.

This proof is arbitrary-order and does not list the four-sets.  The fixed
`n=6` and `n=9` replay matrices only audit (4).

## 3. Generic algebraic recovery

### Theorem 2 (four-hafnian generic tomography)

For `n>=6` in characteristic zero:

- the image of `H_4` has dimension `binom(n,2)`;
- `H_4` is generically finite onto its image;
- some family `B subset binom(V,4)` of size `binom(n,2)` has a Jacobian
  minor nonzero at `1_E`;
- the square submap `(H_I)_(I in B)` is formally, and over `C` analytically,
  locally invertible at `1_E`.

Equivalently, with `a_e` algebraically independent,

```text
K(a_e:e in binom(V,2))
```

is a finite algebraic extension of the subfield generated by the principal
four-hafnians.

### Proof

Theorem 1 gives a nonzero maximal Jacobian minor at `1_E`.  Its determinant
is a polynomial, so full column rank holds on a nonempty Zariski-open subset
of `E`.  The selected `binom(n,2)` coordinate polynomials have algebraically
independent differentials and are therefore algebraically independent in
characteristic zero.  Their field has the same transcendence degree as the
edge field.  The latter is finitely generated over the former, so the
extension is finite algebraic.  The same nonzero minor gives formal inverse
coordinates, and the complex inverse function theorem gives analytic local
inversion at `1_E`.

The conclusion is finite algebraic identifiability, not a rational formula
or a globally single-valued inverse.  In particular,

```text
H_4(-A)=H_4(A),                                         (9)
```

so at least the global sign involution remains.

## 4. The singular fibre is genuinely positive-dimensional

### Proposition 3 (one-edge singular line)

Fix an edge `uv`, set `a_uv=t`, and set every other edge to zero.  Then

```text
H_I(A(t))=0 for every four-set I and every t.           (10)
```

### Proof

Every term of a four-hafnian is a product of two disjoint edge weights.  A
graph supported on one edge has no two-edge matching, so all three products
in (2) vanish.

The desired edge therefore varies on an affine line inside the zero fibre of
`H_4`.  This proves that no universal polynomial, rational, continuous, or
set-theoretic single-valued recovery of all edges from the four-hafnian deck
can hold on the entire graph space.

The differential also vanishes at the zero graph, so the rank-drop locus is
nonempty.  It is not a technical artifact of the proof at `1_E`; it contains
the exact sparse physical escape already present in the `P_7` shallow-tower
control.

## 5. Exact `P_7` interface

In the active cell, let the nine nonroots be the seven blocker ports and two
residual vertices.  A five-root mixed word can delete at most five nonroots.
At maximal deletion depth five, the complement has four vertices, and its
cofactor is exactly one coordinate of (3).  Complementation gives a
bijection

```text
{depth-five deletion sets D} <-> {four-sets I},
C_D=H_I(A),                    I=V minus D.             (11)
```

There are `binom(9,4)=126` such named cofactors and 36 nonroot edges.  Hence
Theorem 2 gives the following conditional transfer.

The label count also locates the necessary root sensor.  A single
two-plane polarization at each of the five roots has coefficient-form space
of dimension only

```text
2^5=32 < 126.                                          (12)
```

Its companion-incidence synthesis map cannot label the whole deck.  By
contrast, the unprojected three-dimensional five-root tensor has ambient
dimension

```text
3^5=243 >=126.                                         (13)
```

Capacity alone therefore does not exclude full-deck exposure from the entire
root tensor.  The next selector problem must use that full tensor, or a
synchronized stack of smaller jets; another single two-plane fan cannot
suffice.

### Corollary 4 (conditional nonlinear escape from the root budget)

Suppose one physical `P_7` witness legally exposes every named depth-five
cofactor in (11), with a common nonroot graph `A`.  If `A` lies in the
full-Jacobian open set of `H_4`, then every direct blocker edge and every
root-budget-invisible one-residual edge is algebraic with only finitely many
branches over those exposed cofactors.

This does not contradict the mixed-root deletion theorem.  That theorem says
the desired depth-seven edge cofactor is absent as a **linear column** from
