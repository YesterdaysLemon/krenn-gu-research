# Majority-subset internal-edge ideal hierarchy

## Status

**Verified arbitrary-order characteristic-zero necessary condition.**  On
every vertex subset larger than half the graph, a simple matching count
forces a corresponding power of the internal-edge ideal.  A hypothetical
GHZ matching tensor must place each contracted monochromatic pure tensor in
that ideal power.

At the first level, duality gives a new entangled block-harmonic obstruction:
every tensor killed by all internal pair contractions must also kill all
three diagonal pure tensors.  The previously used multi-star obstruction
tests decomposable tensors in this common kernel; the statement here applies
to the whole, generally much larger, entangled kernel.

This is a hierarchy of necessary conditions, not a proof of Krenn--Gu.

## Block-squarefree edge ideals

Let `V` have `n=2m` vertices, with local spaces `E_i=K^3` and bases
`e_(i,0),e_(i,1),e_(i,2)`.  Regard every edge block as a tensor

```text
W_ij in E_i tensor E_j.                               (1)
```

For `S subset V`, work in the block-squarefree algebra

```text
A_S = tensor_(i in S) (K direct_sum E_i),             (2)
```

where a product using two positive-degree factors from one block is zero.
Let `I_S` be the ideal generated by the internal edge tensors

```text
{W_ij : i,j in S, i<j}.                               (3)
```

Its multidegree-`S` part in the `r`th power is spanned by expressions

```text
W_(i1,j1) ... W_(ir,jr)
  product_(k in S minus all endpoints) v_k,           (4)
```

where the `r` internal edges are disjoint and `v_k in E_k`.

## The majority-edge count

Fix `S` with

```text
|S|=m+r,    1<=r<=m-1.                                (5)
```

The complement is nonempty and has size `m-r`.  In a perfect matching, let

```text
a = number of edges internal to S,
b = number of crossing edges,
d = number of edges internal to V minus S.            (6)
```

Counting endpoints on the two sides gives

```text
2a+b=m+r,
2d+b=m-r.                                             (7)
```

Subtracting yields the exact identity

```text
a=d+r.                                                (8)
```

In particular, every perfect matching uses at least `r` internal edges of
`S`.

## The ideal-power theorem

Let `T_W in tensor_(i in V) E_i` be the full coloured matching tensor.  For
a colour `c`, contract every vertex of `V minus S` with the coordinate
covector `e_(j,c)^*`, and denote the resulting tensor on `S` by

```text
T_(S,c).                                              (9)
```

### Theorem 1

For every block system `W`,

```text
T_(S,c) belongs to (I_S^r)_(multidegree S).           (10)
```

If `T_W=Delta_3`, then the complement in (5) is nonempty and the same
contraction kills the other two GHZ summands.  Therefore

```text
tensor_(i in S) e_(i,c)
  belongs to (I_S^r)_(multidegree S)                 (11)
```

for every colour `c` and every `S` satisfying (5).

Proof.  Expand `T_W` over perfect matchings.  After the complement
contractions, an internal-complement edge becomes a scalar and a crossing
edge becomes a vector in its `S` endpoint.  Every internal-`S` edge remains
one generator `W_ij` of `I_S`.  Equation (8) says that each surviving term
has at least `r` such generators, proving (10).  The GHZ contraction then
gives (11).

The upper bound `r<=m-1` matters: when `S=V`, there is no complementary leg
with which to select one GHZ colour.  At that endpoint only the sum of the
three pure tensors is automatically in `I_V^m`.

## First dual level: entangled block harmonics

Take `r=1`, so `|S|=m+1`.  For every pair `{i,j} subset S`, contraction with
the edge tensor defines

```text
C_ij(W): tensor_(k in S) E_k^*
          -> tensor_(k in S minus {i,j}) E_k^*.        (12)
```

Collect these maps in the direct sum

```text
C_S(W) = direct_sum_(i<j in S) C_ij(W).               (13)
```

Also define the diagonal evaluation map

```text
delta_S(h)
 = (h(tensor_(i in S)e_(i,0)),
    h(tensor_(i in S)e_(i,1)),
    h(tensor_(i in S)e_(i,2))).                       (14)
```

### Corollary 2 (entangled harmonic obstruction)

Every hypothetical witness satisfies

```text
ker C_S(W) subset ker delta_S                         (15)
```

for every `(m+1)`-vertex set `S`.  Equivalently,

```text
rank [C_S(W); delta_S] = rank C_S(W).                 (16)
```

Proof.  The annihilator of the full multidegree part of `I_S` is exactly the
intersection of the kernels of the pair-contraction maps (12).  Pairing
(11) with an element of that annihilator gives all three components of
(14) equal to zero.

In apolar language, elements of `ker C_S(W)` are block-squarefree harmonic
differential tensors.  There is also a direct pigeonhole proof.  Write the
global edge quadratic as `q=sum W_ij`.  Applying `m+1` block-distinct
derivatives to `q^m`, some quadratic factor receives two derivatives.  That
Leibniz term contains one of the contractions (12), so every `h in ker C_S`
annihilates `q^m`.  Multiplication by an arbitrary squarefree differential
on `S^c` sees only the desired one-variable-per-vertex sector, avoiding the
usual unobserved-Gaussian-sector problem.

A decomposable element

```text
h = u_i tensor ... tensor u_j
```

in `ker C_S` is a pairwise-orthogonal transversal and recovers the existing
multi-star obstruction.  Corollary 2 also detects entangled common-kernel
tensors that have no decomposable representative.  That is the new part.

## Higher dual levels

For general `r`, dualizing (11) says that every functional annihilating the
full multidegree component of `I_S^r` must annihilate the three diagonal pure
tensors.  These are higher block-catalecticant conditions.  In coordinates,
one may assemble the span of all products of `r` disjoint internal edge
tensors as the columns of a matrix `C_(S,r)`; a witness requires

```text
rank [C_(S,r); delta_(S,r)] = rank C_(S,r).           (17)
```

The useful point is not to build this matrix by listing supports.  Products
of edge tensors define a symbolic ideal power, so one can use apolarity,
flattenings, determinantal geometry, or representation-theoretic
decomposition of `I_S^r`.

Overlapping majority sets give a sheaf-like compatibility problem: their
internal-edge ideals share generators, while their three forced pure classes
must agree on intersections.  This is a plausible source of the missing
local-to-global contradiction.

At eight vertices, the successor
[`five-root three-colour boundary-incidence theorem`](EIGHT_VERTEX_FIVE_ROOT_THREE_COLOUR_BOUNDARY_INCIDENCE_CODIMENSION_THREE_THEOREM.md)
combines the `r=1`, five-root specialization with unconditional five-root
intersection.  Its incidence projection puts every induced `K_5` tuple of a
witness whose ten blocks are all nonzero in a closed projective coefficient
envelope of codimension at least three.  That successor is a new proof-DAG
combination and elimination
step; it does not change the arbitrary-order hierarchy proved here.

## Literature interface

The matching tensor is the squarefree block projection of a power of a
quadratic.  Recent apolar work on powers of quadrics identifies harmonic
generators of their annihilator ideals
([arXiv:2411.03161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03161)), while border-rank work
on powers of ternary quadrics develops the relevant catalecticant geometry
([arXiv:2208.07921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07921)).  Theorem 1 is more
special and more elementary: the block multigrading and the majority count
(8) produce an entire ideal-power hierarchy without a nondegeneracy
assumption.

The hierarchy also resembles symbolic powers and transversal ideals in
commutative algebra, but `I_S` is tensor-valued and depends on the same edge
blocks across overlapping sets.  Standard monomial-edge-ideal theorems do
not apply verbatim; the exact content available now is (11), (15), and their
determinantal reformulations.

## Next symbolic target

The first useful test should be `r=1`, not a larger support shell:

1. choose an `(m+1)`-set adapted to the current roots, blockers, and two
   residual ports;
2. construct `C_S(W)` as a block contraction map, retaining tensor entries;
3. use the forced lower mixed-jet frames to exhibit either an entangled
   element of `ker C_S` with nonzero `delta_S`, or prove the rank equality;
4. compare two overlapping majority sets before moving to `r=2`.

Failure of (16) is a global obstruction.  Passing (16) narrows the next step
to overlap compatibility or the higher ideal powers in (17).
