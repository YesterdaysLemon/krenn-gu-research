## 1. The physical projection of the common-cofactor family

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero field and pass to its algebraic closure when
discussing polynomial images.  Let `Q` be an even residual set of order `q`.
For its contracted hollow symmetric edge matrix `A`, put

```text
h=haf(A),
C(A)_ij=haf(A[Q minus {i,j}])  for i!=j,
C(A)_ii=0.                                          (2)
```

Choose `n` scalar port directions.  Their residual incidence columns are
vectors `r_1,...,r_n in K^q`, and (1) becomes

```text
k_ij=r_i^T C(A)r_j,                 i!=j.            (3)
```

The diagonal values `r_i^T C(A)r_i` are not physical two-port responses.
Introduce independent latent diagonal coordinates `d_1,...,d_n`, and let

```text
Y(d)_ij = k_ij  for i!=j,
Y(d)_ii = d_i.                                       (4)
```

Write `J_(n,q)` for the ideal generated by all `(q+1) x (q+1)` minors of
the generic symmetric matrix `Y(d)`, and define

```text
I_(n,q)=J_(n,q) intersect K[k_ij : 1<=i<j<=n].       (5)
```

This is the symmetric factor-analysis ideal: algebraically, it is the
projection of a symmetric rank variety after forgetting the diagonal.

### Theorem 1 (exact universal two-port ideal)

For every even `q>=2`, the Zariski closure of all corrected scalar two-port
families (3) is

```text
F_(n,q)={offdiag(Y): Y=Y^T and rank Y<=q}.            (6)
```

Its universal polynomial ideal is exactly `I_(n,q)`.  The same statement
already holds after fixing one residual matrix `A_0` with `C(A_0)`
nonsingular.  For `q>=4`, `A_0` may moreover be chosen with

```text
haf(A_0)=0                                           (7)
```

and complete nonzero residual support.  Consequently neither varying the
hafnian cofactor form nor imposing the torus-zero branch produces any
additional polynomial equation at the corrected two-port level.

### Proof

The canonical completion of (3) is

```text
Y=R^T C(A)R,
R=[r_1 ... r_n],                                    (8)
```

so `rank Y<=q`.  This proves containment in (6).

Conversely, fix a residual matrix `A_0` for which `C_0=C(A_0)` is
nonsingular.  Such a matrix exists at `q=2`, where

```text
C_0=[0 1;1 0],                                      (9)
```

and at every even `q>=4` the explicit torus-zero family in
`RESIDUAL_HAFNIAN_TORUS_ZERO_FULL_RANK_COFACTOR_BOUNDARY.md` supplies one.
Over the algebraic closure, every symmetric matrix `Y` of rank at most `q`
has a factorization

```text
Y=R^T C_0R.                                         (10)
```

Indeed, quotient `Y` by its radical, classify the resulting symmetric form
by congruence, and pad its factor with zero rows to dimension `q`.  Hence the
fixed cofactor form already realizes every point of the symmetric rank
variety and therefore every off-diagonal projection in (6).

The elimination theorem says that the ideal of the closure of this
projection is precisely (5).  All equations are defined over the original
characteristic-zero field, so base change to its algebraic closure does not
alter the universal polynomial ideal.  This proves the theorem.

The theorem sharpens the earlier statement that the rank bound is sharp.  It
identifies the **whole** physical universal ideal, including equations caused
by the missing diagonal, and proves that its source is symmetric completion
rather than a further scalar-hafnian identity.

## 2. Two residual vertices and the pentad

For `q=2`, write the two incidence coordinates at port `i` as `a_i,b_i`.
Equation (3) is

```text
k_ij=a_i b_j+b_i a_j.                               (11)
```

For five named ports `1,...,5`, define

```text
P_12345 =
   k12 k13 k24 k35 k45 - k12 k13 k25 k34 k45
 - k12 k14 k23 k35 k45 + k12 k14 k25 k34 k35
 + k12 k15 k23 k34 k45 - k12 k15 k24 k34 k35
 + k13 k14 k23 k25 k45 - k13 k14 k24 k25 k35
 - k13 k15 k23 k24 k45 + k13 k15 k24 k25 k34
 - k14 k15 k23 k25 k34 + k14 k15 k23 k24 k35.       (12)
```

### Theorem 2 (five-port principal ideal)

Over every characteristic-zero field,

```text
I_(5,2)=(P_12345).                                   (13)
```

Equivalently, an off-diagonal five-port array belongs to the Zariski closure
of the common two-residual response family if and only if its pentad
vanishes.  The response maps on at most four scalar ports are dominant, so
there is no nonzero universal polynomial equation before five ports.

### Proof

Substitute (11) into (12).  Expanding the twelve bilinear products and
collecting monomials gives zero identically.  This is a fixed polynomial
identity; the primary and independent replays use unrelated symbolic
representations to check the cancellation.

It remains to prove that there is no further equation.  The Jacobian of the
ten functions (11), with rows ordered

```text
12,13,14,15,23,24,25,34,35,45,
```

has the following `9 x 9` minor: take the first nine rows and the columns

```text
a1,a2,a3,a4,a5,b1,b2,b3,b4.
```

Its determinant is

```text
-4 b5 (a1 b2-a2 b1)(a1 b3-a3 b1)
      (a2 b3-a3 b2)(a4 b5-a5 b4).                   (14)
```

It is nonzero.  At

```text
(a1,...,a5)=(1,2,3,4,5),
(b1,...,b5)=(2,5,10,17,26),                          (15)
```

the value is `-39520`.  Thus the irreducible image closure has dimension at
least nine.  The nonzero equation (12) bounds its dimension by nine, so it is
an irreducible hypersurface.

For completeness, the pentad is irreducible.  Regard it as

```text
P_12345=A k45+B.                                     (16)
```

The coefficient `A` is linear in `k23`, with constant coefficient

```text
k12 k13 (k24 k35-k25 k34).                           (17)
```

The three irreducible factors in (17) divide neither its `k23` coefficient:
this is immediate for `k12,k13`, while the determinant
`k24 k35-k25 k34` is excluded by setting
`k24=1,k25=k34=k35=0`.  Hence `A` is irreducible by Gauss's lemma.  Finally,
at

```text
k12=k13=k24=k35=0,
k14=k15=k23=k25=k34=1,                               (18)
```

one has `A=0` and `B=-1`, so `A` does not divide `B`.  Therefore
`gcd(A,B)=1`, and the primitive degree-one polynomial (16) is irreducible.

The irreducible nine-dimensional image closure contained in the irreducible
nine-dimensional hypersurface `P_12345=0` must equal that hypersurface.
This proves (13).

For four ports, one `6 x 6` Jacobian minor (all six response rows and columns
`a1,a2,a3,a4,b1,b2`) has determinant

```text
2(a3 b4-a4 b3)
 [a1 b2 b3 b4+a2 b1 b3 b4-a3 b1 b2 b4-a4 b1 b2 b3]. (19)
```

Its value at the truncation of (15) is `13640`, proving dominance.  The
smaller-port maps are its coordinate projections.

## 3. Invariant and block-port forms

Under independent port rescalings

```text
k_ij -> t_i t_j k_ij,
```

every monomial of (12) has degree two at every port, so

```text
P_12345 -> (product_i t_i^2) P_12345.                (20)
```

A permutation of the five ports multiplies the displayed generator by the
sign of the permutation.  Thus its zero set is independent of labels and
scales: the pentad is a relative invariant of the port torus semidirect the
symmetric group.

Now restore arbitrary local port spaces `V_i`.  For chosen vectors
`x_i in V_i`, evaluate the corrected bilinear blocks as

```text
k_ij(x)=K_ij(x_i,x_j).                               (21)
```

If the blocks share one two-residual cofactor channel, then

```text
P_12345(k_ij(x))=0                 for all x_i.       (22)
```

The left side is multihomogeneous of degree two in each `x_i`.  Its complete
coefficient tensor lies in

```text
Sym^2(V_1^*) tensor ... tensor Sym^2(V_5^*).          (23)
```

A single nonzero coefficient is therefore an exact, basis-independent,
no-anchor obstruction to the common two-residual decomposition.  No latent
diagonal block, inverse, genericity assumption, or choice of a bipartition is
needed.

For six or more ports every five-subset pentad must vanish.  This note does
**not** claim that the five-subset pentads generate `I_(n,2)` for arbitrary
`n`; higher factor-analysis invariants can occur.  Formula (5), rather than
an unsupported generator claim, is the exact all-`n` statement.

1. **Corrected two-port ideal.**  At arbitrary residual order, its exact
   universal ideal is (5).  Searching for an additional polynomial involving
   only `C(A)` or one corrected response level cannot succeed.
2. **Two residuals.**  The completion condition `chi_off<=1` in

   corrected two-port blocks can be tested by the tensor pentad (22).  A
   nonzero value rules out that two-residual branch before any support
   classification.

   the factor-analysis elimination ideal `I_(n,q)`, not the principal-
   cofactor polar ideal, which is already dominant.  Deeper deletion decks
   can still impose genuinely hafnian integrability equations.
5. **Coordinate boundary.**  The pentad is polynomial and remains valid when
   individual port incidences or response entries vanish.  It requires no
   torus localization.
