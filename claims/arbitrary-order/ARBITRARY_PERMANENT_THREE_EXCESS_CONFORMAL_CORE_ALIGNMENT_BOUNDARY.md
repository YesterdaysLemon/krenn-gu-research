## Backbone-aligned induced cycles

Let `K=C_(2 ell)` be the induced conformal circuit containing all three
excess cells.  Write its two internal perfect matchings as `A,B`, and choose
a perfect matching `N` of `G-V(K)`.  Suppose that

1. `F=A union N` lies in one selected pure-backbone union
   `H=M_0 union M_1 union M_2` and induces a word `w`; and
2. `F'=B union N` is eligible for the same word.

Every source of `K` changes partners between `F` and `F'`.  Exceptional-
source localization therefore puts every one of those sources in `P_*`.
Conversely, `K` contains every excess cell, so it contains every exceptional
source.  Hence

```text
V_source(K)=P_*,             ell=|P_*| in {2,3}.     (1)
```

Thus an aligned circuit is `C_4` or `C_6`, regardless of `m`.  Localization
also confines every same-word competitor to the corresponding port modes
and sources.  Since `K` is induced, its two internal perfect matchings are
the only terms in this coefficient after the common exterior monomial is
removed.

The exact boundary matrices are therefore

```text
C_4:  X=[[d_1,q_12],[q_21,d_2]],
      d_1 d_2+q_12 q_21=0,                          (2)

C_6:  X=[[d_1,q_12,0],
          [0,d_2,q_23],
          [q_31,0,d_3]],
      d_1 d_2 d_3+q_12 q_23 q_31=0.                 (3)
```

The `C_6` face is `(a,b,c,u,v)=(0,0,0,-1,0)`, up to
orientation.  It supplies one soluble negative-gain binomial, not the full
five-variable `B_3` obstruction.

## Exact coloured-extension criterion

For each colour `c`, let `G_c` be the support graph of the coordinate-`c`
cell components.  Given a perfect matching `F` of the uncoloured support,
choose a supported colour `chi(e)` for each `e in F` and put

```text
F_c={e in F:chi(e)=c}.                              (4)
```

There are pure perfect matchings `M_c` with `F_c subset M_c` for all three
colours if and only if

```text
G_c-V(F_c) has a perfect matching for every c.       (5)
```

Indeed, deleting the endpoints of the fixed partial matching is exactly the
residual perfect-matching extension problem, independently in each colour.
This is the precise alignment condition for `F` to lie in a selected
backbone union with the chosen labels.

The conformal theorem supplies an uncoloured complement matching.  It does
not supply any of the three residual conditions (5).  Matching-coveredness
is edge extendability, not simultaneous coloured partial-matching
extendability.

## A sharp aligned-cycle countermodel

All indices below are modulo three.  Use modes

```text
x_0,x_1,x_2,y_0,y_1,y_2
```

and sources

```text
p_0,p_1,p_2,q_0,q_1,q_2.
```

Take the 18 mandatory cells

```text
A_j=x_j p_j,       N_j=y_j q_j,
C_j=x_j q_j,       D_j=y_j p_j,
C'_j=x_j q_(j+1),  D'_j=y_j p_(j+1),                (6)
```

partitioned into pure matchings

```text
M_0={C_0,D_0,A_1,A_2,N_1,N_2},
M_1={A_0,N_0,C_1,C_2,D_1,D_2},
M_2={C'_j,D'_j:j in Z/3}.                            (7)
```

Add the three excess cells

```text
E_j=x_j p_(j-1).                                    (8)
```

Then `K=A union E` is an induced `C_6`, and
`N={N_0,N_1,N_2}` matches its complement.  Every mandatory edge belongs to
one displayed pure matching, while every `E_j` belongs to `E union N`.
Moreover the support is connected: the paths
`x_j-q_(j+1)-y_(j+1)-p_(j+1)-x_(j+1)` link successive index blocks.
Hence the support is matching-covered.  Each mode has one mandatory edge of
each colour, so its three coordinate covectors have rank three.  The support
has exactly `18+3=21=3*6+3` cells.

Give the mixed word colour one at `x_0,y_0` and colour zero at the other four
modes.  Give every mandatory cell its colour from (7), and set

```text
E_0[1]=-1,              E_1[0]=E_2[0]=1.            (9)
```

The eligibility graph for this word is exactly `K union N`.  The outer
matching is forced and the induced cycle has its two internal matchings, so
the complete coefficient is represented by

```text
X=[[1,0,-1],
   [1,1,0],
   [0,1,1]],               per(X)=0.                (10)
```

This is a characteristic-zero symbolic cancellation with no hidden third
term.  It is not a `P_6 -> Delta_3` restriction: the other forbidden words
are not imposed.  It proves that support count, matching-coveredness, local
rank, pure backbones, conformality, and one aligned mixed coefficient do not
jointly exclude the first surviving layer.

## An arbitrary theta has no internal toric equation

Let the three internally disjoint theta paths be odd paths

```text
P_i=(e_(i,1),...,e_(i,2 ell_i+1)).                  (11)
```

For formal independent edge indeterminates `w(e)`, define

```text
O_i=product_(t odd)  w(e_(i,t)),
E_i=product_(t even) w(e_(i,t)).                    (12)
```

The three internal perfect-matching monomials are

```text
T_i=O_i E_j E_k,
Z_K=T_1+T_2+T_3.                                    (13)
```

They have zero toric ideal.  The first edge of `P_i` occurs in `T_i` and in
neither other term.  Comparing its exponent in any proposed binomial
relation forces the two exponents of `T_i` to agree, for each `i`.
Therefore the theta matching polytope is a two-simplex.  The `B_3` cubic does
not originate from the three theta matchings alone.  After localizing at
`E_1 E_2 E_3!=0`, one may equivalently factor

```text
Z_K=E_1 E_2 E_3 (O_1/E_1+O_2/E_2+O_3/E_3).         (13a)
```

Specializing the formal weights may create accidental relations or zeros;
the zero-toric-ideal claim concerns the formal monomial map.

## The aligned theta--chord signature

Suppose one theta matching together with a common exterior complement is the
distinguished matching `F` in the selected backbone union, and the other two
theta matchings with that same complement are eligible for the same word.
Pairwise localization again puts every theta source in `P_*`; the carrier
contains all excess cells, so equality holds.  Hence it has at most three
sources.  A simple theta has at most one length-one branch path; its other two
odd paths have length at least three, so each bipartition shore has at least
three vertices.  There are therefore exactly three sources and three modes,
the branch lengths are `1,3,3`, and, after relabeling, its port matrix is

```text
X_theta=[[X_11,X_12,X_13],
         [X_21,X_22,0],
         [X_31,0,X_33]].                            (14)
```

Its normalized terms are `1,a,b`.  The two missing completing chords are
`X_23,X_32`.  Define

```text
r=u/a=X_23 X_31/(X_21 X_33),
s=v/b=X_21 X_32/(X_22 X_31).                        (15)
```

Then exactly

```text
u=a r,             v=b s,             c=r s,        (16)
1+a+b+c+u+v=1+a+b+a r+b s+r s,                      (17)
u v=a b c.                                          (18)
```

The theta triangle is `{1,a,b}`; the chord-completion triangle is
`{c,u,v}`.  For the bare theta, `r=s=0`.  If it is a complete forbidden
coefficient, then `1+a+b=0`.  All three terms are nonzero, so the three
complementary channels

```text
1,                 1+b=-a,             1+a=-b       (19)
```

are nonzero.  Thus a coefficient-induced bare theta would prove the desired
complementary-minor conclusion.

Simultaneous channel vanishing in the completed matrix instead forces

```text
a=b=-1,            r s=-1,              r+s=-2.     (20)
```

Both completing chords must be eligible, and over `C`

```text
{r,s}={-1+sqrt(2),-1-sqrt(2)}.                      (21)
```

The prior full bypass matrix realizes this sharply:

```text
X=[[1,1,1-sqrt(2)],
   [-1,1,1],
   [1+sqrt(2),-1,1]].                               (22)
```

Omitting `X_23,X_32` leaves theta sum `1+a+b=-1`; the two chords contribute
`c+u+v=1`.  The completed permanent and all three complementary permanents
vanish.  This is a weighted boundary countermodel, not a full restriction.

## Boundary-signature convolution

Let `R=E(G) minus E(K)`.  For `S subset V(K)`, let `Theta_S` be the weighted
sum of matchings using edges of `K` and covering exactly `V(K) minus S`.
Let `Omega_S` be the weighted sum of matchings using edges of `R` whose
covered vertex set is exactly
`S union (V(G) minus V(K))`: they cover every exterior vertex and avoid
`V(K) minus S`.  Partitioning each global perfect matching by its `K`-edges
gives the exact identity

```text
Z_G=sum_(S subset V(K)) Theta_S Omega_S,
Theta_empty=T_1+T_2+T_3.                            (23)
```

Conformality guarantees at least one support matching in the empty-boundary
complement sector.  It does not force the aggregate `Omega_empty` nonzero for
a chosen word, and it does not force `Omega_S=0` for `S!=empty`.  Chords and
cut-crossing matchings therefore survive.  Nor must the matching supplied by
the conformal theorem satisfy the coloured extension conditions (5).

The missing bridge is consequently one of:

- coefficient-inducedness killing every nonempty boundary sector;
- an incidence theorem excluding simultaneous eligibility of both
  theta-completing chords; or
- a rule identifying which theta and chord channels occur in each selected
  pure-backbone phase copy.

The later apolar boundary-quotient theorem resolves the second alternative
under the tight completed-theta hypotheses: an aligned conformal minimal
theta containing all three excess cells cannot have both completing chords
eligible in the same coefficient.  It quotients all nonempty boundary sectors
and uses a uniquely selectable exterior conformal matching.  See
`ARBITRARY_PERMANENT_THREE_EXCESS_APOLAR_BOUNDARY_QUOTIENT_THEOREM.md`.

## Exact multi-cycle exchange-lattice criterion

Suppose several aligned induced cycles yield nonzero monomial gain equations

```text
x^(lambda_s)=-1,            lambda_s in Z^q.         (24)
```

Over `C*`, this system is inconsistent exactly when there is an integer
relation

```text
sum_s z_s lambda_s=0              with sum_s z_s odd. (25)
```

Necessity follows by multiplying (24) to the powers `z_s`.  Conversely, if
every relation has even coefficient sum, `lambda_s -> -1` defines a
character of the lattice generated by the `lambda_s`.  Since `C*` is a
divisible group, that character extends to `Z^q`, giving a solution of (24).

One aligned circuit supplies one freely soluble relation.  A global
contradiction requires an odd dependency among several physically aligned
exchange vectors.  This lattice-parity target is exact and remains
arbitrary-order without enumerating the matching interiors.
