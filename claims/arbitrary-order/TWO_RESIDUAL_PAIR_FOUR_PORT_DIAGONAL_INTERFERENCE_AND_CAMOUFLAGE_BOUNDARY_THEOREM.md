# Two-residual pair/four-port diagonal interference and camouflage boundary

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero two-depth detector and sharp same-graph
counterboundary.**  On one physical two-residual response window with four
ternary ports, the six residual-present pair tensors and the residual-present
four-port tensor obey a quadratic interference identity.  The hidden
interference tensor is generated by two common residual incidence rows, so
every one-port flattening has rank at most two.

This gives a bounded target-coupled detector.  If the pair tensors are legally
target-derived and diagonal, and three colours are active across complementary
pair depths at one port, then a displayed `3 x 3` grid of nine four-port mixed
coefficients cannot all vanish.  The determinant identity below therefore
exposes an actual nonzero mixed target coefficient, rather than an abstract
response defect.

The three-colour activity hypothesis cannot be omitted from this route.  An
exact rational physical response
has rank-two residual incidence at every port, diagonal pair tensors, and a
four-port tensor with three nonzero pure coefficients and every mixed
coefficient zero, while two corrected pair blocks remain mixed.  The same
two-depth target data admit two inequivalent corrected channels.  Thus the
complete pair-plus-four-port mixed target ideal does not by itself identify
the corrected response, supply the residual-absent deck, or prove Universal
Supply.

The breadth is one complete four-port window (all six pairs).  The depth is
the residual-present pair and four-port layers.  The common hidden data are
the two residual incidence rows used by same-graph physicality; they are not

is no overlap transition group in this one-chart theorem.  It neither glues

## 1. Physical response notation

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero field.  Let

```text
U={1,2,3,4}
```

be four ports, with finite-dimensional spaces `V_u`; the Krenn--Gu
application has `dim V_u=3`.  Fix a physical residual pair `Q={q0,q1}` and
fixed residual contractions.  Write

```text
h=B_(q0,q1),
a_u=B_(q0,u),
b_u=B_(q1,u),
K_uv=a_u tensor b_v+b_u tensor a_v,                    (1)
```

where the fixed residual vectors have already been inserted.  Let `B_uv` be
the direct port block.  The residual-present pair response is

```text
D_uv:=z_uv=h B_uv+K_uv.                                (2)
```

Let `T:=z_1234` be the residual-present four-port response.  Products of
blocks on disjoint pairs are canonically reordered into
`tensor_(u in U) V_u^*`.  For any six pair blocks `X_uv`, define

```text
C(X)=X_12 X_34+X_13 X_24+X_14 X_23.                  (3)
```

The four-port matching partition gives

```text
T=h C(B)
  +(K_12 B_34+B_12 K_34)
  +(K_13 B_24+B_13 K_24)
  +(K_14 B_23+B_14 K_23).                             (4)
```

No target equation has been used in (1)--(4).

## 2. Exact pair/four-port interference

### Theorem 1 (polynomial interference identity)

Every physical response in Section 1 satisfies

```text
h T=C(D)-C(K).                                         (5)
```

This identity is polynomial and remains valid at `h=0`.

### Proof

For one complementary pair partition `{e,f}`, equation (2) gives

```text
D_e D_f-K_e K_f
 =h^2 B_e B_f+h(K_e B_f+B_e K_f).                     (6)
```

Sum (6) over the three perfect matchings of `U` and compare with (4).
This proves (5).  No division by `h` occurs.  QED.

When `h!=0`, (5) is equivalently the elimination obtained by substituting
`B=(D-K)/h`.  Once `h!=0` and `D` are fixed, the remaining hidden
decomposition affects `T` only through the common corrected compound `C(K)`.

### Theorem 2 (common-row rank bound)

The corrected compound has the exact expansion

```text
C(K)=2 sum_(A subset U, |A|=2)
        (tensor_(u in A) a_u)(tensor_(v in U-A) b_v).  (7)
```

Consequently every one-port flattening of `C(K)` has rank at most two.

### Proof

Expand the three products in (3).  Fix a choice of two ports carrying an
`a` row and two carrying a `b` row.  Exactly two of the three perfect
matchings pair every `a` port with a `b` port, so the corresponding word
occurs with coefficient two.  These are all terms, proving (7).

At a fixed port `u`, every summand of (7) has its `u` factor in
`span{a_u,b_u}`.  The `u`-flattening row space is therefore contained in that
two-dimensional space.  QED.

The proof uses a permanental/zeon expansion.  No exterior or Pluecker
identity is being imported.

## 3. Three-active-colour detector

### Exact target-attachment input

The target conclusion below has one additional input, never an output of this
theorem.  Call the physical tensors `D_uv=z_uv` and `T=z_1234` **legally
target-attached** when constant `K`-linear coefficient selectors on the full
graph equation, independent of the open port coordinates, have outputs
exactly these tensors for the same named residual pair, residual contractions,
and physical response window, and their outputs on the GHZ target are
diagonal in the fixed port bases.

A selector may first produce a known nonzero constant multiple of one tensor;
it must be normalized by that constant before (5) is used.  A separately
proved constant target-diagonal nuisance may be subtracted first, but the
result must then equal the named physical tensor exactly.  Different
unrecorded scalings at different pairs do not qualify.  Function-field or
port-dependent inverses, contracted scalar reconstructions, and six unrelated
pair factorizations do not qualify.

Fix GHZ basis covectors `e_(u,0)^*,e_(u,1)^*,e_(u,2)^*` at every port.
Assume each pair response is diagonal:

```text
D_uv=sum_(c=0)^2 d_(uv,c) e_(u,c)^* tensor e_(v,c)^*. (8)
```

Fix one port `u`.  For every colour `c`, choose

```text
v_c in U-{u},
delta_c in {0,1,2}-{c},
e_c={u,v_c},
f_c=U-e_c,                                             (9)
```

and assume the complementary depth product

```text
g_c=d_(e_c,c) d_(f_c,delta_c) !=0.                    (10)
```

Call (10) **three-colour pair-depth activity at `u`**.

Let `beta_c` be the word on `U-{u}` which has colour `c` at `v_c` and
colour `delta_c` at the other two ports.  The three `beta_c` are distinct:
their colour multiplicities identify `c` as the unique odd-multiplicity
colour and `delta_c` as the multiplicity-two colour.

Form the `3 x 3` matrix of actual four-port response coefficients

```text
G_(r,c)=T(r at u, beta_c on U-{u}),       r,c in {0,1,2}. (11)
```

Every entry of `G` is a mixed word.  Let `A` be the same selected submatrix
of the `u`-flattening of `C(K)`.

### Theorem 3 (nine-word determinant detector)

Under (8)--(10),

```text
A=diag(g_0,g_1,g_2)-hG,
rank A<=2,
det(diag(g_0,g_1,g_2)-hG)=0.                          (12)
```

Hence:

1. if `h=0`, the three-active stratum is impossible;
2. if `h!=0`, at least one of the nine displayed mixed coefficients in `G`
   is nonzero.

### Proof

Evaluate `C(D)` on row `r` and column `beta_c`.  For `r=c`, the full word
has two occurrences of `c` on `e_c` and two occurrences of `delta_c` on
`f_c`.  There is exactly one perfect matching whose two edges join equal
colours, and its value is `g_c`.

If `r=delta_c`, the word has multiplicities `3+1`; if `r` is the third
colour, it has multiplicities `2+1+1`.  A product of two diagonal pair
blocks vanishes on either pattern.  Thus the selected matrix of `C(D)` is
exactly `diag(g_0,g_1,g_2)`.

Restrict (5) to these three rows and three columns.  Theorem 2 gives
`rank A<=2`, proving (12).  At `h=0`, (12) contradicts
`g_0g_1g_2!=0`.  At `h!=0`, if every entry of `G` were zero, then `A` would
be an invertible diagonal matrix, again a contradiction.  QED.

### Corollary 4 (target-coupled contradiction)

Suppose the six tensors `D_uv` and the tensor `T` are supplied by constant,
legally target-attached selectors from one hypothetical Krenn--Gu witness in
the sense just defined.  If (10) holds, the nine entries (11) are actual
mixed GHZ coefficients.  Theorem 3 proves that at least one is nonzero when
`h!=0`; at `h=0` the hypotheses are inconsistent.  Either conclusion
contradicts the target equations.

In particular, if all three diagonal entries of every `D_uv` are nonzero,
then (10) holds at every port (choose any different `delta_c` and any
complementary split), so no four-port target-diagonal response can exist.

The words "constant", "synchronized", and "same physical" are
load-bearing.  Same-graph physicality proves (5) but does not make hidden
principal-deletion tensors target-derived.  A function-field sensor inverse
is not a constant target selector.  Six independently factorized pair blocks
do not prove the common rows used in Theorem 2.

## 4. Exact sharp camouflage at two active colours

The conclusion of this two-depth route is false if three-colour activity is
omitted, even after imposing
the complete pair and four-port mixed target equations and requiring three
nonzero pure four-port coefficients.

Work over `Q`, take `h=1`, and use the ternary bases `e0,e1,e2`.  Put

```text
a_1=a_2=e0,       b_1=b_2=e1,
a_3=a_4=e1,       b_3=b_4=e0.                         (13)
```

Thus every local residual frame has rank two and

```text
K_12=K_34=E_01+E_10,
K_13=K_14=K_23=K_24=E_00+E_11.                       (14)
```

Choose the diagonal pair targets

```text
D_12=D_34=E_22,
D_13=E_00+E_11,
D_24=2E_00+2E_11,
D_14=E_00+(2/3)E_11,
D_23=3E_00+2E_11,                                    (15)
```

and set

```text
B_uv=D_uv-K_uv.                                        (16)
```

Equations (2) and (16) make every pair response exactly the diagonal tensor
`D_uv`, while (5) gives

```text
T=3 e0^tensor4+(4/3)e1^tensor4+e2^tensor4.            (17)
```

Thus all `78` mixed ternary four-port response coefficients vanish and all
three pure
coefficients are nonzero.  Nevertheless `K_12` and `K_34` have nonzero mixed
entries.

This is an honest physical two-residual response.  To realize it as a block
graph, take ternary residual spaces, contract both residual vertices against
`e0`, use `E_00` on the residual edge, use `e0 tensor a_u` and
`e0 tensor b_u` on the two residual--port shores, and use (16) on the direct
port edges.  The perfect-matching response is exactly (13)--(17).

At every port, colours `0` and `1` are active in the sense of (10), while
colour `2` is not: its only incident diagonal target edge has a complementary
edge carrying only colour `2`.  The threshold in Theorem 3 is therefore
attained sharply.

There is also exact nonidentifiability.  Replace

```text
K -> -K,        B -> D+K.                              (18)
```

The pair data `D` are unchanged and `C(-K)=C(K)`, so the four-port tensor is
also unchanged.  Both signs are physical (replace every `a_u` by `-a_u`).
They are not related by a residual `O(J)` gauge, since such a gauge preserves
the bilinear blocks `K_uv`.  Hence even the complete two-depth target data do
not identify the corrected channel.

These are diagonal pair and pure four-port **response data**, not a claimed
legal target attachment.  The control is not a Krenn--Gu witness.  It is one
fixed residual response slice and does not satisfy the full graph target,
maximal-root supply, or all remaining deletion equations.  It proves
sharpness only for this pair-plus-four-port route; it does not prove that
three-colour activity is necessary for every possible detector.

## 5. The `h=0` supply boundary

At `h=0`, equation (2) gives `D=K`.  Pair target diagonality therefore forces
the corrected pair blocks themselves to be diagonal.  It still does not
supply the residual-absent deck `B`.

For example, take at every port

```text
a=e0+e1,       b=e0-e1,
K=2E_00-2E_11.                                         (19)
```

This is a rank-two residual frame and a diagonal pair family.  Both of the
following direct decks have the same pair responses `D=K` and the same
four-port response `T=0`:

```text
B_uv=0 for every pair;

B_12=K,       B_34=-K,       all other B_uv=0.         (20)
```

In the second line the two contributions
`K_12 B_34+B_12 K_34` cancel, and every other term of (4) is zero.  The two
residual-absent Wick decks are different.  Thus `h=0` removes mixed corrected
pair ambiguity but not deck supply.
