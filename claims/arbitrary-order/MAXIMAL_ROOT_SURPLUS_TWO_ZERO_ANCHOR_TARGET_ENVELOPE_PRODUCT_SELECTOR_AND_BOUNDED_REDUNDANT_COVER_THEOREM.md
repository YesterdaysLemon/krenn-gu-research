# Maximum-root surplus-two zero-anchor target envelope, product selector, and bounded redundant cover

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero arbitrary-root physical-module theorem.**  Continue
the promoted two-probe zero-anchor branch of `GLS26`--`GLS27`.  For every
promoted pair target, its complete transverse nuisance lies in an explicit
root envelope generated by the residual-shore tangent and the root supports
of all *other* promoted pair suppliers.  Two exact positive criteria follow.

1. A desired supplier outside its own envelope survives the complete target
   nuisance and gives a legal constant `GLS8` selector, although its physical
   response can still be zero.
2. A projected pure-diagonal direction outside the envelope gives a legal
   selector with the named nonzero physical response.

Consequently, simultaneous failure of every useful pair row forces the
projected diagonal space to remain spanned after deleting any one supplier
label.  In the quotient by the residual-shore tangent, every such spanning
certificate uses at most four supplier labels.  If every desired pair tensor
is fully absorbed, the stronger conclusion holds that every nonzero supplier
vector belongs to a relation on at most five labels.

The theorem includes every pointwise nuisance-rank and response-zero fibre.
Its Laurent-family corollary uses a principal open only to choose one source
contraction and does not discard exceptional fibres from any universal
failure claim.  The coordinate-shore forms `C12/C21/C22`, synchronization,
activity, a simultaneous downstream package, and arbitrary-`r` detector entry
remain open.  Thus this theorem does not close the supply/attachment node or
the global Krenn--Gu conjecture.  The global conjecture remains
**UNRESOLVED**.

## 1. The complete target envelope

Put

```text
Tbar=P_Q(T_Q) subset E,
P=binom(Uhat,2),
t_D=P_Q(g_D) in E tensor V_D^*,
W_D=Slice_D(t_D) subset E                 (D in P).     (4)
```

For a receiving pair target `D`, define the **foreign-supplier envelope**

```text
A_D=Tbar+sum_(E' in P, E'!=D) W_(E') subset E.         (5)
```

Let `N_D^tr` be the complete transverse nuisance of `GLS22`--`GLS23`.

### Theorem 1 (exact zero-anchor target envelope)

At every point satisfying (1),

```text
N_D^tr subset A_D tensor V_D^*                         (6)
```

for every promoted pair target `D in P`.

#### Proof

Use the exact `GLS23` decomposition label by label.

- Its top-anchor nuisance is
  `K(p omega) tensor V_D^*=0`.
- The label `Q` contributes zero because `P_Q(q)=0`.
- A one-`Q` label `{q_s,u}` has, before any target slicing, all of its
  root coefficients in `Tbar` by equations (6)--(8) of `GLS26`.  If `u` is
  in `D`, the retained `u` factor is padded by the missing target factor; if
  `u` is outside `D`, that factor is sliced and both target factors are
  supplied by the identity.  In either case its complete contribution lies
  in `Tbar tensor V_D^*`.
- For a promoted pair label `E'!=D`, `GLS23` contributes a partial slice of
  `t_(E')`, padded by the target factors absent from `E'`.  Equation (3) shows
  that every root coefficient of every such partial slice lies in `W_(E')`.
  Its contribution is therefore contained in `W_(E') tensor V_D^*`.

The desired label `E'=D` is omitted from nuisance.  Summing the displayed
containments proves (6).  `square`

The inclusion is deliberately one-way.  The envelope can contain vectors
which are not nuisance vectors for target `D`.

## 2. Product selectors and the response gate

For `c in {0,1,2}`, set

```text

v_(D,c)=tensor_(u in D)e_(u,c)^*,

```

### Theorem 2 (supplier-coloop legal selector)

If

```text
W_D notsubset A_D,                                    (8)
```

then `[t_D]!=0` in `K_D^tr/N_D^tr`.  More precisely, there are
`rho in E^*` and `eta in V_D` such that the product functional

```text
mu_D=rho tensor eta                                   (9)
```

annihilates `N_D^tr` and has `mu_D(t_D)!=0`.  After normalization it
lifts through `GLS22` to a legal constant full `GLS8` selector.

#### Proof

Choose `w in W_D-A_D`.  By (2), some `eta in V_D` gives
`w=(id tensor eta)(t_D)`.  Finite-dimensional separation supplies
`rho(A_D)=0` and `rho(w)!=0`.  Then (6) shows that (9) kills the complete
nuisance, while `mu_D(t_D)=rho(w)!=0`.  Scale `mu_D` to the denominator-free
`GLS22` normalization `mu_D(t_D)=p`, and use the full/transverse quotient
isomorphism.  `square`

Condition (8) proves legal survival only.  The physical response may vanish.

### Theorem 3 (diagonal-coloop useful row)

If

```text

```

then target `D` has a legal constant full `GLS8` selector with the named

#### Proof


Thus the transverse pure class `[d_(D,c)^tr]` is nonzero.  The complete-target

that response exists.  `square`

The product construction is pointwise and constant in the retained target

### Theorem 4 (deletion-stable diagonal cover)

If no promoted pair target is a useful nonzero-response row, then for every
`D in P`,

```text
L subset sum_(E' in P, E'!=D) Wbar_(E').              (15)
```

For every `0!=ell in L` and every excluded label `D`, there are distinct
labels `E_1,...,E_k`, none equal to `D`, and vectors
`w_j in Wbar_(E_j)` such that

```text
ell=w_1+...+w_k,                 k<=b_Q<=4.            (16)
```

#### Proof

The contrapositive of Theorem 3 gives `Delta_Q subset A_D` for every `D`.
Quotienting by `Tbar` gives (15).  In a representation of `ell` supplied by
(15), combine all summands carrying the same label.  Prune the resulting
vectors to a basis of a minimal subfamily spanning `ell`.  At most `b_Q`
linearly independent vectors remain, proving (16).  `square`

The conclusion concerns the reconstructed diagonal space `L`.  It does not
say that every direction in every `Wbar_D` is redundant: a surviving
zero-response supplier can lie outside the diagonal cover.

### Theorem 5 (bounded supplier relations under full absorption)

Assume the stronger condition

```text
t_D in N_D^tr for every D in P.                       (17)
```

Then

```text
Wbar_D subset sum_(E' in P, E'!=D) Wbar_(E')          (18)
```

for every `D`.  Hence every `0!=w in Wbar_D` belongs to a relation

```text
w+w_1+...+w_k=0,      w_j in Wbar_(E_j),
E_j distinct and E_j!=D,                k<=b_Q.       (19)
```

Thus the relation uses at most `b_Q+1<=5` distinct supplier labels.

#### Proof

By (6) and (17), `t_D in A_D tensor V_D^*`.  Slice all target factors to get
`W_D subset A_D`; quotienting gives (18).  Express `-w` using the other
supplier spaces, combine equal labels, and prune to at most `b_Q` independent
summands exactly as in Theorem 4.  `square`

Full absorption is essential in Theorem 5.  Useful-row failure alone proves
only (15).  For example, in the exact quotient model

```text

Wbar_1=Wbar_2=K e_1,       Wbar_3=K e_2,              (20)
```

the diagonal line remains spanned after deleting any label, but `Wbar_3` is
not contained in `Wbar_1+Wbar_2`.  It models a surviving supplier whose
physical response is zero.

### Theorem 6 (generic useful row or fixed redundant cover)

Exactly one of the following holds over `F`.

#### U. Diagonal-coloop attachment

For some fixed label `D`,

```text
Delta_(Q,F) notsubset A_(D,F).                        (22)
```

Let `r_D=dim_F A_(D,F)` in one fixed finite generator matrix.  One nonzero
augmented `(r_D+1)`-minor witnesses (22).  After clearing it together with
`h_Q`, `p`, and the selected shore-rank minors, there is a nonempty
principal open of fully supported residual contractions on which the same
target `D` satisfies (10).  Every point of that open supplies the legal named
nonzero-response row of Theorem 3.

#### R. Diagonally redundant supplier cover

For every label `D`,

```text
Delta_(Q,F) subset A_(D,F),                           (23)
```

or equivalently

```text
L_F subset sum_(E'!=D) Wbar_(E',F).                   (24)
```

Choose an `F`-basis of `L_F`.  For every basis vector and every deleted label,
there is a certificate of the form (16) using at most `b_Q` fixed supplier
labels.  Clearing all finitely many rational coefficients and the minors
fixing the selected bases gives one nonempty principal open on which those
same certificates specialize.

#### Proof

For each fixed `D`, function-field membership of the finitely generated
spaces either holds or is witnessed by an augmented minor.  If one fails,
clear the nonzero witness minor and the already declared source/rank factors;
Theorem 3 applies at every specialization in the resulting open.  If none
fails, quotient by `Tbar_F`, choose bases, use the dimension argument of
Theorem 4, and clear the finitely many rational coefficients and basis minors.
All cleared factors are nonzero in the Laurent domain, so infinitude of `K`
gives a torus point in the declared open.  `square`

Branch R is a necessary generic structure for universal useful-row failure;
it is not sufficient for failure because (6) is only an upper bound on the
actual nuisance.  The theorem makes no assertion that the same certificates
extend across every rank-drop or assignment-change divisor.  Such fibres
remain governed pointwise by Theorems 1--5 and the exact `GLS22` Fitting
criterion.

Over `F` on the `C12/C21/C22` shore-cover branch of `GLS27`, `L_F=0`; at every
specialization lying in the pointwise coordinate-shore-cover locus, `L=0`.
Criterion (10) is then unavailable.  A supplier-coloop selector from Theorem
2, if present, annihilates every pure diagonal column; the complete target
identity forces its named physical response to be zero.  Exceptional fibres

Complete mixed equations are still needed to attach or exclude the normal
