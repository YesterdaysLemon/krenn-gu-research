# Maximum-root surplus-two zero-anchor two-deficient matching integrability and kernel-edge-zero localization

## Status and scope

**Exact characteristic-zero same-source restriction theorem (`GLS64`).**
Continue from the sole exactly-two-deficient family retained by `GLS63`.
Thus the two deficient joint probe maps have rank two and common kernel
`K e_c`, the four other labels are injective and nonaxis, at least three of
their cross products have identically zero `c` coordinate, no cross product
has identically zero `d` or `e` coordinate, and the deficient-pair companion
has a nonzero binary diagonal contraction.

This document couples three different levels of the `GLS63` mixed-kernel
hierarchy through one denominator-free matching identity.  The pair-
contracted equations kill all six effective port-edge scalars.  The
one-deficient-kernel equations kill four three-port matching cofactors.  An
exact complementary-edge identity then shows that the raw edge between the
two deficient labels vanishes on their common kernel:

```text
W_(nm)(e_c,e_c)=0.                                    (1)
```

Consequently the complete `GLS63` exactly-two-deficient residual is confined
to the divisor (1), for both `|E_c|=3` and `|E_c|=4`.  This is a genuine
source-integrability consequence: all scalars come from one physical edge
table and every vanishing equation comes from the same complete hierarchy.

The theorem does **not** exclude the divisor (1).  On that divisor the
effective port blocks retain the cross terms
`a_i tensor b_j+b_i tensor a_j`, and the raw four-port matching scalar can
remain nonzero.  The three-port and four-port residuals, profiles with three
or more deficient maps, the unique-nonrigid branch, nonzero anchor,
arbitrary-root attachment, responses, selectors, synchronization, nuisance
survival, activity, and global resolution all remain open.  The global
Krenn--Gu conjecture remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 1. Residual notation

Let `F_0` be the characteristic-zero coefficient field generated by the
hypothetical witness.  The identities below live in the polynomial domain
over `F_0` obtained by adjoining the probe and generic kernel coordinates;
write `F` for its fraction field.  Let the deficient labels be `n,m`, and
let

```text
U={0,1,2,3},       Z=E_c subseteq U,       |Z| in {3,4}. (2)
```

Choose the generic kernel vectors `x_n in K_n` and `x_m in K_m`.  For every
`i in U`, let `k_i=p_i cross q_i` be its polynomial cross product and put

```text
eta=W_(nm)(x_n,x_m),
A_i=W_(ni)(x_n,k_i),        B_i=W_(mi)(x_m,k_i),
w_(ij)=W_(ij)(k_i,k_j).                              (3)
```

For an unordered pair `{i,j}` define its effective two-kernel edge scalar

```text
delta_(ij)=eta w_(ij)+A_i B_j+B_i A_j.               (4)
```

For each `u in U`, if `U-{u}={i,j,k}`, define the one-kernel four-vertex
matching scalars

```text
C_u^A=A_i w_(jk)+A_j w_(ik)+A_k w_(ij),
C_u^B=B_i w_(jk)+B_j w_(ik)+B_k w_(ij).              (5)
```

Finally the raw four-port matching scalar is

```text
H=w_(01)w_(23)+w_(02)w_(13)+w_(03)w_(12).            (6)
```

In the `S={n,m}` member of the `GLS63` hierarchy one takes `R=empty` and
`C=U`.  The scalar denoted `h_(nm)` there is exactly
`H_U(k_U)`, the raw four-port matching scalar (6).  Its nonzero binary
equation therefore gives

```text
H!=0.                                                 (7)
```

Here `H` is the complementary four-port deck in that equation; it must not
be confused with `eta`, the raw deficient--deficient edge evaluation.

## 2. Companion nonvanishing

For a label `s`, write `X_s,Y_s` for its two probe-incidence maps and
`J_s=(X_s,Y_s)`.  For distinct labels define the physical companion

```text
g_(st)(z_0,z_1)
 =X_s(z_0) tensor Y_t(z_1)+Y_s(z_1) tensor X_t(z_0). (8)
```

### Lemma 1 (rank-two/nonaxis companion nonvanishing)

Suppose `rank J_s>=2`, while `J_t` is injective and `t` is not on a pure
probe axis.  Then `g_(st)` is not the zero tensor polynomial.

### Proof

The nonaxis hypothesis makes both `X_t` and `Y_t` nonzero.  If `X_s=0`,
then (8) being zero would give `Y_s tensor X_t=0`; this contradicts
`rank J_s>=2` and `X_t!=0`.  The case `Y_s=0` is symmetric.

Assume all four maps are nonzero and (8) vanishes.  Over the infinite field
`F_0`, choose `z_0` outside the two proper kernels of `X_s,X_t` and choose
`z_1` outside the two proper kernels of `Y_s,Y_t`.  Equality of the two
nonzero decomposable tensors in (8) makes `X_s(z_0)` proportional to
`Y_s(z_1)`.  Keeping one variable fixed and varying the other on a nonempty
open set shows that both images `im X_s` and `im Y_s` lie on that same line.
Hence `rank J_s<=1`, a contradiction. `square`

The lemma applies to every pair inside `U`, since both labels are injective
and nonaxis.  It also applies to `(n,u)` and `(m,u)` because the deficient
maps have rank two.

## 3. Same-source equations at two hierarchy levels

### Lemma 2 (all six effective pair scalars vanish)

For every `{i,j} subseteq U`,

```text
delta_(ij)=0.                                         (9)
```

### Proof

Let `{s,t}=U-{i,j}`.  In the `GLS63` hierarchy contract `n,m` at their
generic kernel vectors and contract `i,j` at `k_i,k_j`, leaving only `s,t`
among the auxiliary labels.  The source is exactly

```text
g_(st) delta_(ij).                                    (10)
```

Indeed the complementary four-vertex physical deck on `{n,m,i,j}` has its
three matchings `eta w_(ij)`, `A_iB_j`, and `B_iA_j`.

Since `|Z|>=3`, the contracted pair `{i,j}` meets `Z`.  The deficient kernel
contractions kill target colours `d,e`, and that member of `Z` kills target
colour `c`; hence the target of (10) is zero.  Lemma 1 gives `g_(st)!=0`, so
coefficientwise equality in the domain `F` gives (9). `square`

### Lemma 3 (all one-kernel cofactors vanish)

For every `u in U`,

```text
C_u^A=C_u^B=0.                                       (11)
```

### Proof

Contract only `n` among the deficient labels and contract every member of
`U-{u}` at its cross product, leaving `m,u`.  The sole structurally retained
source pair is `{m,u}`.  Its actual complementary four-vertex deck is

`C_u^A`: pair `n` to one contracted port and match the other two contracted
ports.  Thus the equation is

```text
g_(mu) C_u^A=0.                                      (12)
```

The target is zero.  The `n` contraction kills `d,e`, while `U-{u}` meets
`Z` and therefore kills `c`.  Lemma 1 gives `g_(mu)!=0`, proving
`C_u^A=0`.  Exchanging `n,m` gives `C_u^B=0`. `square`

The condition `|Z|>=3` is load-bearing in both lemmas.  No retained deck is
assumed nonzero; only the displayed companions are proved nonzero.

## 4. Complementary-edge matching identity

### Lemma 4 (denominator-free integrability identity)

If `{k,l}=U-{i,j}`, then

```text
2 eta H + sum_(u in U) B_u C_u^A
 =sum_({i,j} subseteq U) w_(ij) delta_(kl),           (13)
```

where the sum on the right has all six unordered edges.  Symmetrically,

```text
2 eta H + sum_(u in U) A_u C_u^B
 =sum_({i,j} subseteq U) w_(ij) delta_(kl).           (14)
```

### Proof

In `sum_u B_u C_u^A`, the coefficient of `w_(ij)` is

```text
A_k B_l+B_k A_l,       {k,l}=U-{i,j}.                 (15)
```

Substitute (4) for (15).  The resulting six terms
`eta w_(ij)w_(kl)` count each of the three perfect matchings twice, hence
sum to `2 eta H`.  Rearrangement gives (13).  Exchanging `A,B` gives
(14). `square`

This is an identity in the polynomial ring.  In particular it remains valid
on `eta=0`; no localization was used to derive it.

## 5. Deficient-edge-zero localization

### Theorem 5 (`GLS64`)

Every hypothetical witness in the `GLS63` exactly-two-deficient residual
satisfies

```text
eta=W_(nm)(x_n,x_m)=0.                               (16)
```

Equivalently, because both kernels are the line `K e_c`,

```text
W_(nm)(e_c,e_c)=0.                                   (17)
```

### Proof

Insert Lemmas 2 and 3 into (13).  It gives

```text
2 eta H=0.                                           (18)
```

The field has characteristic zero and `H!=0` by (7), so `eta=0`.  Write the
independently chosen generic vectors as
`x_n=alpha_n e_c`, `x_m=alpha_m e_c`, where
`alpha_n alpha_m!=0` in the fraction field.  Then (17) follows. `square`

Thus the `D(eta)` part of both the three-zero and four-zero residuals is
empty.  The conclusion is stronger than excluding one displayed
mixed-orientation table, but weaker than excluding the complete residual.

## 6. Sharp boundary and next obligation

The scalar identity cannot by itself remove `eta=0`.  For example, set
`eta=0`, take `A_i=1`, `B_i=0` for all four ports, and

```text

w_(02)=w_(13)=0.                                     (19)
```

Then all `delta_(ij)` and all `C` cofactors vanish while `H=2`.  This is only
an exact control for the scalar equations, not a physical GHZ witness.

On the retained divisor the effective port blocks are


every zero shore and rank-drop fibre.  Fibre-level independent-deck or
pointwise orientation arguments are insufficient.
