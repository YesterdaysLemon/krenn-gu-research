# Support-one common plane: retained cofactor vanishing

**Proved analytic lemma over C, independently reviewed on 2026-09-04.** This is a proof leaf of the [complete parent theorem](../TWO_ROOT_DIAGONAL_LEG_SOURCE_EXCLUSION_THEOREM.md), not a global exclusion.

## Parent, assumptions, and exact reduction

Use the [parent statement](../TWO_ROOT_DIAGONAL_LEG_SOURCE_EXCLUSION_THEOREM.md) and [N8R2S source reduction](../TWO_ROOT_DIAGONAL_LEG_COMMON_PLANE_REDUCTION_THEOREM.md).

Assume the full ternary eight-vertex matching tensor is GHZ; Q,L_i,M_j are invertible; opposite root legs are nonzero alpha_i E_ii and beta_j E_jj; every physical AA and BB edge is a nonzero matrix unit. The opposite R planes are arbitrary. Suppose the A inactive image planes coincide with coordinate plane U=ker e_0^T. Relabelling covers any support-one normal, and exchanging the shores covers the transposed case.

N8R2S gives, on A inactive and ALL B vectors fully open,

  H=0, P_0 C_AB+T C_BB=0.

The two nonzero rows of P_0 form Z=[x_0,x_1,x_2], where each x_i is an independently arbitrary two-vector: L_i maps its inactive plane isomorphically to U. All operations below are on this full physical one-shore slice. There is NO assumption that H vanishes with A normal coordinates activated.

Projecting to the common normal only gives

  C_BB,01=C_BB,02=0.

The remaining cofactor G=C_BB,12 is retained throughout. It is the actual four-hafnian on A_0,A_1,A_2,B_0.

Let X_ij be the AB edges with only the A slots restricted, p_i the inactive AA edge on the complementary pair, and q_j the FULL BB edge on the complementary pair. Every p_i has bilinear rank at most one, possibly zero; every q_j is a nonzero polynomial because the full BB block is a nonzero unit. Put tau_j=beta_j z_Bj[j], also nonzero polynomials. Exact matching and the full corrected matrix source give

  p^T X=(G,0,0),
  H=per X+q_0 G=0,
  C:=C_AB=per2cof(X)+p q^T,
  Z C[:,0]=0,
  Z C[:,1]=-tau_2 G e_2,
  Z C[:,2]=-tau_1 G e_1.                            (1)

Here e_1,e_2 denote the two coordinate basis vectors of U, and per2cof uses unsigned complementary 2x2 permanents. In particular the free G has NOT been discarded from either corrected source column.

Work over K=C(all B coordinates), then in the polynomial ring K[x_0,x_1,x_2]. This fraction-field passage discards no physical B specialization: the final conclusions are polynomial identities. It is legal because full q_0 and tau_2 are nonzero polynomials. No inactive R determinant is used.

## 1. The root-kernel cofactor column actually vanishes

Write k=(det(x_1,x_2),-det(x_0,x_2),det(x_0,x_1))^T. The polynomial kernel of generic Z is generated by k. The minors have gcd one, and endpoint multidegrees therefore turn the first zero column equation in (1) into

  C[:,0]=k f,

where f belongs to K and is independent of all A variables. We prove f=0.

Set v_i=(a_i,b_i)=(X_i1,X_i2); each v_i is a linear row in its own independent x_i. For complementary indices i,j,k, the cofactor equation is

  a_j b_k+a_k b_j+q_0 p_i=f k_i,                    (2)

and p^T X[:,1]=p^T X[:,2]=0. If f!=0, every right side is a nondegenerate rank-two bilinear form on the complementary A pair.

### Rank-two two-column matrix

Suppose [X[:,1],X[:,2]] has rank two over K(x_0,x_1,x_2).

No row v_i can be identically zero: then a complementary pair involving that row would make the left side of (2) just q_0 p, of bilinear rank at most one.

Suppose a row frame has rank one, v_i=l_i(x_i) w_i with w_i a fixed nonzero K-row. If the other two rows are dependent, they have one common constant row direction by independence of their vertex variables. Since the whole matrix has rank two, its kernel condition forces p_i=0. Their permanent pair then has bilinear rank at most one, contrary to (2).

If instead the other two rows are independent, set x_i on ker l_i. Their kernel equation becomes p_j v_j+p_k v_k=0, so both p_j and p_k vanish on ker l_i, hence are divisible by l_i. Every term in (2) for a pair involving i is then divisible by l_i, again giving bilinear rank at most one, a contradiction.

Consequently all three row frames are invertible 2x2 matrices. If p!=0, its kernel vector is proportional to the three determinant minors of these row frames. Those minors are nondegenerate irreducible bilinear forms in different complementary variable pairs and have gcd one. Thus the proportionality factor is polynomial; endpoint degrees make it a constant in K. A nonzero p_i would have bilinear rank two, contrary to the unit-edge rank bound.

If p=0 instead, the three equations (2) identify symmetric common-middle-form pairings F_j J_+ F_k^T with nonzero scalar multiples of the same alternating 2x2 form, with all F_i invertible. The first two pair equations make two frames proportional, so their mutual symmetric Gram matrix cannot equal the nonzero alternating third target. This is the same elementary three-frame obstruction used in N8R2S. Hence this alternative is impossible too.

### Rank at most one two-column matrix

Rank zero contradicts (2) immediately. In rank one, no row is zero for the preceding reason. Independent vertex variables force

  v_i=l_i(x_i) w

for one common nonzero K-row w. Let sigma=2 w_1 w_2. Then (2) reads

  sigma l_j l_k+q_0 p_i=f k_i,

and p^T[v_0;v_1;v_2]=0 gives sum_i p_i l_i=0. Multiply the three equations by l_i and sum:

  3 sigma l_0 l_1 l_2=f sum_i k_i l_i.

Set x_0=x_1=x_2=x. All k_i vanish. Each nonzero linear form l_i(x) remains a nonzero polynomial, so their product is nonzero and characteristic zero forces sigma=0. Equation (2) would then equate q_0 p_i, of rank at most one, with a nonzero alternating rank-two form. Contradiction.

We have proved, without setting G to zero,

  C_AB[:,0]=0.                                      (3)

## 2. Two mixed cubic coefficients control the remaining row frames

Now (2) with f=0 gives

  p_i=-(a_j b_k+a_k b_j)/q_0.

Substituting into the two kernel equations p^T X[:,1]=p^T X[:,2]=0 yields

  a_0 a_1 b_2+a_0 a_2 b_1+a_1 a_2 b_0=0,
  a_0 b_1 b_2+a_1 b_0 b_2+a_2 b_0 b_1=0.           (4)

These are exact polynomial coefficients, not sign or numerical conditions. Equivalently the product of the three binary forms a_i s+b_i t has no s^2 t or s t^2 term.

Assume for contradiction G!=0. In particular p!=0.

## 3. A zero row cannot carry nonzero G

Suppose v_i=(a_i,b_i)=0. Then p_j=p_k=0, and G=p_i Y_i with Y_i=X_i0. Nonzero G makes p_i and Y_i nonzero. The other two rows need not be classified.

In corrected source column 1, put

  F=Y_j b_k+Y_k b_j+p_i q_1.

The exact equation is

  x_i F+Y_i[x_j b_k+x_k b_j+tau_2 p_i e_2]=0.       (5)

All bracketed coefficients and F are independent of x_i. The map x_i to x_i F has rank two if F!=0, whereas the other summand has rank at most one because Y_i is a scalar linear form. Hence F=0, and since Y_i!=0 the bracket is zero.

Put D=[x_j,x_k]. Cramer's polynomial identity then gives

  det(D) b_k=-tau_2 p_i (adj(D)e_2)_1.              (6)

The determinant is an irreducible nondegenerate bilinear polynomial. It divides neither the nonzero bilinear-rank-one p_i nor the nonzero linear polynomial (adj(D)e_2)_1. Since tau_2 is a nonzero field scalar, the right side of (6) is nonzero and not divisible by det(D), a contradiction. Thus every row v_i is nonzero when G!=0.

## 4. All nonzero rows have rank one

Suppose one row frame v_i has rank two. Set a_i=0,b_i!=0 by choosing x_i. The first identity in (4) forces a_j a_k=0, so one other a-row is identically zero. Set b_i=0,a_i!=0; the second identity forces one other b-row identically zero. Because all rows v_j are nonzero, these two zero choices must belong to different vertices. But then the surviving mixed term a_i a_k b_j (or its symmetric counterpart) in (4) is a product of three nonzero polynomials, a contradiction.

Thus write every row as

  a_i=A_i l_i(x_i), b_i=B_i l_i(x_i),

with l_i nonzero and A_i,B_i in K. If some A_i=0, the two scalar identities in (4) successively force every A_i=0, since no row is zero. Then all p_i vanish, contrary to G!=0. Similarly no B_i can vanish. Hence all A_i,B_i are nonzero.

It follows that

  p_i=eta_i l_j l_k.

The eta_i are nonzero as well: putting r_i=B_i/A_i, the first scalar identity says r_0+r_1+r_2=0, so r_j+r_k=-r_i!=0. No choice of a cube root or support subcase is needed.

## 5. Corrected matrix source forces the remaining X column to share these factors

Let r_i be a nonzero vector spanning ker l_i in the common two-dimensional x coordinates, and put d_i=Y_i(r_i), where Y_i=X_i0.

In source column 1, set x_i=r_i and x_j=r_j, leaving l_k nonzero. Every p vanishes, so G=0 at this specialization. The only surviving cofactor entries are Y_j b_k and Y_i b_k. Since B_k l_k is nonzero, the exact source gives

  r_i d_j+r_j d_i=0                                  (7)

for each of the three unordered pairs.

These three relations force all d_i=0. If one d_i were nonzero, (7) would make all other d_j nonzero and r_j=-(d_j/d_i)r_i. Their remaining pair equation would become -2 d_j d_k r_i/d_i=0, impossible in characteristic zero.

Therefore Y_i vanishes on ker l_i and has the form Y_i=D_i l_i with D_i in K. Consequently

  G=g l_0 l_1 l_2,
  C_i1=c_i l_j l_k

for field scalars g,c_i. Divide the corrected source column 1 by the nonzero product l_0 l_1 l_2:

  sum_i c_i x_i/l_i=-tau_2 g e_2.                   (8)

Each x_i is independently variable. Hold l_i=1 and vary x_i along its nonzero kernel direction r_i; equation (8) forces c_i r_i=0, hence every c_i=0. Its right side then gives g=0 because tau_2!=0. This contradicts G!=0.

We conclude the retained free cofactor is identically zero:

  G=C_BB,12=0.                                      (9)

Together with the normal projection, C_BB=0 on the full one-shore slice. All steps above explicitly retained G until the contradiction.

## 6. Exact reuse and exclusion endpoint

Section 1 is a kernel-column lemma valid with its scalar q_0 replaced
by any q in the coefficient field, including zero; it never divides by q.
The Cramer subargument in Section 3 remains valid when q_0 is zero once
p_j=p_k=0 and p_i D_i!=0 have independently been established: it then
uses the displayed corrected source column. Section 3 originally uses
q_0!=0 to obtain those prerequisites, whereas the two-coordinate proof
supplies them separately from independence of its two nonzero rows.
Sections 2-5 as a whole use q_0 nonzero. This distinction is retained
when the two-coordinate proof reuses the algebraic arguments after its
explicit column combination.

The retained G has been proved zero, so C_BB vanishes on the full
one-shore slice. The [uniform zero-gauge contradiction](uniform-zero-gauge.md)
then supplies all three nonzero target colours using one common-column
specialization and one fixed torus annihilator. No missing colour is
silently restored through the original coordinate normal. This closes
the complete coordinate-normal case, with arbitrary opposite image planes.
