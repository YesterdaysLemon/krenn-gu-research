## Exact statement

Use the standard one-based ordering of the 252 physical entries
`W_uv[a,b]` at `n=8`. Assume the following 18 entries vanish:

```text
19, 73, 92, 109, 163, 181, 182, 190, 199,
200, 208, 209, 217, 227, 229, 230, 235, 238.
```



particular, entries 172 (`W35[0,0]`) and 244 (`W67[0,0]`), which appeared as
nonzero assumptions in the earlier source-core projection, may vanish.

```text

```

## Division-free certificate

Write the four full hafnians as `F1,...,F4`, and put

```text
a = W23[0,0]   b = W23[1,0]   c = W35[0,0]   d = W35[0,1]
x = W05[0,1]   y = W14[0,0]   s = W56[0,0]
z0 = W67[0,0]  z1 = W67[1,0]
H  = h(0145;0000)
K0 = h(012467;000010)
K1 = h(012467;001000).
```

Direct perfect-matching expansion under the 18 zero assumptions gives

```text
F1 = a*z1*H + c*K0
F2 = a*x*y*z1 + d*K0
F3 = b*(s*t*y + z0*H) + c*K1,  where t = W07[0,0]
F4 = b*x*y*z0 + d*K1.
```

Define

```text
R = a*z1*(d*F3-c*F4) - b*z0*(d*F1-c*F2)
N = a*b*s*t*y*z1.
```

Integer polynomial expansion gives `R=N*d` and hence

```text
N*F2 - K0*R = a^2*b*s*t*x*y^2*z1^2.
```

The right side is nonzero under exactly the seven nonzero assumptions. No
division, localization, or nonzero cofactor hypothesis is used. A fully
expanded certificate in the ideal generated by the four full hafnians is

```text
(K0*b*z0*d)*F1
+ (N-K0*b*z0*c)*F2
- (K0*a*z1*d)*F3
+ (K0*a*z1*c)*F4
= a^2*b*s*t*x*y^2*z1^2.
```

Because this is an identity over the integers with right-side coefficient
one, its conditional obstruction is valid over every field. The Krenn--Gu
application in this repository remains over the complex numbers.
