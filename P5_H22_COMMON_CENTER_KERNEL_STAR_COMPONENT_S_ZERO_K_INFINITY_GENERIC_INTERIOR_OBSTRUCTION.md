# Component twenty-three `s=0,k=infinity` generic interior weighted-`H22` obstruction

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero generic function-field theorem.**  Let

```text
K=Q(r,t).
```

On component twenty-three's simultaneous `s=0,k=infinity` corner, the
complete homogeneous weighted-`H22` incidence of the fixed normalized family
is empty over `K`.  This covers every affine marking and every point of the
homogeneous weight line over the generic parameter point.

The underlying all-pair interior has

```text
r*t*(r-t)*(r*t-1) != 0.                           (1)
```

The proof is deliberately stated over `Q(r,t)`, not over the entire integral
localization defined only by (1).  Its selected ordinary-weight minors also
invert `r+t`, `r^2-1`, and `t^2-1`, and the special-weight row-module
certificates are computed over the full coefficient field.  Therefore the
divisors

```text
r+t=0,    r=+/-1,    t=+/-1                      (2)
```

are not silently claimed.  The already proved coordinate axes, `r=t`, and
`rt=1` divisors are separate exact theorems.  The antidiagonal `r+t=0` and
the parameter-infinity boundary are now closed by separate exact packages;
the `r=+/-1` and `t=+/-1` specializations remain open away from their already
closed intersections.  Arbitrary ambient/source changes, arbitrary order,
global gluing, and the global Krenn--Gu conjecture remain **UNKNOWN** or
**UNRESOLVED** as appropriate.  No finite-field calculation is used.

## Generic corner rows

Put

```text
A=(1,1,0,0),   C=(1,-1,0,0),
B=(0,0,1,1),   D=(0,0,1,-1).
```

After the legal row rescaling at `k=infinity`, the corner rows are

```text
alpha=(A,D,B+rD,B+tD),
beta =(B,B,C,C).                                   (3)
```

All sixteen pure coefficients vanish except

```text
T1111=-4.                                          (4)
```

In edge order `01,02,03,12,13,23`, the pair profile over `K` is

```text
(3,3,3,3,3,4).                                    (5)
```

The gcd of the nonzero edge-`23` maximal minors is

```text
8*(r-t)*(r*t-1),                                  (6)
```

which explains the two non-coordinate pair-rank divisors removed in (1).

## Weighted shared-extension system

Mark every plane by

```text
marked_i=beta_i+h_i alpha_i.                      (7)
```

This represents every genuine marked basis after rescaling its second row.
Let `x=(x0,...,x3;y0,...,y3)` be the common fifth-coordinate extension.
For a common homogeneous weight, expand the fourteen mixed words for each of
`D01` and `D23`, and let `M` be their combined `28 x 8` coefficient matrix.

In diagonal order

```text
(A01,B01,A23,B23),                                (8)
```

genuineness requires both beta diagonals to be nonzero and at least one alpha
diagonal to be nonzero.  Full mixed rank is fatal, as is membership of either
beta diagonal in the mixed row module.

## Ordinary finite weights

Use `[lambda:1]` and work first with `lambda^2!=1`.  The verifier checks seven
exact eight-by-eight minors.  Put

```text
C=256*(lambda-1)^4*(lambda+1)^4*(r-t).
```

The first four hold without a marking specialization:

| rows | determinant |
|---|---|
| `(0,1,2,3,8,9,12,16)` | `C*h2*h3*r*t^2*(r^2-1)` |
| `(0,1,3,5,8,9,12,16)` | `-C*h2*r*t*(r^2-1)*(h1*t-1)` |
| `(0,1,3,4,8,9,12,16)` | `-C*h3*t^2*(r^2-1)*(h1*r-1)` |
| `(0,1,3,7,8,9,12,16)` | `C*h0*r*t^2*(r^2-1)` |

The remaining three use rows `(0,1,3,8,9,12,13,16)`:

| restriction | determinant |
|---|---|
| `h0=h3=0,h1=1/t` | `256*r^2*(lambda-1)^5*(lambda+1)^3*(r-t)*(t^2-1)` |
| `h0=h2=0,h1=1/r` | `256*t^2*(lambda-1)^5*(lambda+1)^3*(r^2-1)*(r-t)` |
| `h0=h2=h3=0` | `256*r*t*(lambda-1)^5*(lambda+1)^3*(r-t)*(rt+1-h1(r+t))` |

Since every displayed non-marking factor is a unit in `K`, these minors give
a complete marking branch tree.  Full rank fails only at

```text
h0=h2=h3=0,       h1=(r*t+1)/(r+t).              (9)
```

On (9), the mixed matrix kills exactly the line generated by

```text
z0=(0,0,-1,-1;0,1,0,0).                          (10)
```

The seven-by-seven minor on rows `(0,1,3,8,9,12,16)` and columns
`(0,1,2,3,4,6,7)` is

```text
128*r*t^2*(lambda-1)^4*(lambda+1)^3
    *(r^2-1)*(r-t)/(r+t).                         (11)
```

Thus the residual rank is exactly seven.  Evaluating the four diagonals on
(10) gives

```text
(2*(lambda+1)*(r+t), 0, 0, -2*(lambda+1)).       (12)
```

The required beta diagonal `B01` is zero.  Hence the sole ordinary binary
residual is nongenuine; no ternary test is needed.

## Exceptional and projective weights

The remaining weights are checked by exact standard-basis reduction over

```text
Q(r,t)[h0,h1,h2,h3].                              (13)
```

The diagonal-membership table is

| weight | `(A01,B01,A23,B23) in M` | killing diagonal |
|---|---|---|
| `lambda=1` | `(no,yes,yes,no)` | `B01` |
| `lambda=-1` | `(yes,no,yes,yes)` | `B23` |
| `[1:0]` | `(no,yes,yes,no)` | `B01` |

Thus every shared mixed-kernel extension at an exceptional or projective
weight has a required beta diagonal equal to zero.  Together with the
ordinary branch calculation, this proves generic function-field emptiness
for the complete homogeneous weight line.

## Exact boundary of the claim

This theorem advances the corner from several closed divisors to its generic
two-parameter point.  It does **not** prove every point of the baseline open
(1): the parameter divisors (2) require separate localized calculations, and
the coefficient-field row reductions do not record a uniform denominator
for specialization.  The antidiagonal is now supplied by a separate exact
theorem, leaving `r=+/-1` and `t=+/-1` as the unresolved finite
specializations.  In particular, the function-field theorem alone is not a
complete integral closure of the finite corner.
