# Component twenty-three corner `r=0` finite-`H22` compatibility obstruction

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero normalized-divisor theorem.**  On the
simultaneous `s=0,k=infinity` corner of component twenty-three, every finite
weighted-`H22` ternary incidence is empty on the affine coordinate divisor

```text
r=0,       t finite.
```

For `t!=0`, the complete genuine shared `D01/D23` binary incidence survives
and is described explicitly, but one missing-third-row matrix has full rank
on the entire survivor.  At `t=0`, even the shared binary incidence is empty.

In particular, the normalized all-pair point

```text
(r,t)=(0,2)
```

has a genuine shared finite weighted-`H22` **binary** incidence for the certified
`D01/D23` contraction pair.  Thus a uniform mixed-module obstruction on this
corner is false.  The certificate is rational and is checked directly over
`Q`; no finite-field calculation is used.

This is a counterexample to the proposed **shared-module obstruction route**,
not a counterexample to the Krenn--Gu conjecture.  The certificate supplies
two compatible binary contractions of one five-coordinate extension.  A
second exact calculation below proves that neither it nor any other genuine
member of the complete `r=0,t!=0` survivor family extends through the linear
one-third-row equations of a ternary restriction.  The projective weight,
the projective `t` endpoint, the rest of the corner, arbitrary source order,
global gluing, and the global conjecture remain **UNKNOWN** or
**UNRESOLVED** as appropriate.

## The corner is a genuine valuative limit

Put

```text
A=(1,1,0,0),   C=(1,-1,0,0),
B=(0,0,1,1),   D=(0,0,1,-1).
```

The component-twenty-three family is

```text
U0=<A,B>,
U1=<A+kD,B+sC>,
U2=<s(A-C)+B+rD,C>,
U3=<-s(A+C)+B+tD,C>,
```

on the purity hypersurface

```text
1-rt=ks(t-r).                                      (1)
```

Take an indeterminate `epsilon` and the exact arc

```text
r=0,   t=2,   k=1/epsilon,   s=epsilon/2.          (2)
```

Equation (1) is identically satisfied.  After the legal row rescaling
`A+kD -> epsilon*A+D`, the limit at `epsilon=0` is

```text
alpha=(A,D,B,B+2D),
beta =(B,B,C,C).                                    (3)
```

Direct expansion of the sixteen restricted permanents gives only

```text
T1111=-4.                                           (4)
```

The six pair-image ranks in edge order `01,02,03,12,13,23` are

```text
(3,3,3,3,3,4).                                     (5)
```

Hence (3) is a nonzero all-pair point, not one of the lower-pair endpoints.
For the full corner family the edge-23 four-minor gcd is
`8(r-t)(rt-1)` up to the harmless sign convention for gcds; at `(0,2)` it is
nonzero.

## Complete finite shared-incidence projection on `r=0`

Let `t!=0`, retain an arbitrary finite weight `lambda`, and saturate the
twenty-eight common mixed equations by

```text
A01*B01*B23*t.
```

Here `A23=0` identically, so this is exactly the genuine incidence open.
Exact block elimination of the eight extension coordinates gives, in both
directions,

```text
<h3,h0,t*h1-1>.                                    (6)
```

Thus every genuine shared incidence has

```text
h=(0,1/t,H,0).                                     (7)
```

On (7), the mixed matrix kills

```text
z0=(0,0,-1,-1;0,1,0,0),                           (8)
```

and the seven-by-seven minor on rows `(1,3,4,8,9,12,16)` and columns
`(0,1,2,3,4,6,7)` is

```text
-128*t^2*(lambda-1)^4*(lambda+1)^3.               (9)
```

The four diagonals on `z0` are

```text
(A01,B01,A23,B23)
 =(2t(lambda+1),2H(lambda-1),0,-2(lambda+1)).     (10)
```

Consequently every genuine point has

```text
t*H*(lambda-1)*(lambda+1) != 0,                  (11)
```

the mixed rank is exactly seven, and its projective kernel is the single
line generated by (8).  Conversely, (7)--(11) construct that complete
genuine paired-incidence family.  At `t=0`, a separate exact saturation by
`A01*B01*B23` returns the unit ideal, so no finite paired incidence is lost
at the excluded origin.

## A rational member of the survivor family

Specialize the complete family by marking (3) with

```text
beta_i -> beta_i+h_i*alpha_i,
h=(0,1/2,3,0),                                    (12)
```

and take finite homogeneous weight

```text
[lambda:1]=[2:1].                                 (13)
```

Use the common extension vector, in the order of the four alpha and four
marked-beta extension coordinates,

```text
z=(x0,x1,x2,x3;y0,y1,y2,y3)
 =(0,0,-1,-1;0,1,0,0).                           (14)
```

The corresponding five-coordinate rows are

```text
alpha0=(1, 1,0, 0, 0),   marked0=(0, 0,1,  1,0),
alpha1=(0, 0,1,-1, 0),   marked1=(0, 0,3/2,1/2,1),
alpha2=(0, 0,1, 1,-1),   marked2=(1,-1,3,  3,0),
alpha3=(0, 0,3,-1,-1),   marked3=(1,-1,0,  0,0). (15)
```

The finite contraction rows are

```text
q01=(1,2,0,0,0),   q23=(0,0,1,2,0).             (16)
```

Equivalently, deleting the fifth row after permanent expansion gives

```text
D01(v,e)=(2v0+v1,v2,v3,e),
D23(v,e)=(v0,v1,2v2+v3,e).                       (17)
```

For each contraction, expand all sixteen four-mode binary coefficients.
All fourteen mixed coefficients vanish.  In diagonal order

```text
(A01,B01,A23,B23)
```

the remaining values are exactly

```text
(12,6,0,-6).                                     (18)
```

The combined `28 x 8` mixed matrix has rank seven, and (8) spans its
one-dimensional kernel.  Since

```text
B01*B23=-36 != 0,       A01=12 != 0,             (19)
```

the established shared weighted-`H22` incidence criterion is genuinely
satisfied.  The vanishing `A23` is allowed because the criterion requires
both beta diagonals and at least one of the two alpha diagonals.

## Every paired survivor fails ternary compatibility

Let `gamma_i in Q^5` be the missing third row in mode `i`.  For each mode,
keep one `gamma_i`, choose `alpha` or `marked` independently in the other
three modes, and contract with each row in (16).  The resulting sixteen
one-`gamma` mixed coefficients form a `16 x 5` linear matrix `F_i`.

Every full ternary lift extending the corresponding five-coordinate rows
would require

```text
F_i gamma_i=0,       i=0,1,2,3.                  (20)
```

In row order consisting of the eight binary words for `q01` followed by the
eight for `q23`, exact five-by-five witnesses are

| mode | rows (zero based) | determinant |
|---|---|---:|
| `0` | `(0,1,2,7,9)` | `288` |
| `1` | `(0,2,4,7,8)` | `20736` |
| `2` | `(0,2,7,8,15)` | `-648` |
| `3` | `(0,2,3,7,8)` | `1944` |

These displayed values are the specialization of the uniform mode-zero
witness

```text
det F_0[0,1,2,7,9]
 =8*H*t^2*(lambda-1)^4*(lambda+1).               (21)
```

It is nonzero everywhere on the genuine open (11).  Hence `gamma_0=0` for
every member of the complete paired-survivor family.  At the displayed
rational point all four `F_i` have rank five, and (20) forces every missing
row to zero.
This contradicts a genuine ternary local map and kills its third-colour
diagonal.  The candidate is therefore an exact necessary-incidence survivor
but not a `P_5 -> Delta_3` restriction.  There is no need to expand the
remaining coefficients containing two or more `gamma` rows: the linear
stage is already inconsistent.

## Exact boundary of the claim

The theorem closes every marking and every **finite** weight on the affine
`r=0` divisor of this fixed normalized corner chart.  It does not cover the
projective weight, `t=infinity`, `r!=0`, arbitrary source/ambient changes,
arbitrary order, or global gluing.
