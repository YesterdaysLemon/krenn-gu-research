# Component 21: extension-kernel marked-H31 leading obstruction

## Status and scope

This note closes the two exceptional marked-`H31` extension-kernel lines left
open by the finite-base extension-infinity partial closure for component 21.
The argument is exact over characteristic zero and permits arbitrary finite
markings and arbitrary subordinate component, marking, and extension terms.

Combined with the pinned partial closure, it proves that every fixed-order
marked-`H31` extension-pole arc over the finite nonzero component-21 sheet is
empty.

Finite-weight `H22` common-kernel directions, the zero base `p=q=0`,
component-parameter infinity, marking poles and their intersections,
arbitrary source transformations, arbitrary ambient transformations, and
omitted projective charts remain **UNKNOWN**.  This is not an arbitrary-order
local-to-global theorem.  The global Krenn--Gu conjecture remains
**UNRESOLVED**.

No finite-field computation is used as proof.

## The two coefficient-kernel lines

Use the component bases

\[
\begin{aligned}
 a&=(A+pB,\ell A+C,C,D),\\
 b&=(C+qB,A,B+\kappa A,A+\ell C)
\end{aligned}
\]

from the pinned partial closure.  Its exact Fitting cover leaves only

\[
 E_\epsilon:\qquad
 \kappa=0,\quad \ell=\epsilon,\quad q=\epsilon p,
 \quad p\ne0,\qquad \epsilon\in\{+1,-1\}.       \tag{1}
\]

For distinguished coordinate `d=2,3`, let `M_d` be the `16 x 8` linear
extension-coefficient map.  Put `sigma_2=-1` and `sigma_3=+1`.  In unmarked
coordinates its kernel on (1) is generated by

\[
 Z_{\epsilon,d}=\left(
 -\frac12,-\epsilon,0,\frac{\sigma_d}{2p},
 \frac{\epsilon}{2},0,-\frac1{2p},1
 \right).                                      \tag{2}
\]

The pinned nonzero `7 x 7` minor proves that this is the complete kernel.

## Finite markings and the surviving necessary equation

For arbitrary finite marking coordinates `h_0,...,h_3`, replace

\[
 b_i\longmapsto b_i+h_i a_i,
 \qquad
 Z_{b_i}\longmapsto Z_{b_i}+h_i Z_{a_i}.        \tag{3}
\]

This is the invertible triangular basis change in both the binary and
extension coordinates.  Write `Z^h_{epsilon,d}` for (2) after (3).  Direct
exact substitution gives

\[
 M_d^h Z^h_{\epsilon,d}=0                       \tag{4}
\]

for every `h_i`, both signs, and both `d=2,3`.

Let `O_d^h(z)` be the `8 x 4` mode-three one-marked obstruction map used in
the complete component-21 marked-`H31` ideals.  Each entry of `O_d^h` is
linear in the extension coordinates: after fixing the marked mode, every
remaining permanent has only one extension column.  In the row indexed by
the all-alpha word `000` on modes zero, one, and two, and in column two of
the retained four-coordinate frame, exact substitution yields

\[
 \left(O_d^h(Z^h_{\epsilon,d})\right)_{000,2}=1. \tag{5}
\]

Identity (5) is independent of `p`, every `h_i`, `epsilon`, and `d`.  Thus the
coefficient-kernel direction fails a necessary graph equation even though
its leading binary coefficient tensor is zero.  More generally, linearity
gives the value `c` on every nonzero representative
`c Z^h_{epsilon,d}` of the kernel line.

## Valuative consequence

Let `t` be a uniformizer after any finite Puiseux extension, and suppose a
marked-`H31` arc has finite component and marking centre on (1).  If its
extension coordinates have pole order `m>0` and nonzero leading direction on
the line (2), then for some `c!=0`

\[
 z(t)=t^{-m}\bigl(cZ^h_{\epsilon,d}+O(t)\bigr).
\]

Component and marking coefficients are regular at the centre.  Since the
obstruction map is linear in `z`, equation (5) gives

\[
 \left(O_d^{h(t)}(z(t))\right)_{000,2}
   =t^{-m}\bigl(c+O(t)\bigr),                    \tag{6}
\]

which cannot vanish.  Subordinate extension terms and component or marking
derivatives occur only in higher powers of `t` and cannot cancel (6).

The pinned partial closure already excludes every centre away from (1) and
every leading direction outside (2).  Therefore:

\[
 \boxed{\text{every fixed-order marked-}H_{31}\text{ extension-pole arc over
 the finite nonzero component-21 sheet is empty.}}
\]

This conclusion is confined to the displayed finite source and ambient
chart and to finite markings.  In particular, it is not a properness claim
for the boundaries listed above.
