# Universal five-blocker divisibility lemma

## Status

This is an arbitrary-order algebraic consequence of the
[`FOUR_BLOCKER_IDEAL_OBSTRUCTION.md`](FOUR_BLOCKER_IDEAL_OBSTRUCTION.md).
It turns the pointwise five-blocker lower bound around a rank-at-least-two
edge into five fixed polynomial identities.

It is a structural theorem, not a global contradiction and not a resolution
of the Krenn--Gu conjecture.

## Setup

Work over `C` in a hypothetical three-colour witness.  Fix vertices `p,r`
and orient their edge block as a matrix `M`:

```text
B_pr(x,y) = x^T M y.
```

Assume

```text
rank(M) >= 2.                                           (1)
```

For an outside vertex `u`, orient the two incident blocks by

```text
B_pu(x,z) = x^T A_u z,
B_ru(y,z) = y^T C_u z.                                 (2)
```

Fix a colour `c`, and let `a,b` be the other two colours.  Define the
bilinear blocker determinant

```text
f_(u,c)(x,y)
  = det[
      x^T A_u
      y^T C_u
      e_c^T
    ]

  = +/- x^T F_(u,c) y,                                 (3)

F_(u,c)
  = A_u[:,a] C_u[:,b]^T
    - A_u[:,b] C_u[:,a]^T.
```

The irrelevant sign in (3) depends only on the order chosen for `a,b`.

## Theorem

There are five distinct outside vertices

```text
u_1,...,u_5
```

and fixed colours `c_1,...,c_5` such that

```text
F_(u_j,c_j) = lambda_j M                               (4)
```

for scalars `lambda_j in C`.

Equivalently, each selected blocker determinant is identically a scalar
multiple of the root-edge equation:

```text
f_(u_j,c_j)(x,y)
  = +/- lambda_j B_pr(x,y).                            (5)
```

Thus these are not blocker vertices selected afresh for each zero pair.
One fixed five-vertex pattern holds on a Zariski-dense set of fully
supported zeros of `B_pr`.

## Proof

### The zero hypersurface is irreducible

Put

```text
g(x,y) = x^T M y.
```

A bilinear polynomial factors into two nonconstant linear forms exactly
when its coefficient matrix has rank one.  Assumption (1) therefore makes
`g` irreducible.  Hence

```text
H = Z(g) subset C^3 x C^3
```

is irreducible.

No coordinate variable divides `g`.  Consequently `H` is not contained
in any coordinate hyperplane, and

```text
H^times = H intersect (C^*)^3 x (C^*)^3               (6)
```

is a nonempty dense open subset of `H`.

### A finite constructible cover

For `(x,y) in H^times`, the pair is fully supported and zero-coupled.
The four-blocker ideal obstruction says that at least five distinct
outside vertices block at least one of the three colours.

For fixed `u,c`, the condition

```text
e_c^* belongs to
span(B_pu(x,-), B_ru(y,-))                              (7)
```

is constructible: stratify the two-row matrix by rank, or equivalently
write (7) using the existence of two scalar coefficients.  There are only
finitely many ways to choose five distinct outside vertices and one
blocked colour at each.

It follows that `H^times` is a finite union of constructible sets of the
form

```text
intersection_(j=1)^5 blocker(u_j,c_j).                 (8)
```

An irreducible variety cannot be a finite union of subsets whose Zariski
closures are all proper.  Therefore one fixed choice in (8) is dense in
`H^times`, and hence dense in `H`.

### Divisibility

Actual blocker membership in (7) implies that the three rows in (3) are
linearly dependent.  Thus every `f_(u_j,c_j)` vanishes on the dense set
from (8), and then on all of `H`.

The prime ideal of the irreducible hypersurface `H` is generated by `g`.
Therefore

```text
g divides f_(u_j,c_j).                                 (9)
```

Both polynomials have bidegree `(1,1)`, so the quotient in (9) is a
scalar.  Comparing coefficient matrices gives (4).

## Rank consequences

Every matrix in (3) has rank at most two, since

```text
F_(u,c)
  = [A_u[:,a], -A_u[:,b]]
    [C_u[:,b],  C_u[:,a]]^T.                           (10)
```

Consequently:

1. if any `lambda_j` is nonzero, then `rank(M)=2`;
2. if `rank(M)=3`, then all five `lambda_j` vanish and

   ```text
   F_(u_j,c_j)=0.                                      (11)
   ```

For (11), equality of the two outer products implies that at least one
of the two incident complementary-column pairs is compressed:

```text
rank[A_u[:,a],A_u[:,b]] <= 1
or
rank[C_u[:,a],C_u[:,b]] <= 1.                          (12)
```

Indeed, if the common outer product is nonzero, both column pairs have
rank one.  If it is zero, either both `A` columns vanish, both `C`
columns vanish, or each pair has at most one potentially nonzero column.

Thus every rank-three root edge has five fixed outside vertices at which
one of the two incident blocks has a fixed two-colour compression.  By
pigeonhole, at least three of those five compressions occur at the same
root endpoint, though their omitted colours need not agree.

## Verification

Run:

```text
python verify_universal_five_blocker_divisibility.py
python audit_universal_five_blocker_divisibility.py
```

The primary verifier reconstructs (3) symbolically, checks the rank-two
factorisation (10), and checks the scalar-multiple and rank
consequences.  The independent audit exhausts all pairs of `3 x 2`
matrices over `F_3`, verifying the zero-outer-difference implication
(12) in 531,441 cases.  These computations audit the algebraic formulas;
the irreducibility and constructible-cover argument above is the proof
over `C`.

## Boundary

The new identities do not yet rule out the case in which all five
scalars in (4) vanish.  The next target is to propagate the forced
one-sided compressions in (12) through adjacent rank-three edges, or to
show that they contradict the connectivity and pure-matching
requirements of a support-minimal witness.
