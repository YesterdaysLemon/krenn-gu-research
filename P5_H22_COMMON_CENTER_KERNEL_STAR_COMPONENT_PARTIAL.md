# Weighted-`H22` partial closure for component twenty-three

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero partial theorem.**  For the common-center-kernel
star component twenty-three, the complete generic weight-at-infinity
weighted-`H22` incidence is empty for every affine marking.  The complete
finite-weight incidence is also empty on the exact function-field slice
`t=3` over `C(r)`, and at the generic component point for the canonical
marking `h=0`.

The finite-weight calculation with all four markings retained over the full
two-parameter field `C(r,t)` remains **UNKNOWN**.  Several exact rational
fibres have the same empty module, but those specializations are audits and
are not promoted to a generic theorem.  Special/projective component fibres,
the arbitrary-order local-to-global reduction, and the global Krenn--Gu

## Shared mixed module

Use the normalized rows from
[`P5_H31_COMMON_CENTER_KERNEL_STAR_COMPONENT_GENERIC_OBSTRUCTION.md`](P5_H31_COMMON_CENTER_KERNEL_STAR_COMPONENT_GENERIC_OBSTRUCTION.md),

```text
alpha=(A, A+kD, A-C+B+rD, -A-C+B+tD),
beta =(B, B+C, C, C),
k=(1-rt)/(t-r),
```

and mark `beta_i -> beta_i+h_i alpha_i`.  Let `z=(x0,...,x7)` be the shared
fifth-coordinate extension.  For the same homogeneous weight, form both
binary contractions `D01` and `D23`, and let `M` be the module generated by
their twenty-eight mixed coefficient rows in `z`.

The inherited pure `P_4` support is the all-beta coefficient.  Consequently
every genuine weighted-`H22` lift requires both beta diagonals `B01` and
`B23` to be nonzero, while at least one of `A01,A23` must be nonzero.  It is
therefore enough to prove `B01 in M`; no ternary survivor analysis is needed
on a chart where that membership holds.

All rational component denominators are cleared by one common multiplier per
module row.  Clearing entries separately is invalid and is not used.

## Generic weight infinity

Over

```text
C(r,t)[h0,h1,h2,h3]
```

the direct infinity maps retain `(X0,X2,X3,e)` for `D01` and
`(X0,X1,X2,e)` for `D23`.  Exact standard-basis reduction gives

```text
M = <e1,e2,e3,e4,e5,e6,e7,e8>.                 (1)
```

The verifier checks both inclusions in (1), not only equality of ranks.  All
four diagonals `A01,A23,B01,B23` reduce to zero modulo `M`.  In particular
`B01=0` on every shared mixed kernel, contradicting genuineness.  The generic
weight-at-infinity fibre is empty for every marking.

## Exact finite partial theorems

On the finite chart `[lambda:1]`, two exact calculations give

```text
M=<e1,e2,e3,e4,e6,e7,e8,(lambda-1)e5>.          (2)
```

The first is over `C(r,t)[lambda]` at the canonical marking `h=0`.  The
second retains every marking over the exact slice

```text
C(r)[h0,h1,h2,h3,lambda],   t=3.                (3)
```

Again the verifier checks both module inclusions.  In both calculations

```text
A01 in M,  A23 in M,  B01 in M,  B23 notin M.   (4)
```

Thus `B01` vanishes on every shared mixed kernel and the corresponding
finite weighted-`H22` incidences are empty.  Equation (2) also records the
only possible shared extension direction at `lambda=1`; (4) kills its
required inherited beta diagonal.
