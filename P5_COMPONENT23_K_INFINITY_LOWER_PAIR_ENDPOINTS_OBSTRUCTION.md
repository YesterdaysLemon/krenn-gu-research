# Component twenty-three `k=infinity` lower-pair endpoint obstructions

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero theorem at two normalized projective-parameter
points.**  On component twenty-three, put `q=1/k` and take the `q=0`
boundary on which `t=r`.  At each of the two lower-pair endpoints

```text
r=t=+1,                 r=t=-1,
```

the complete affine-marked `H31` fibre and the complete homogeneous-weight
`H22` fibre are empty for the fixed normalized contraction order.  All
calculations are over `Q`; no finite-field calculation is used.

This is a pointwise theorem on the displayed normal-form boundary.  It does
not cover arbitrary ambient or source bases, omitted Grassmann charts,
other projective component-parameter directions, arbitrary contraction
order, or the local-to-global reduction.  The Krenn--Gu conjecture remains
**UNRESOLVED**.

## Exact lower-pair placement

With

```text
A=X0+X1,  C=X0-X1,  B=X2+X3,  D=X2-X3,
```

the endpoint rows are

```text
alpha=(A,D,A-C+B+rD,-A-C+B+rD),
beta =(B,B+C,C,C).                                  (1)
```

For either sign the pair profile, in edge order
`01,02,03,12,13,23`, is

```text
(3,3,3,4,4,2).                                      (2)
```

The rank-two edge `23` has two reduced Segre-kernel points.  At `r=+1`
their zero-product support pairs are `{1,2}` and `{0,2}`; at `r=-1` they
are `{1,3}` and `{0,3}`.  They are genuine binary supports meeting in one
coordinate.  Hence this is the overlapping-secant stratum of the original
six-dimensional lower-pair component, not the disjoint-secant component.

The placement is explicit.  Reorder the modes as `(2,3,0,1)`.  For `r=+1`
use source order `(X2,X1,X0,X3)`, and for `r=-1` use
`(X3,X1,X0,X2)`.  In both cases the four row spaces become the overlapping
secant normal form of
[`P4_OVERLAPPING_SECANT_LOWER_PAIR_CLASSIFICATION.md`](P4_OVERLAPPING_SECANT_LOWER_PAIR_CLASSIFICATION.md)
at

```text
(s,t,lambda_flag,u,n,v)=(1,1,2,1,2,1).              (3)
```

The pure tensor in the basis (1) has only `T_1111=-4` nonzero.  Replacing
`beta_i` by the omitted projective marking `alpha_i` makes the inherited
pure coefficient zero, because each of `T_0111,T_1011,T_1101,T_1110`
vanishes.  Thus `[alpha_i]` cannot carry the required nonzero inherited
pure factor and is automatically nongenuine.  Every genuine marking is
therefore represented by the four affine shifts

```text
beta_i -> beta_i+h_i alpha_i;                        (4)
```

The verifiers assert both the nonzero all-beta coefficient and the four
vanishing projective-marking coefficients before using this affine cover.

## Marked `H31`

For source-coordinate deletion `d`, let `M_d(h)` be the `14 x 8` mixed
extension matrix and let `A_d,B_d` be its two binary diagonal rows.  Exact
elimination of the eight extension entries, after normalizing `A_d=1` and
inverting `B_d`, leaves the following complete marking schemes:

| sign | deletion | marking `(h0,h1,h2,h3)` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+1` | `0` | `(0,1,1/2,0)` |
| `+1` | `1` | `(0,1,0,1/2)` |
| `+1` | `2` | `(0,1,1/2,1/2)` |
| `+1` | `3` | none |
| `-1` | `0` | `(0,-1,1/2,0)` |
| `-1` | `1` | `(0,-1,0,1/2)` |
| `-1` | `2` | none |
| `-1` | `3` | `(0,-1,1/2,1/2)` |

At every listed marking, `rank M_d=5`.  In the exact nullspace basis used by
the verifier, write an extension as `c0 K0+c1 K1+c2 K2`.  For deletion zero,
the two diagonals are nonzero multiples of

```text
c1-c2,                   2c1+c2,
```

and the mode-zero minor on rows `0137` is

```text
-256/27 (c1-c2)(2c1+c2)^2.                         (5)
```

For deletion one the two diagonals are nonzero multiples of

```text
c1-c2,                   c1+2c2,
```

and the mode-zero minor on rows `0237` is

```text
-256/27 (c1-c2)(c1+2c2)^2.                         (6)
```

For the last surviving deletion (`2` at `r=+1`, `3` at `r=-1`), the two
diagonals are nonzero multiples of `c1-c2` and `c1+c2`.  Two mode-two
minors are

```text
rows 0127: -32 c1(c1-c2)(c1+c2),
rows 0137: -16 c2(c1-c2)(c1+c2).                   (7)
```

Thus every genuine binary extension has a rank-four marked local map:
(5) and (6) are immediately nonzero when both diagonals are nonzero, while
in (7) at least one of `c1,c2` is nonzero.  This excludes every marked `H31`
lift at both endpoints.

## Weighted `H22`

For the same marking and shared extension, form both labelled contractions
`D01` and `D23`.  On the finite homogeneous-weight chart `[lambda:1]`, let
`M` be the module generated by their twenty-eight mixed rows over
`Q[h0,h1,h2,h3,lambda]`.  With `e1,...,e8` the standard row generators, the
exact standard bases are

```text
r=+1:
<e1,e4-e3,e7,e8,lambda e2,(lambda-1)e3,
 (lambda-1)e5+e2,lambda e6-e3,h3 e3,h2 e3,
 (h1-1)e2-e6+e3,(h1-1)e3,h0 e3>,                  (8+)

r=-1:
<e1,e2,e4-e3,e6-e3,e7,e8,(lambda-1)e3,
 (lambda-1)e5,h3 e3,h2 e3,(h1+1)e3,h0 e3>.        (8-)
```

Both inclusions are checked against the raw mixed-row modules.  In diagonal
order `(A01,A23,B01,B23)`, membership is

```text
(no,yes,yes,no)                                    (9)
```

for both signs.  Since the inherited pure tensor is the all-beta word, a
genuine weighted lift requires both `B01` and `B23` nonzero.  But `B01 in M`
forces `B01=0` on every shared mixed kernel.  This closes every finite
homogeneous weight at once, including `lambda=0,+1,-1`.

On the projective weight chart, the `r=+1` mixed module is all of `Q[h]^8`.
For `r=-1` it is

```text
<e1,e3,e4,e5+e2,e7,e8,(h1+1)e2-e6>,               (10)
```

and all four diagonals belong to it.  Hence the projective weight is empty
at both endpoints as well.

## Existing coverage and boundaries

The generic marked-`H31` and weighted-`H22` theorems for the original
six-dimensional lower-pair component do not specialize automatically to
these parameter-boundary points.  The equal-weight binary theorem also does
not cover all homogeneous weights.  Equations (5)--(10) are fresh pointwise
certificates and are the only coverage claimed here.
