# Component 21: the \(p=q=0\) first-normal blow-up transfers to the vertical-\(U_0\) obstruction

## Status and scope

This note proves an exact characteristic-zero result for the collapsed
\(p=q=0\) point of the displayed component-21 chart.  The exceptional divisor
of the first projectivized normal blow-up is the projective vertical-\(U_0\)
line.  Its finite chart is the normalized vertical sheet already classified
in `P5_COMPONENT21_VERTICAL_U0_PROJECTIVE_BOUNDARY_COMPLETE_OBSTRUCTION.md`.
The one missing endpoint is classified here by eight direct unit-ideal
computations.  Consequently every point of this exceptional divisor is empty
for fixed-order marked \(H_{31}\) and homogeneous weighted \(H_{22}\), including
both weight charts, finite \(\ell\), and \(\ell=\infty\).

The finite chart is a transport theorem, while the endpoint is a new exact
elimination.  The cited vertical-sheet primary and independent audit were
replayed before this note was written.  The new primary and no-import audit
independently certify the normal-cone identification, pin all three dependency
hashes, and reconstruct the endpoint unit ideals with different permanent
algorithms.

The claim is only about the first normal blow-up inside the displayed
component chart, with finite \(\kappa\) and the fixed source/target order used
by the certified vertical sheet.  It does **not** classify the central zero
tensor without a nonzero normal direction, an iterated compactification after
the first exceptional divisor, a \(\kappa=\infty\) direction, or arbitrary
ambient, source, or projective degenerations.  The arbitrary-order
local-to-global reduction remains open.  The global Krenn--Gu conjecture
remains **UNRESOLVED**.

No finite-field calculation is used as proof.

## The regular chart and its zero tensor

Put

\[
 A=(1,1,0,0),\quad C=(1,-1,0,0),\quad
 B=(0,0,1,1),\quad D=(0,0,1,-1).
\]

Use the regular basis of the mode-zero plane

\[
 r_0=A+pB,\qquad r_1=C+qB,
 \]

and the unchanged bases

\[
 (a_1,b_1)=(\ell A+C,A),\quad
 (a_2,b_2)=(C,B+\kappa A),\quad
 (a_3,b_3)=(D,A+\ell C).
\]

With \((a_0,b_0)=(r_0,r_1)\), direct permanent expansion gives exactly

\[
 T_{0111}=4p,\qquad T_{1111}=4q,
\]

and the other fourteen pure coefficients vanish.  Thus the basis itself is
regular at \(p=q=0\), but the pure \(P_4\) tensor there is zero.  In
particular, the central point alone has no projective \(P_5\) restriction;
one must retain a nonzero leading direction.

## Exact normal-cone identification

Let \(H=\langle A,C\rangle\).  In the Grassmann chart around \(H\), the plane
\(U_0(p,q)\) is the graph of

\[
 f_{p,q}:H\longrightarrow\langle B\rangle,\qquad
 A\longmapsto pB,\quad C\longmapsto qB.
\]

Equivalently, its Pluecker vector is

\[
 (A+pB)\wedge(C+qB)
 =A\wedge C+qA\wedge B+pB\wedge C.                 \tag{1}
\]

For a nonzero first normal direction \([P:Q]\), the exceptional Pluecker
vector is

\[
 QA\wedge B+PB\wedge C=(QA-PC)\wedge B.           \tag{2}
\]

Hence the exceptional plane is canonically

\[
 U_0^{\mathrm{exc}}[P:Q]=\langle QA-PC,B\rangle.   \tag{3}
\]

On \(Q\ne0\), put \(\alpha=P/Q\).  Equation (3) becomes
\(\langle A-\alpha C,B\rangle\), precisely the finite chart of the certified
vertical-\(U_0\) sheet.  The endpoint \([P:Q]=[0:1]\) is \(\alpha=0\), while
\([1:0]\) is \(\langle C,B\rangle\), precisely \(\alpha=\infty\).  Therefore
the map

\[
 [P:Q]\longmapsto\alpha=[P:Q]
\]

identifies the complete exceptional \(\mathbf P^1\) with the complete
projective \(\alpha\)-line of that vertical sheet.  This is an equality of
Pluecker points, so it does not rely on subtracting an ambient vector from a
basis vector of \(U_0(p,q)\).

In the finite \(\alpha\)-chart the vertical intrinsic basis has only
\(T_{1111}=4\) for finite \(\ell\), and only \(T_{1111}=-4\) at
\(\ell=\infty\).  The cited obstruction theorem covers every finite
\(\alpha\), including \(\alpha=0\), in both of those \(\ell\)-charts.  It does
not itself include \(\alpha=\infty\); that endpoint is treated next.

## Direct \(\alpha=\infty\) certificate

At \([P:Q]=[1:0]\), use the regular replacement plane

\[
 U_0^{\mathrm{exc}}=\langle C,B\rangle.
\]

For finite \(\ell\), take

\[
 a=(C,\ell A+C,C,D),\qquad
 b=(B,A,B+\kappa A,A+\ell C).
\]

Only \(T_{1111}=4\) is nonzero.  At \(\ell=\infty\), take

\[
 a=(C,A,C,D),\qquad b=(B,C,B+\kappa A,C),
\]

where only \(T_{1111}=-4\) is nonzero.

For marked \(H_{31}\), distinguished vertices zero and one have identically
zero all-\(a\) diagonal and fail Hall immediately.  For each of distinguished
vertices two and three, in each \(\ell\)-chart, form the ideal generated by
the fourteen mixed equations, normalize one binary diagonal to one, invert
the other, and adjoin all 32 entries of the mode-three one-marked map.  Exact
reduced Groebner calculation over \(\mathbb Q\) gives the unit ideal in all
four cases.  Parameters \(\kappa,h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3\), and finite \(\ell\), remain
polynomial variables; no generic denominator is inverted.

For weighted \(H_{22}\), the all-\(a\) diagonal of the \(D_{01}\) contraction
is identically zero, so Hall forces \(D_{01}\) to be pure and \(D_{23}\) to be
binary.  In each finite/infinite homogeneous weight chart and each
finite/infinite \(\ell\)-chart, impose the fifteen unwanted \(D_{01}\)
coefficients, normalize its all-\(b\) coefficient, impose the fourteen mixed
\(D_{23}\) coefficients, invert both \(D_{23}\) diagonals, and adjoin all 32
entries of its mode-three obstruction map.  The four resulting ideals are
unit ideals over \(\mathbb Q\).  Again all markings and \(\kappa\) are retained
polynomially.

Thus the endpoint proof consists of eight direct characteristic-zero unit
certificates: four for marked \(H_{31}\) and four for weighted \(H_{22}\).

## DVR and Puiseux directions

Let \(p(t),q(t)\) lie in a characteristic-zero DVR, or in a finite Puiseux
extension, and suppose they are not both identically zero.  Divide by their
smallest valuation.  Their leading pair \((P,Q)\ne(0,0)\) determines the
unique exceptional point above.

* If \(v(p)>v(q)\), then \([P:Q]=[0:1]\) and \(\alpha=0\).
* If \(v(p)<v(q)\), then \([P:Q]=[1:0]\) and \(\alpha=\infty\).
* If \(v(p)=v(q)\), then \(\alpha=P/Q\) is finite and nonzero.

Thus unequal valuations introduce no missing point of this first exceptional
divisor.  This statement concerns the \((p,q)\)-normal direction only.  It
does not assert properness for simultaneous poles in other parameters or for
arbitrary source and extension data.

### Why the source-torus gauge does not yet remove \(\kappa=\infty\)

The exact component-21 parameterization has the source-torus stabilizer

\[
 (p,q,\kappa,t_0,t_1)\longmapsto
 (\sigma p,\sigma q,\kappa/\sigma,\sigma t_0,\sigma t_1).
\]

The characteristic-zero component verifier was replayed and checks this
identity in Grassmann coordinates.  Over a field and for \(\kappa\ne0\), the
choice \(\sigma=\kappa\) gives the invariants \(P=p\kappa\), \(Q=q\kappa\),
and \(\kappa'=1\).  Constant invertible diagonal source changes are legal:
the permanent is multiplied only by the product of the column scalars.

This does not by itself prove a \(\kappa=\infty\) boundary theorem.  Along a
DVR arc where \(\kappa\) has a pole, \(\sigma=\kappa\) is not a unit and the
diagonal source change does not extend as an automorphism of the fixed DVR
lattice; \(t_0,t_1\) acquire poles, and extension or marking coordinates may
do so as well.  A proper quotient/lattice comparison would be needed before
transporting such a special fibre.  Therefore \(\kappa=\infty\) remains
outside this atlas, even though \([P:Q]\) again suggests the same vertical
plane on the generic fibre.

## Transported obstruction

The pinned vertical-sheet theorem proves, by eight exact saturated
characteristic-zero eliminations and explicit kernel/minor certificates, that
its finite \(\alpha\)-chart, including its complete \(\ell\)-compactification,
has no marked \(H_{31}\) fibre and no weighted \(H_{22}\) fibre.  Its
independent no-import audit reconstructs the same incidence schemes with a
separate permanent algorithm and proves the eight full mode-3-zero ideals are
unit ideals.  The direct certificates in the preceding section supply the
remaining \(\alpha=\infty\) endpoint.

Equations (1)--(3) identify every first-normal exceptional point here with one
and only one point of that certified sheet.  The source/target order, the
remaining component-21 planes, and the parameters \(\kappa,\ell\) are
unchanged.  Therefore:

**The complete first projectivized \((p,q)\)-normal exceptional divisor over
the component-21 point \(p=q=0\) contains no fixed-order marked \(H_{31}\)
fibre and no homogeneous weighted \(H_{22}\) fibre.**

This closes the ordinary first normal direction, including all equal- and
unequal-valuation DVR/Puiseux arcs whose remaining parameters specialize
inside the cited normalized vertical compactification.  It does not turn the
zero tensor at the centre into a projective point and does not close the
explicitly excluded directions above.
