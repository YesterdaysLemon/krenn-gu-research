# Three clean windows do not recover one complete pair-face block

## Status

**Exact characteristic-zero selector-matroid no-go.**  In the canonical
`P_7` blocker profile, every clean four-window avoids the unique triple
blocker and lies on the same six double blockers.  Suppose, optimistically,
that one clean window is available from each of the three pure colour charts
and that every marked-star observation on those windows is legally exposed.

Even then, the combined observations recover at most five of the six pair
faces of any selected window.  A nonzero invisible pair-face direction
always survives.  The bound five is attained, so the statement is sharp.

This closes the route "one clean window per colour implies partition
closure."  It does not exclude additional lower jets with non-star defect
signatures, more than three clean windows, or mixed-colour equations.

The proof classifies nine constant selector-matroid isomorphism types and
gives an explicit integer kernel witness for each.  It performs no graph,
support, alignment, or colour-word enumeration.

## 1. Marked-star observation spaces

Let the six double blockers be

```text
P={1,2,3,4,5,6}.                                      (1)
```

For a four-window `W subset P`, its marked-star observation matrix has one
row for every `i in W` and one column for every pair `e in binom(P,2)`:

```text
A_W[i,e]=1  if i in e subset W,
          =0  otherwise.                              (2)
```

Thus `A_W x` is the vector of unsigned degrees of the pair-face weighting
`x` in the induced `K_4` on `W`.  Over characteristic different from two,
`rank A_W=4`; its restriction to the six edges of `W` has a two-dimensional
kernel.

For three windows `W_0,W_1,W_2`, put

```text
A=[A_W0;A_W1;A_W2].                                   (3)
```

Let `T=binom(W_0,2)` be the six target columns and let `N` be the other nine
nuisance columns.

### Lemma 1 (recovery rank)

The dimension of the target pair-face space linearly recoverable while
cancelling every nuisance face is

```text
rec(W_0;W_1,W_2)=rank A-rank A[:,N].                  (4)
```

In particular, all six target faces are recoverable if and only if every
`x in ker A` has `x|_T=0`.

Proof.  Equation (4) is the dimension of the row space of `A` modulo the
subspace generated by nuisance columns.  Dually, failure of full recovery is
equivalent to a kernel vector with nonzero target projection.

## 2. Constant isomorphism reduction

By relabelling the six blockers, fix

```text
W_0=1234.                                              (5)
```

An additional window different from `W_0` has one of two forms:

```text
B: three vertices of W_0 and one of {5,6};
C: two vertices of W_0 and both of {5,6}.              (6)
```

The stabilizer `S_4 x S_2` of (5), together with interchange of `W_1,W_2`,
reduces two additional windows to the nine rows below.  The types are
distinguished by `B/C`, their intersection size, and, for the two `B/B`
intersection-three cases, whether the outside vertex agrees.  Repeated
copies of `W_0` add no row space and are easier.

Use edge order

```text
12,13,14,15,16,23,24,25,26,34,35,36,45,46,56.        (7)
```

For compactness, a displayed signed edge sum denotes the corresponding
integer pair weighting; omitted edges have weight zero.

## 3. Sharpness

Case 2 attains five.  For

```text
(W_0,W_1,W_2)=(1234,1256,1456),                       (8)
```

exact integer elimination gives

```text
rank A=11,             rank A[:,N]=6.                 (9)
```

For a lower-rank certificate, the submatrix of `A` on its first eleven rows
and edge columns

```text
12,13,14,15,16,23,24,25,26,34,45                     (10)
```

has determinant `4`.  For the nuisance matrix, rows

```text
(W_1,1),(W_1,2),(W_1,5),(W_1,6),(W_2,4),(W_2,5)      (11)
```

and nuisance columns

```text
15,16,25,26,45,56                                    (12)
```

have determinant `-2`.  Direct row reduction supplies the matching upper
bounds.  Hence (4) equals five in characteristic zero.

### Theorem 2 (three-window pair-face no-go)

For any three four-subsets of a six-element port set and any chosen one of
them as target,

```text
rec(W_0;W_1,W_2)<=5.                                  (13)
```

The upper bound is sharp.

Proof.  Repeated target windows add nothing.  Otherwise apply the
`S_4 x S_2` reduction and the nine explicit kernel witnesses.  Sharpness is
(8)--(12).

## 4. Consequence for the canonical P7 windows

The canonical blocker types are

```text
012, 01,01, 02,02, 12,12.                             (14)
```

Every clean window constructed by marked permanental Laplace contains four
double blockers and excludes the unique `012` blocker.  Therefore one clean
window from each pure colour chart is exactly a triple of four-subsets on
the same six-element set in Theorem 2.

Even granting independent top selectors and every marked-star lower
observation on all three windows, one selected pair block retains at least
one invisible direction.  The two-residual dual-Wick four-point equation
cannot be evaluated from these observations alone.

The result does not say that the same three windows occur in a hypothetical
witness; it is stronger as a no-go: no choice of three windows suffices.

## 5. What can still work

The exact missing information can arrive from:

1. a fourth clean window whose star row has a nonzero defect signature;
2. a non-star lower jet supplying one of the two missing pair-defect rows;
3. a mixed-colour equation coupling the invisible directions of the three
   scalar charts;
4. a direct nonlinear elimination using common physical edge blocks.

The theorem rules out only the three-window linear sensor-fusion route.
