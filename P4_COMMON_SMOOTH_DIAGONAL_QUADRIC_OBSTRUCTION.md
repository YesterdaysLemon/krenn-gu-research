# A spinor obstruction on the common smooth diagonal-quadric locus

## Status

This is an exact algebraic-geometric theorem over `C`.

Let

```text
(U_0,U_1,U_2,U_3) in Gr(2,4)^4
```

and let `ell_i=P(U_i^perp)` be the four annihilator lines.  Suppose
that the `ell_i` lie on one smooth diagonal quadric and that each
`ell_i` lies on a unique diagonal quadric, equivalently

```text
dim D(ell_i)=1,

D(ell)={d : sum_j d_j X_j^2 vanishes on ell}.       (1)
```

Then the restriction of `P_4` to
`U_0 tensor U_1 tensor U_2 tensor U_3` is not both nonzero and
decomposable.

Thus a nonzero pure restriction whose four annihilator lines share a
smooth diagonal quadric must meet the block-line jump locus of the
cubic diagonal-quadric map.  This explains why the apparent new
mixed-ruling spinor solutions have dimension only four after restoring
the diagonal source torus: they are boundary strata, not new
five-dimensional components.

The theorem is an obstruction inside the common-quadric locus.  It
does not prove that every pure restriction has a common diagonal
quadric, does not classify every pure `P_4` component, and does not
settle `H31`, `H22`, or the global prize problem.

## Normalize the quadric

A smooth diagonal quadric has four nonzero coefficients.  A diagonal
source rescaling, which changes `P_4` only by an overall nonzero
factor, normalizes it over `C` to

```text
Q: X_0^2+X_1^2+X_2^2+X_3^2=0.                      (2)
```

The two rulings of `Q` have the following affine line charts:

```text
L(s)=span(
 (1,-i,-s,-i s),
 (s, i s,1,-i)),

R(s)=span(
 (1,-i, s,-i s),
 (s, i s,-1,-i)).                                  (3)
```

Polynomial bases of their ordinary annihilator planes are

```text
U_L(s)=span(
 (s^2-1, i(s^2+1),2s,0),
 (-(s^2+1),i(1-s^2),0,2i s)),

U_R(s)=span(
 (1-s^2,-i(s^2+1),2s,0),
 (-(s^2+1),i(1-s^2),0,2i s)).                      (4)
```

The second row in (4) has been rescaled by `i`; this does not change
the plane and makes every restricted permanent coefficient rational
over `Q(s_0,s_1,s_2,s_3)`.

For either ruling, the signed cubic Pluecker formula for the unique
diagonal quadric is

```text
d_j=(-1)^j product_{a<b, a,b != j} p_ab.
```

Substitution of (3) gives

```text
delta(L(s))=delta(R(s))
           =2s(s^4-1)(1,1,1,1).                    (5)
```

The missing point `s=infinity` is also a block line.  Consequently a
line in either ruling has `dim D(ell)=1` exactly when its parameter is
finite and

```text
s(s^4-1) != 0.                                     (6)
```

## Spinor ruling patterns

Up to permuting the four modes and exchanging the two rulings, four
lines on `Q` have one of only three patterns:

```text
LLLL,   LLLR,   LLRR.                              (7)
```

For a pattern `rho` and parameters `s_0,...,s_3`, let `T_rho` be the
`2 x 2 x 2 x 2` tensor obtained by restricting `P_4` to the four
planes (4).  Let `I_rho` be the ideal of all single-mode flattening
`2 x 2` minors, and let `K_rho` be the ideal generated by the sixteen
entries of `T_rho`.  The closure of the locus where `T_rho` is
nonzero and decomposable has ideal

```text
J_rho = I_rho : K_rho^infinity.                    (8)
```

After primitive deduplication, the systems contain

```text
pattern   nonzero tensor positions   distinct entries   flattening minors
LLLL                 14                     7                  52
LLLR                 14                    13                  88
LLRR                 14                    13                  88.          (9)
```

Exact characteristic-zero saturation in a global degree order gives

```text
J_LLLL=(1),

dim J_LLLR=1,
dim J_LLRR=1.                                      (10)
```

Put

```text
B=product_{j=0}^3 s_j(s_j^4-1).                    (11)
```

The decisive second saturation is

```text
(J_LLLR : B^infinity)=(1),
(J_LLRR : B^infinity)=(1).                         (12)
```

Together with `J_LLLL=(1)`, equations (10)--(12) prove the theorem.
Every nonzero pure mixed-ruling solution lies on `B=0`, hence has at
least one block annihilator line.

For transparency, reduced bases for the two nonempty affine closures
are:

```text
J_LLLR:
s0*s1+s0*s2+s1*s2+s3^2,
s0*s2^2+s1*s2^2-s0*s3^2-s1*s3^2,
s1^2*s2^2-s1^2*s3^2-s2^2*s3^2+s3^4,
s0*s3^4+s1*s3^4+s2*s3^4-s0-s1-s2,
s1^2*s3^4+s1*s2*s3^4+s2^2*s3^4-s3^6
  -s1^2-s1*s2-s2^2+s3^2,
s2^3*s3^4-s2*s3^6-s2^3+s2*s3^2;

J_LLRR:
s0*s2+s1*s2+s0*s3+s1*s3,
s0*s1+s2^2+2*s2*s3+s3^2,
s0^2+s1^2-2*s2^2-3*s2*s3-2*s3^2,
s2^2*s3+s2*s3^2,
s1^2*s2-s2^3+s1^2*s3-3*s2^2*s3
  -3*s2*s3^2-s3^3,
s1^3-s1*s2^2-s1*s2*s3-s1*s3^2.                   (13)
```

For example, the affine chart `T_0001 != 0` of `LLRR` contains the
two rational curves

```text
(s0,s1,s2,s3)=(t,0,-t,t),
(s0,s1,s2,s3)=(0,t,-t,t),                         (14)
```

and both have a block line at parameter zero.  This is the geometric
reason the initial mixed-ruling calculation looked promising but did
not produce another component.

## Consequence for component classification

For a pure restriction, the opposite-pair inequality

```text
dim(U_i U_j)+dim(U_k U_l) <= 7
```

forces an exceptional pair in every perfect matching of `K_4`.
The minimal exceptional-pair graphs are a star and a triangle.  A
rank-three exceptional pair produces a diagonal quadric through its
two annihilator lines.  The theorem above closes the semisimple
common-smooth-quadric subcase: a component-sized family cannot remain
away from the block-line base locus.

The next conceptual classification target is therefore finite:

1. prove that the generic point of every pure component is governed
   by the star/triangle exceptional-pair geometry;
2. classify its `2+2` and `1+3` block-line centers;
3. identify every resulting block branch, including the mixed
   zero-product orientation that has since produced a sixth certified
   component orbit.

This is a structural route, not a search through plane coefficients.
