# A five-edge odd-cycle obstruction in 74 additional `C10` orbits

## Status

This is an exact finite theorem inside the `P_5` exact-three-coordinate,
exact-three-partial `C10` boundary.  It excludes 74 support-semantic
survivor orbits not covered by the binary-fork or triangle rules.
Together the three sparse rules exclude 1,515 of the 11,751
independently audited `C10` orbits.

It does **not** exclude the remaining 10,236 `C10` orbits, close the
other `P_5` branches, or prove the global Krenn--Gu conjecture.

## The local identity

Let `P,E0,...,E4` be six forbidden mixed-colour coefficient
polynomials.  Suppose that, after the standard spanning-tree gauge,

```text
P  = 1 + m*A0,
E0 = A0 + A1,
E1 = A1 + A2,
E2 = A2 + A3,
E3 = A3 + A4,
E4 = A4 + A0,
```

where `m` is a squarefree monomial; `m=1` is allowed.  Alternating
around the odd cycle gives

```text
E0 - E1 + E2 - E3 + E4 = 2*A0,
2*P - m*(E0-E1+E2-E3+E4) = 2.
```

All six mixed coefficients would have to vanish in a monochromatic
restriction, giving `0=2`.  Thus no common affine zero exists over
characteristic zero.

This argument uses neither the Laurent saturation equation nor a
nonzero assumption on a pure-colour amplitude.

## Structural meaning

The fork, triangle, and five-cycle rules are one family: odd cycles of
pair-sum coefficients force twice a latent sparse polynomial to vanish.
A constant-containing coefficient then turns that into `0=2`.

The next exact test is the same search on simple seven-cycles, followed
by general sparse monomial-linear certificates not generated by
