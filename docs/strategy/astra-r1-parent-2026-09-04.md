## Complete finite supply and exact source condition

Every pure coefficient is nonzero, so choose one nonzero perfect matching
per colour. The three chosen matchings are edge-disjoint because a physical
matrix unit cannot have two different monochromatic labels. Their union
is a spanning cubic graph with a specified proper three-edge-colouring.
It need not be connected. This is a selected scaffold, not the essential
physical skeleton: the other sixteen unit blocks remain present.

A finite cover may fix the colour-0 matching by vertex permutation,
enumerate every ordered edge-disjoint pair of additional matchings, and
quotient only by explicitly proved symmetries. Any orbit compression
must be independently checked. No unproved graph catalogue is assumed.

There are 105 perfect matchings. A fixed matrix-unit labelling maps each
matching to one colour word and one nonzero scalar monomial. Consequently
a forbidden word cannot occur exactly once. This is only a NECESSARY
support condition: repeated monomials may fail to cancel, and pure sums
may cancel too. A SAT label table is not a GHZ counterexample. A negative
support result needs an exhaustive cover and exact independently checked
certificate before it can become a mathematical proof leaf.

The full source, if a support survives, consists of the exact Laurent sums
for every matching-word fibre and nonzero pure coefficients. Do not fix
selected matching weights to one while keeping pure amplitudes one unless
that gauge is proved legal. Any apparent exact weighted GHZ solution
triggers dedicated independent validation.
