**Geometric reading.**  For a 3-vs-3 cut with the outer side pinned and
independent, inner vertex `t` has three rows `r_{t,alpha} in C^3`.  Two
colours per inner vertex give `Y_t in C^{3x2}` and a column plane `V_t`.
The eight cube words are the restriction of the trilinear form `perm` to
`V_0 x V_1 x V_2`, a `2 x 2 x 2` tensor `A`.  Write `l_t` for the Plücker
(normal) vector of `V_t`.

## 1. Step 1: the hyperdeterminant  [PROVED]

Theorem 1 of the theorem document:

- `Det(A) = F(l_0, l_1, l_2)`.  This is a 21-term, tridegree `(2, 2, 2)`,
  symmetric, **absolutely irreducible** polynomial.  The factorization
  through the Plücker coordinates is forced (it is an `SL_2^3`-invariant);
  there is no further factorization.
- `F` is a quadratic form in the six **permutation monomials**
  `P_s = l_0[s0] l_1[s1] l_2[s2]` alone:
  `F = det(L)^2 - 8 (e2(P_even) + e2(P_odd))`, where `L` has rows `l_t`.
- The three "slice determinants" are the binary quadratic covariants
  `q_t`.  On `V_0`,
  `q_0(v) = det(Y_1^T B_v Y_2) = l_1^T adj(B_v) l_2 = (l_1.v)(l_2.v) - 2 sum_u l_1[u] l_2[u] v_u^2`,
  with `adj(B_v) = v v^T - 2 diag(v)^2`.  `F` is 4 times the bordered
  discriminant of `q_0` on `l_0^perp`; by symmetry the same holds for
  `q_1` and `q_2`.  These are the classical relations: all three
  discriminants equal `Det`.
- Values: `F(e_1, e_2, e_3) = 1`, so three distinct coordinate planes give
  a nondegenerate cube.  `F(l, l, l) = -48 (l_1 l_2 l_3)^2`.
  `F(l, l, m) = -8 (l_1 l_2 l_3) perm(l, m, m)`.

**Verdict on Step 1.**  The hyperdeterminant is the wrong invariant for
rigidity.  `Det = 0` is a hypersurface (degenerate cube).  Identical
vanishing is a *linear* condition on `l_0 (x) l_1 (x) l_2`: the tensor
must lie in a fixed 5-dimensional space `S` (Step 2).  `F` lies in the ideal generated by those
linear conditions, so it adds nothing.  The useful output of Step 1 is the
mechanism behind that linear condition, the polarization identity.

## 2. Step 2: basis-free rigidity and why the degree is three  [PROVED]

Theorem 2 and Corollaries 3–4 of the theorem document:

- **Polarization identity** (over `Z`).  For every row triple `r`,
  `sum_{ijk} (-1)^{i+j+k} Y_0[r0,1-i] Y_1[r1,1-j] Y_2[r2,1-k] A_ijk = -perm(l_0 x e_r0, l_1 x e_r1, l_2 x e_r2)`.
  Contracting each cube slot with the symplectic dual of a row of `Y_t`
  turns the cube into the permanent evaluated at the vectors
  `l_t x e_{r_t} in V_t`.
- **The degree-three Plücker part of the ideal** is
  `J_3 = Ann(S)`, of dimension 22, where
  `S = span{e_a^{(x)3}, eps_+, eps_-}`.  It holds over every field of
  characteristic not two, and over `Q` it is the whole trilinear-Plücker
  part of the ideal (exact ranks).  `J_3` contains the 18 two-equal
  monomials (Proposition 4) **and** the same-parity differences
  `P_s - P_s'`, which are new.
- **Basis-free statement (Corollary 3).**  Assume characteristic not two.
  If the cube vanishes, then `l_0 (x) l_1 (x) l_2 in S`.  Hence some
  `l_t = 0`, or `l_0, l_1, l_2` lie on one coordinate axis.  For planes:
  **`perm` vanishes on `V_0 x V_1 x V_2` iff the three Plücker vectors are
  multiples of one `e_u`, i.e. `V_0 = V_1 = V_2 = H_u`.**  The proof uses
  only the degree-three certificates and the classification of rank-one
  tensors in `S`.
- **Why 3.**  Every Plücker polynomial in the ideal has degree at least 2
  in each `Y_t` (an `SL_2`-invariant), so its multipliers have degree at
  least 3.  Rigidity is already a linear condition on one Plücker
  coordinate per inner vertex.  So 3 is both necessary and sufficient.
  - Two-equal monomials are in the ideal at degree 3.
  - A permutation monomial is not in the ideal, but its square is
    (Nullstellensatz exponent 2, multiplier degree 9).
  - The all-equal monomials are not in the radical (`H_a^3` is isotropic).

  This explains the measured "minimal degree exactly 3" on the 51-entry
  survivor's slice (multilinear-shape note, Section 2), with one caveat:
  that measurement used physical-entry targets on a support, where
  invariance does not apply verbatim.

## 3. Step 3: the valid clause family PR

### 3.1 The `T`-expansion  [PROVED]

Let `A` be even, `T = {t_0, t_1, t_2} subset A`, `R = A - T`, `c` a word on
`R`, and `w` the word with `alpha_k` at `t_k` and `c` on `R`.  Split the
perfect matchings of `A` by how `T` is matched.  Either no edge lies inside
`T` (an injection `pi : T -> R`), or exactly one does (`|T| = 3`).  This
gives the exact identity, for every `W`:

```text
T_A(w) = sum_{pi: T -> R injective} prod_k W_{t_k pi(t_k)}[alpha_k, c_{pi t_k}] T_{R - pi(T)}(c)
       + sum_{i<j, k = third} W_{t_i t_j}[alpha_i, alpha_j] sum_{u in R} W_{t_k u}[alpha_k, c_u] T_{R-u}(c).
```

### 3.2 Rule PR and its soundness  [PROVED]

Fix `A` (`|A| >= 6`), `T`, `R`, `c`, a 3-set `C subset R`, and two distinct
colours `alpha_t != alpha'_t` at each inner vertex.  These give 8 cube
words `w_ijk`.  Put `r_{t,alpha} = (W_tu[alpha, c_u])_{u in C}` and
`Y_t = [r_{t,alpha_t}, r_{t,alpha'_t}]`.  The premises are conjunctions of
model literals (`g` = entry nonzero, `m[B, v]` = `T_B(v) != 0`):

1. **(h)** `m[R - C, c]`: the complementary coefficient of the columns is
   nonzero.  It is `TRUE` if `R = C`, and a `g` literal if `|R - C| = 2`.
2. **(outside)** For every cube word, every summand of the `T`-expansion
   other than the injections onto `C` has a factor that the model makes
   zero.  The summands are:
   - for an injection with image `S != C`: `not m[R - S, c]`, or one
     entry `not g`;
   - for an inner-edge term: `not g` on the inner entry, or, for each
     `u in R`, `not g[t_k u]` or `not m[R - u, c]`.

   These are the hypotheses of Theorem 2 of the bilinear-grid document,
   including the killed `a`–`b` term, transposed to an inner triple.
3. **(minors)** For each `t`, a column index `a_t` such that the minor
   `l_t[a_t] = y_b y'_d - y_d y'_b` of `Y_t` (on the other two columns
   `b, d`) is a single nonzero monomial: both factors of one product are
   supported, and the other product has an unsupported factor.  The
   `a_t` are **not all equal**.

**Conclusion:** `m[A, w_ijk]` for some cube word.  The clause is
`not(premises) OR (m[A, w_000] OR ... OR m[A, w_111])`.

*Soundness.*  Let `W` be any complex configuration (not necessarily a
witness) at any even order, and let `A` be any level.  Assume premises
1–3 hold for the actual zero pattern, and suppose all eight `T_A(w_ijk)`
vanish.

- By 3.1 and premise 2, `T_A(w_ijk) = h * perm(Y_0[:,i], Y_1[:,j], Y_2[:,k])`,
  with `h = T_{R-C}(c) != 0` by premise 1.
- So the cube tensor of `(Y_0, Y_1, Y_2)` vanishes.
- By premise 3, `l_t[a_t] != 0` for every `t`; in particular every
  `l_t != 0`.
- By Corollary 3 (over `C`), there is a `u` with every `l_t` a multiple of
  `e_u`.  Then `l_t[a_t] != 0` forces `a_t = u` for all `t`, which
  contradicts premise 3.

The GHZ target is not used.  A witness enters only through `(G)` at
`A = V`, where the mixed cube words are forced to `m = false`.  ∎

**Mode `deg3`** keeps only the instances with an explicit degree-three
certificate.  These are the instances where `(a_t)` is two-equal, or a
permutation `s` such that some same-parity `s' != s` has an identically zero
minor (neither product supported).  The certificate `P_s - P_s'` then
specializes to the single monomial `P_s` (Corollary 4 (iii)).  The default
mode `value` uses Corollary 3, which is the radical statement.

**Relation to earlier rules.**  At `n = 6`, `A = V`, premise 2 says that
the outer side is independent at `c` (or that the inner entries vanish),
and premise 1 is vacuous.  So PR at `n = 6` is Corollary 5 with the
review's repaired support condition, extended by the binomial
certificates.  At `n >= 8`, the `T_{R-S}` are order-`(|A| - 6)` and the
`T_{R-u}` order-`(|A| - 4)` recursive coefficients, so PR is a clause
family at every level.  Theorem 2 of the bilinear-grid document (two free
vertices, a full third row) is a different specialization and is not
encoded.

### 3.3 Implementation

Flags (script `claims/finite/n08/explore_general_block_recursive_support_model.py`):

- `--plane-rigidity`: PR at every level `|A| >= 6`;
- `--plane-top-only`: only `A = V`;
- `--plane-mode value|deg3`;
- `--pattern-file`: fix every `g` from a `physical-support-v1` fixture;
- `--max-rounds`.

The clauses are added lazily, in the same CEGAR loop as `--holonomy`.

- **Encoder.**  `GSM.plane_instances` builds, for each violated instance, a
  clause from the model's own false literals (one zero witness per
  summand, three `g`'s and one negative `g` per minor).  Every such clause
  is an instance of PR.
- **Checker.**  `check_plane` re-derives the violated instances from the
  decoded supports.  It evaluates the summands directly, without SAT
  variables.  A SAT model is accepted only if `check_model`,
  `check_holonomy` (when enabled) and `check_plane` all pass.  The checker
  is a separate implementation of the same rule, not an independent audit.
- **Symmetry breaking.**  It stays sound: PR is invariant under vertex
  relabelling and colour permutation.

**Smoke tests [SMOKE]** (scratch script, not committed).
Random sparse integer `W` (entries `+-1` or `+-1, +-2`, densities
0.25–0.5); every `T_A(w)` was computed exactly and its zero pattern used as
`m`.

- `n = 6`, 150 configurations: PR premises held (with any cube values) in
  50,299 instances, with 0 violations.  In 5,834 direct tests, whenever
  premises 1–2 held, `T_A(w) = h * perm` exactly, with 0 failures.
- `n = 8`, 3 configurations: 35,403 instances, 0 violations; 3,108 direct
  expansion tests at levels 6 and 8, with 0 failures.

## 4. Step 4: gluing at `n = 8`

### 4.1 What PR needs outside the cut at the top level

Take `n = 8` and `A = V`, so `|R| = 5` and `R - C = {x, y}`.  PR fires on
a mixed cube only if all of the following hold.

- **(h)** `g[x y, c_x c_y]`: the two non-column outside vertices are joined
  at `c`.
- **(outside, injections)** For each of the other nine 3-sets `S subset R`
  and every cube word, the complementary outside pair `R - S` is not
  joined at `c`, or every injection `T -> S` has a dead entry.
- **(outside, inner edge)** Each inner edge that is live at the cube
  colours is killed.  Either `t_k u` is dead, or the four-vertex coefficient
  `T_{R-u}(c)` vanishes; the latter is an `m` literal that may vanish by
  cancellation.
- **(minors)** At each inner vertex, a single-monomial minor; and the
  minors are not all on one axis.

At `n = 6` the first three conditions reduce to "the outer triangle is
independent at `c`".  At `n = 8` they ask that the outside 5-set, seen
through the attachments of `T`, carry exactly one live complementary pair.

### 4.3 The occurrence statement

**OCC-PR(n)** (the GL-node obligation for this rule).  Every witness of
order `n` admits an inner triple `T`, a colouring `c` of `R = V - T`,
columns `C subset R` and colour pairs `alpha_t != alpha'_t` such that:

- (i) `T_{R-C}(c) != 0`;
- (ii) for each of the eight words, every summand of the `T`-expansion
  other than the injections onto `C` vanishes;
- (iii) the three column planes `V_t` are two-dimensional and not all
  equal to one coordinate plane;
- (iv) the eight cube words are mixed.

By the value form of Section 3.2 (Corollary 3 directly), OCC-PR(n) implies
that no witness of order `n` exists.  So OCC-PR(n) is exactly as strong as
the exclusion it would give.  It is the precise statement a gluing theorem
would have to supply; it is not a lemma toward one.  Its support form
replaces (i)–(iii) by premises 1–3 of PR.

**Evidence.**  The `n = 8` SAT model above satisfies `(L)(F')(G)` and the
killers, but the support form of OCC-PR fails at every top-level cut.
This is evidence **against** deriving OCC-PR(8) from the support-level
axioms with killers.  It is **not** evidence about actual witnesses: the
model is a relaxation, and its supports carry no weights.  The outcome with
H2/H3 is in Section 3.4.

**Sharper next lemma (not attempted).**  Condition (ii) is where gluing
fails: dense outside graphs leave several complementary 3-sets live.  If
only the inner-edge terms are killed, the slice is the **weighted
multi-column permanent**

```text
Phi_h(x, y, z) = sum_{S subset R, |S| = 3} h_S perm(x|_S, y|_S, z|_S),    h_S = T_{R-S}(c),
```

a trilinear form on `C^{|R|}`.  The load-bearing question is plane rigidity
for `Phi_h` on three 2-planes in `C^{|R|}`: for which coefficient patterns
`h` does `Phi_h` vanish identically on `V_0 x V_1 x V_2`?  The polarization
identity applies verbatim, with `l_t` replaced by the Plücker vector of
`V_t` in `Lambda^2 C^{|R|}`.  So the degree-three part is again a linear
condition on `l_0 (x) l_1 (x) l_2`, and the question is which rank-one
tensors lie in its kernel.
