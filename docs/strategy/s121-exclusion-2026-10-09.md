**The single most load-bearing fact:** S121 violates the already-proved rule
`H3` at the top level with purely structural premises.  At hub 0 the four
mixed words `T(a,0,0,2,b,1,0,1)`, `a in {0,1}`, `b in {1,2}`, have exactly the
two Laplace terms of partners 2 and 3 at three corners.  The fourth corner
has one extra term, partner 5.  The resulting four-word identity forces a
product of six support entries to vanish.  S121 was produced by a run without
`--holonomy`.  No new mechanism is needed.

## 3. The exclusion  [EXACT]

### 3.1 The four words

Write `E_ab = T(w_ab)` with `w_ab = (a, 0, 0, 2, b, 1, 0, 1)`: colour `a` at
vertex 0, colour `b` at vertex 4, and the fixed colours `0, 0, 2, 1, 0, 1` at
vertices `1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7`.  All four words are mixed.  Expand at the hub
`p = 0`.  On S121 (every other entry zero) the live terms are:

| corner `(a, b)` | live matchings | partners of 0 with a nonzero term |
|---|---|---|
| (0, 1) | 3 | 2, 3 |
| (0, 2) | 3 | 2, 3 |
| (1, 1) | 3 | 2, 3 |
| (1, 2) | 4 | 2, 3, 5 |

The terms are:

```text
t2(a,b) = W02[a,0] * P_b,   P_b = W13[02] W45[b1] W67[01]                       (one monomial)
t3(a,b) = W03[a,2] * Q_b,   Q_b = W12[00] W45[b1] W67[01] + W14[0b] W27[01] W56[10]
t5      = W05[11]  * Z,     Z   = W13[02] W24[02] W67[01]                       (one monomial)
```

The partner-5 term is live only at `(1, 2)`.  At `a = 0` its entry
`W05[0,1]` lies outside S121, because block `05` is lower triangular.  At
`(1, 1)` its complementary coefficient `T_{1,2,3,4,6,7}(0,0,2,1,0,1)` has no
supported matching.

### 3.2 The identity

Each term factors as a function of `a` (the hub entry) times a function of
`b` (the complementary six-vertex coefficient, which contains vertex 4).
Hence

```text
t_k(0,1) t_k(1,2) = t_k(0,2) t_k(1,1)        for k = 2, 3.
```

Substituting `t2 = E - t3` at the three two-term corners gives the
polynomial identity, which the verifier checks exactly on S121 (part B):

```text
t2(0,1) * t5 = t2(0,1) E12 - E02 E11 + E02 t3(1,1) + t3(0,2) E11 - E01 t3(1,2).
```

The left side is the monomial

```text
W02[00] W05[11] W13[02]^2 W24[02] W45[11] W67[01]^2.
```

### 3.3 Statement

**Proposition.**  Over every field, no configuration supported within S121
with the six entries `W02[00], W05[11], W13[02], W24[02], W45[11], W67[01]`
nonzero has matching tensor `GHZ(8,3)` up to the torus.  In particular S121
is not fibre-realizable with exact support.

*Proof.*  At a witness all four words are mixed, so the right side of the
identity is 0.  The left side is a product of nonzero entries.  ∎

The proof uses no division, no genericity, and no value of a constant word.

**Reading.**  This is a Shape II instance of `H3` (Lemma B′ of
[`holonomy-support-model-2026-10-08.md`](holonomy-support-model-2026-10-08.md)):

- level `A = V` and hub `p = 0`;
- partners `k1 = 2` and `k2 = 3`;
- varied vertices `u = 0` and `v = 4`;
- three corners with live set exactly `{2, 3}`, and the fourth with the
  single extra term of partner 5.

Equivalently, it is the `(k, r, s) = (2, 1, 1)` case of the adjugate
identity (2) in
[`multi-partner-grid-transport-2026-10-09.md`](multi-partner-grid-transport-2026-10-09.md),
with `p_I = W02[0,0]`, `q_{I^c} = P_1` and `sigma = t5`.  The binomial `Q_b`
never needs to be nonzero: only the `k = 2` factors at the opposite corner
enter the conclusion.

**Scope.**  This is a finite statement about one Boolean pattern.  Verifier
part C reduces the zero hypotheses on `K_8`.  The identity still holds when
only 24 of the 26 outside entries occurring in the four unrestricted word
polynomials are set to zero.  The greedy order is arbitrary, so this is not
claimed to be minimal.

### 3.4 Abundance  [STRUCTURAL; verifier part D repeats the count]

A top-level `H3` square is called *structural* when every other Laplace term
at the hub is identically zero on S121.  Such a term has an entry outside
S121, or a complementary coefficient with no supported matching.

- Structural squares give 5,275 violated top-level `H3` instances.  This
  equals the count of holonomy violations in round 1 of the fixed-pattern
  SAT run.
- In 2,285 of them both conclusion factors are single monomials: the
  opposite-corner coefficient `t_k` and the extra term.  Each gives an
  identity of the shape of 3.2 and so excludes S121 by itself.
- These 2,285 squares fall into seven `(hub; partners)` classes:
  `(0; 2,3)`, `(2; 0,1)`, `(3; 0,1)`, `(4; 1,5)`, `(5; 4,6)`, `(6; 5,7)` and
  `(7; 2,6)`.
- The SAT run's first reported violation, at hub 6 with partners `{5, 7}`, is
  in the largest class (1,032 squares).

The obstruction is therefore abundant in S121, not isolated.

## 4. The other mechanisms

- **Killers and anchors** [EXACT, part A].  Every `(v, c)` has a killer block
  and a diagonal anchor.  Examples: `(2, 0)` is anchored by `W23[0,0]`, and
  `(7, 2)` by `W37[2,2]`.
- **PR** [SAT-RUN].  With `g` fixed to S121, killers, anchors and PR at
  every level, the model is SAT in round 1 with checker PASS.  So some
  consistent `m`-assignment has no PR premise instance.
- **`H2`** [SAT-RUN].  With `H2` alone at every level, and with `H2` at the
  top level only, the model is SAT in round 1 with checker PASS.
- **`H3`** [SAT-RUN].  `H3` at the top level alone, with killers and
  anchors, is UNSAT after 2 rounds (7.7 s).  The full run, with killers,
  anchors, `H2` and `H3` at every level and PR, is UNSAT after 2 rounds
  (39.5 s).
- **`M`** [STRUCTURAL].  The test covers every hub `p`, every colour set `A`
  with `|A| = 2` or `3`, and every admissible colouring.  A coordinate whose
  complementary coefficient is a single monomial (certainly live) must be
  covered, in every row of its `R_A` support, by the other possibly-live
  coordinates.  That is necessary for it to lie in their span.  There are
  69,912 instances and no violation.  This test is weaker than full `M`;
  `M` with `|A| = 1` is the killer, which passes.
- **Two-term closure** [STRUCTURAL].  No mixed word of S121 has fewer than
  three live matchings: the minimum is 3, attained by 537 words.  The
  constant words have 12, 9 and 15.  So:
  - the premises of Theorems A and B (two-matching corners or two-term words)
    never occur;
  - S121 is not a binomial support (Theorem C);
  - by Theorem D, full-word BL closure is inert on S121.

  The `H3` instance of Section 3 is invisible to all four theorems.  Its
  two-term structure appears only after the Laplace expansion at the hub,
  which is the recursive variant that Theorem D does not cover.
- **Three-column bilinear grid** [not separately searched].  Its plane
  rigidity consequence is what the PR clause encodes, and PR is silent.  No
  search for value-level Theorem 2 instances was made, because `H3` already
  decides the pattern.

## 5. Why S121 survived, and the pattern of today's survivors  [OBSERVATION]

S121 is the model of the run `s156tri-n8-kapr`, which used killers, anchors
and PR but **not** `--holonomy`.  The anchor note's top-level holonomy run on
the general `n = 8` model (`s156tri-n8-kahtoppr`) timed out, so S121 was
never tested against `H3`.  The fixed-pattern test is cheap: 8 s at the top
level.

All three `n = 8` support-model survivors recorded on 2026-10-09 were
excluded fibre-exactly by an already-proved all-order rule that the run
producing them had not switched on:

| pattern | run that produced it | rule it violates | identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| S128 | killers + PR | `H3` | 4 words |
| S156 | killers + top-level holonomy + PR | diagonal anchor | 16 words |
| S121 | killers + anchors + PR | `H3` | 4 words |

So none of them is evidence that the encoded rule set has a gap.  The
informative object is a model of the full rule set: killers, anchors, `H2`
and `H3` at every level, and PR.  That is what the 6-hour run
`n8-kahpr-full` of another worker is computing; this note did not duplicate
it.

Part 2 of the brief (a direct identity search when nothing fires) was not
needed.  No Laurent-XL, Gröbner or generalized-anchor contraction was run.
