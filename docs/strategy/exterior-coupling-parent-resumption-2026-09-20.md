## Full physical source extension: CSFULL

The first analytic attempts found concrete limits of the S1/S2 route:
higher permanental responses need not lie in the span of the actual
state matrices, and the canonical cubic permanent product is not Jordan.
These controls fail full binary targets and do not refute FB or exterior
coupling. A separate exact support argument ruled out the required cycle
faces for the fixed PSBS tensor support, without ruling out other supports.

We therefore test the stronger antecedent actually supplied by a global
witness. Define **CSFULL** to assert that no common-star array of the
declared kind, for any k>=2 over C, satisfies the full physical GHZ target.
It suffices to exclude its AB-word subsystem: arbitrary center colors x_u
and one uniform color y_u on each component's three leaves. This subsystem
is not assumed in FB or in CSQ4, whose owning definition contains only
component-constant words and physical U4. The new route bypasses those
intermediate parents; it does not silently prove them.

The named downstream consumer is exclusion of the entire common-star
construction branch for a hypothetical global witness. Its upstream supply
is direct restriction of the full physical target to AB words. There is
still no theorem putting arbitrary protected fillings or arbitrary global
witnesses in this support class.

The candidate source identity is

    F(x,y) = sum_(S even) haf A_x[S] haf B_y[S]
                         product_(u outside S) delta(x_u,y_u).

Uniform leaf triples force only AB components; every noncrossing component
contributes its protected identity. Local orthogonal center/leaf
contractions kill every proper S. For odd k there is no surviving term.
For even k the only survivor, S=V, is an outer product in the center and
leaf variables. A two-by-two family of orthogonal contractions has target
matrix [[2,1],[1,1+2^(k-2)]], with nonzero determinant 1+2^(k-1).

This is a candidate all-order branch exclusion. Dedicated adversarial
review has been dispatched to check the physical factorization, all four
cross-orthogonality conditions, the k=1 exception, complex-bilinear
conventions and the CSFULL/CSQ4 distinction. The proof combines the exact
two-hafnian physical source with the complete off-diagonal center/leaf
target, rather than replacing source terms by independent cofactor data.
Acceptance would add a direct conditional branch-closure edge and make
FB unnecessary for that full-source consumer; it would not resolve the
global conjecture or the even-k CSQ4 subsystem.
