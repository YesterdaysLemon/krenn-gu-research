# Full-source parent checkpoint for external review

The global Krenn--Gu conjecture is **UNRESOLVED**. This checkpoint concerns
protected unit K4 components with arbitrary hollow intercomponent matrices
over C. No reduction from arbitrary witnesses to this family is available.

## Short review brief

We attacked arbitrary-order exclusion through two parent implications:
source-preserving component elimination and a support-only paired-minority
condition P1. Neither parent is proved. The useful results are exact source
identities and specific failures of proposed proof mechanisms.

Removing one K4 yields a quartic boundary `K=3+K_2+K_4` coupled to the same
exterior matching functional. Legal quadratic elimination is exactly a
relative-kernel equation. Canonical updates and fibrewise gluing fail on
exact nonwitness controls. Covariant Ward transport is valid, but bare
pointwise Ward-module membership already contains the desired elimination
equation; it does not supply it.

For one complete minority pair, the coefficient is a sum of actual
two-path permanent minors times actual closure seams. With two complete
pairs, the source subtracts explicit repeated-resource hafnians. A literal
twelve-vertex support covers all mixed macro words while all three port
graphs are DAGs, refuting the proposed macro-to-independent-cycle exchange.
It still has 56 uniquely supported one-pair words, so it is not a P1 control
and cannot carry full-source weights.

Under the independent-minority equations, every one-pair matching lies on
one rooted alternating circuit. Same-word exchange components closed under
the protected matching retain every minority; the other components cannot
be isolated against the protected background. An exact Laurent control
also cancels a source row by changing only its closure square, leaving the
path untouched. Thus the remaining argument needs genuine cross-word/color
consistency with shared physical factors and single-use resources.

## Exact objects to review

PSCE's requirement to retain split-leaf columns and a common exterior core.
The P1 attempt combines both PSMR mechanisms: independent-minority cycle
exclusion and the injective exterior-resource expansion. The accepted
two-K4 exclusion is the proposed induction endpoint, not an automatically
available local restriction at larger order.

The proof-distance delta is the elimination of specified proof mechanisms
by exact controls and a new common path/resource description. No exhaustive
cover of protected fillings has closed. The full-source parents are still
open, and neither has acquired an arbitrary-witness supply theorem.

Each owner lists portable exact replay commands. The scripts corroborate
finite identities and controls; the arbitrary-order claims rest on the
written proofs. Native SAT timeouts and sampled DAG completions did not
resolve P1 and are not proof evidence in this package.

The most useful adversarial review would either find a gap in these exact
identities or produce a new implication at one of two precise boundaries:

1. Construct a legal correction from full-source equations by a uniform
   polynomial identity or a rational cover with every denominator-zero
   stratum handled, without replacing the task by pointwise membership.
2. Force a unique one-pair source word, or derive incompatible weighted
   closure equations, from all macro targets and all actual shared-word
   factors. A path-length argument must survive stationary square repairs;
   a product argument must retain repeated-resource corrections.

This is a published-results checkpoint for continued research, not a claim
that the conjecture, SFULL, P1, or the elimination parent has been resolved.
