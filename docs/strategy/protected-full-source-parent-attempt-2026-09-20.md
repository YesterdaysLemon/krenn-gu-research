# Protected full-source parent: extension attempts after PSCF

## Parent and supply

The completed common-star proof PSCF excludes the full physical target
over C at every k>=2. The next parent **SFULL** asks for the same exclusion
for arbitrary hollow intercomponent fillings of k protected unit K4s.
Its supply is a hypothetical full witness already in that scaffold; its
downstream consumer is exclusion of the entire protected construction.
There is no reduction from all witnesses to this scaffold. Global
Krenn--Gu remains **UNRESOLVED**.

This continuation began in an isolated checkout of origin/main after the
owner explicitly resumed research. The accepted PSCF checkpoint is PR350,
merge 4864803a822ca9febb6fceecc51b730594b92ed1. The work here attacks the
parent implication, rather than another sibling in the closed common-star
family.

## Three attempted syntheses and outcomes

1. **Full-source projection.** Combine PSCF's local orthogonality with the
   exact arbitrary-filling physical matching expansion. Grouping the
   entirely internal components still cancels them, but the source has
   additional BB, center-leaf, and degree-four networks. The two committed
   n=8 Laurent controls satisfy every AB word, yet these networks produce
   the rank-two target. Both have exact nonzero split-leaf columns.
   Therefore AB-only extension is refuted, and full nonuniform-leaf
   equations are essential.
2. **Factorizing cut.** Combine protected color matchings and coordinate
   killers with a two-probe cut-null argument. A target-visible cut would
   indeed exclude a full source. However, if every free block is
   invertible, protected edges force a visible cut to keep each K4 whole,
   and the two varying local probe planes then contradict invertibility.
   There are explicit rational controls at every k>=2, and a nonempty
   open of the already-owned generic structural scaffold for k>=5, with
   no such cut. These arrays have nonzero mixed coefficients. Structural
   supply is refuted; full-source separator supply remains open.
3. **Support descent.** Try a nonnegative color-diagonal scaling that
   preserves each protected edge and deletes a live crossing entry.
   Every component-constant word has total weight zero. Hence every
   individual factor of every supported matching for that word must have
   exponent zero. The original crossing core survives every finite
   sequence of these degenerations. Mixed component targets require this
   core to be nonempty to cancel the protected contribution one. Both
   Laurent controls have all 12 crossing entries frozen. Thus homogeneity
   alone cannot drive this induction; it must first establish a new
   inconsistency using the full split-component equations.

## What would close the remaining parent

For every k>=2 and arbitrary hollow protected filling satisfying every AB
target, prove that at least one nonuniform leaf column of the full source
is nonzero. The n=8 controls exhibit the desired conclusion through unique
matching monomials, but an induced local detector cannot be imported into
a larger array: exterior components can add and cancel matching terms in
that same physical column.

Equivalently, the missing step is a shared-factor inconsistency between
the AB rank correction and all full split-leaf zero columns. Independent
cofactor assignments, termwise BB annihilation, structural cut supply, and
protected diagonal homogeneity do not provide it. Increasing a finite
search bound does not establish its arbitrary-order quantifier.

The scientific delta is a closed full common-star branch (PSCF), followed
by two exact extension-route no-go results and one frozen-core obstruction
(PSCE). SFULL remains open, as do arbitrary-witness-to-scaffold supply and
the global conjecture. This is the actual stopping obstruction of this
attempt, not a claim that no other proof approach exists.
