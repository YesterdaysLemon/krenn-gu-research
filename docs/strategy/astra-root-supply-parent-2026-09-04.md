# Root-pair supply parent attempt

Status: active research attempt, not a proved implication. Global status
UNRESOLVED. Base: origin/main 1c78ed0464b89f7fcf50a8b00a07534336b07c09.

## Exact parent and consumer

Over C, let W be a ternary block graph on eight vertices whose full matching
tensor equals Delta_(8,3), and whose maximum fully supported pairwise-zero
root set has cardinality two. Prove that there is a pair i,j admitting a
torus zero such that, for every other u, both physical blocks W_iu and W_ju
are supported on the same outside coordinate column c(u). Individual zero
legs are allowed. The pair may be changed during the proof.

The named downstream consumer is N8R2C, the complete common-coordinate
exclusion in claims/finite/n08/TWO_ROOT_COMMON_COORDINATE_COMPLETION_THEOREM.md.
It has no root-block rank assumption. Proving this supply implication, or
directly excluding its residual incidence types, would exclude the entire
n=8 maximum-r=2 branch. It would not resolve r=1, r=3, or arbitrary order.

## Upstream supply and synthesis

Use maximum-root saturation on every torus fibre, together with the FULL
matrix-valued source identity at any candidate root pair:

    H_B Q + sum_(u<v) H_(B-{u,v}) (P_u R_v^T + P_v R_u^T)
      = diag(product_u z_u[0], product_u z_u[1], product_u z_u[2]).

P_u and R_u are the physical root incidences. Retain the H_B Q term and
all independent outside variables. The completed common-coordinate proof
combines physical cofactor constraints and changes of root pair; the next
attempt must synthesize those mechanisms with the full-source kernel
annihilation constraints to supply the incidence premise itself.

## Sharp controls and success criteria

Maximum-root geometry alone permits rank-two common coordinate planes,
noncoordinate planes, and a single-column leg opposite an unrestricted
leg. Explicit off-source rank-three-root controls in the earlier lab
scratch demonstrate this. Neither blocker quotas nor pure amplitudes may
replace the mixed source. A smaller simultaneous kernel does not provide
the two independent inactive variables used in the completed theorem.

Success is a complete implication proof, a direct exhaustive exclusion,
or an exact obstruction to a proposed intermediate mechanism that leaves
one sharper load-bearing lemma. An actual GHZ witness candidate triggers
dedicated independent validation immediately. An off-source example only
refutes a route that omitted the source hypothesis.

Research workers attack pair exchange, physical kernel/cofactor supply,
and adversarial incidence controls. The coordinator owns integration.
No additional root-rank refinement within N8R2C is needed.

## Synthesis outcome and proof-topology delta

The [joint-kernel boundary](../../claims/finite/n08/TWO_ROOT_JOINT_KERNEL_BOUNDARY_THEOREM.md)
addresses the incidence premise itself. If every outsider at an invertible
root pair has a joint-kernel vector with exactly one zero coordinate, the
full source forces exactly two ports per missing colour. Opening each
pair and taking a rank-one determinant update forces its full physical
channel to factor through that coordinate. This supplies a genuine common
column at one endpoint of each pair. The cofactor proof then works with
one binary anchor and one actual kernel line per pair; it does not enlarge
the line into a plane. The pure orientation exception supplies all six
common columns and invokes the existing N8R2C consumer.

The live delta is N8R2K: every invertible physical edge must have an outsider
whose joint kernel is zero or a coordinate axis. This is a reusable
source-to-incidence implication beyond the completed common-column child.
It closes neither the supply parent nor the entire invertible-root branch.
It says nothing about the existence of an invertible edge in a witness.

The parent attempt also tested kernel-only pair exchange against an exact
off-source construction. Take Q=I, outsiders A_i,B_i, root incidences
W_1Ai=I, W_2Ai=E_ii, W_1Bi=E_ii, W_2Bi=I, and matrix-unit outside edges
containing three disjoint monochromatic perfect matchings. For a reproducible
exploratory choice, number the roots 0,1 and outsiders A0,A1,A2,B0,B1,B2 as
2,3,4,5,6,7. Use E00 on {23,45,67}, E11 on {24,36,57}, E22 on
{25,37,46}, and E01 on the remaining outside pairs oriented by increasing
vertex number. The pure coefficients are (3,3,4); apply
diag(1/3,1/3,1/4) at root 0. This exact rational choice has all pure
amplitudes one, maximum root cardinality two, all
vertex-colour column killers, and no common-coordinate pair anywhere;
the mixed coefficient of word 11000000 is 1/3. It is not a witness. At the base
pair every joint kernel is zero, so kernel restrictions alone cannot
supply the missing full-source contradiction. This exploratory control is
not a proof dependency of N8R2K.

The strictly sharper next source obligation retains zero or coordinate-axis
joint kernels. In particular, opposite invertible and matrix-unit legs
leave a full matrix of physical cofactors in the source. Discarding that
matrix, or substituting the common-column proof at those ports, would lose
the remaining load-bearing equations. Lower-rank root edges remain a
separate residual. The global conjecture is still UNRESOLVED.
