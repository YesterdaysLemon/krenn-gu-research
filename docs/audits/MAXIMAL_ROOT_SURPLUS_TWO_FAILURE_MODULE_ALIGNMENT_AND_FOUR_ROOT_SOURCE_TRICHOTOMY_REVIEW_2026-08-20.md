## Pointwise absorption and the radical--Fitting direction

The pointwise theorem has the right geometric quantifier. It works in the
Laurent localization `R_h`, hence only on the chart where every declared
factor is nonzero, and passes to an algebraic closure only for the geometric
point statement. It does not turn a generic `K(t)` membership statement into
a pointwise statement.

For each rank threshold `j`, adjoining the desired column changes the fibre
rank exactly when the zero sets of `I_j(A)` and `I_j([A|g])` differ. Equality
of all geometric radical ideals is therefore equivalent to absorption at
every geometric torus point. The saturation formula has the correct
direction and records the localization rather than silently dividing by a
chart factor.

The response-gated refinement also has the correct containment direction:

```text
Rho I_j([A|g]) subset sqrt_geom(I_j(A)).
```

If an augmented minor is nonzero on `V(I_j(A))`, the one-column augmentation
forces desired survival there; absence of a live response then kills every
response coordinate. Conversely, at a live survival fibre with nuisance rank
`a`, a nonzero `(a+1)`-minor of the augmentation contradicts the containment
unless all response coordinates vanish. No radical equality stronger than
this response-gated claim is inferred.

The strictness examples correctly separate three notions:

- pointwise fibre absorption;
- Laurent-module membership; and
- function-field membership.

Neither a constant kernel witness nor one sampled contraction is substituted
for the GLS2 function-field quantifier.
