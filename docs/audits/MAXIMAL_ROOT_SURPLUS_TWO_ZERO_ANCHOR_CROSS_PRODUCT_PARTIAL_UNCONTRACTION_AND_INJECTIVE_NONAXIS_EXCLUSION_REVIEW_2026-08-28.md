## Quantifier and ambient-domain audit

Fix one actual source witness (W), one promoted probe pair
(A=\{a_0,a_1\}), and the zero-anchor condition

```text
G_empty^A=W_(a_0,a_1)=0.
```

For a characteristic-zero source field (K) containing the fixed source
coefficients, the probe coordinates (z_0,z_1) are formal indeterminates and
all identities live in the ambient domain (K[z_0,z_1]).  The patched text
correctly states that specializations to rank and cross-product divisor fibres
come after the ambient identity, and that all domain deductions precede such
specialization.  No domain claim is being made in a potentially reducible
fibre quotient.

The target weights mu_c are explicit and nonzero.  Target evaluation therefore
gives

```text
sum_c mu_c z_(0,c) z_(1,c)
  product_(t outside S) (k_t)_c
  tensor_(s in S)e_(s,c)^*.
```

For the pure-axis multiplicity argument, the theorem correctly extends scalars
to the fraction field F of this ambient domain and quotients each pure slot by
the F-line generated by its active full-row covector a_p.  This is a linear
quotient after scalar extension, not a division by a source, deck, response,
or target coefficient.  Full row space makes every coordinate of a_p a
nonzero polynomial, so no fixed coordinate covector is killed by that quotient.

The setup explicitly introduces `z_0,z_1` as probe indeterminates, so the
ambient-polynomial quantifier is stated directly rather than inferred from a
later specialization sentence.

## Partial-uncontraction identity

For (S\subseteq\widehat B), evaluate every contracted label
(t\in\widehat B-S) at

```text
p_t=X_t(z_0,-),   q_t=Y_t(z_1,-),   k_t=p_t cross q_t,
```

so (p_t(k_t)=q_t(k_t)=0).  A pair companion (G_D^A) with
(D\not\subseteq S) is killed in every matching summand by one contracted
factor.  A pair with (D\subseteq S) is structurally retained.  The patched
text correctly qualifies "survives": a retained companion or complementary
deck may still vanish after evaluation.  Labelled tensor-factor order is
understood through the canonical permutation

```text
(tensor_D V_v^*) tensor (tensor_(S-D)V_v^*)
  ~= tensor_S V_v^*.
```

No source term is unexpectedly left over.  The top term is absent precisely
because the zero-anchor edge tensor is zero.  Thus the hierarchy, including
its one-open and two-open members, is a direct partial evaluation of one
same-graph identity, not a reconstruction from independent charts.

For (S=\{s,u\}), the only structurally retained pair is
(D=\{s,u\}).  In labelled slot order ((s,u)), its companion is exactly

```text
g_(su)=p_s tensor q_u + q_s tensor p_u.
```

The plus sign is correct because this is a permanent/matching sum.  No hidden
root--root term belongs to this grade-zero pair companion; the root--root
anchor is the separately omitted (G_empty^A) term.

## Polynomial support consequences

The one-open equation has independent basis tensors (e_{s,c}^*), so each
coefficient satisfies

```text
mu_c z_(0,c) z_(1,c)
  product_(t != s)(k_t)_c = 0.
```

Since (K[z_0,z_1]) is a domain and mu_c, (z_{0,c}), and (z_{1,c}) are
nonzero, every colour has at least two labels in

```text
Z_c={t : (k_t)_c is the zero polynomial}.
```

For an injective joint probe map, `GLS58` gives the two coordinate-axis shore
orientations.  A nonaxis label can lie in at most one (Z_c): equal
orientations at two colours force one shore to be zero and hence a pure
probe axis, while mixed orientations violate complementary projection
surjectivity.

With six nonaxis labels at root order three, the incidence count is exactly a
labelled `2+2+2` partition.  For (Z_c=\{s,u\}), the other two colour
components of the two-open RHS vanish, while

```text
lambda_c=mu_c z_(0,c) z_(1,c)
  product_(t notin {s,u})(k_t)_c
```

is a nonzero polynomial.  The complementary deck contraction is scalar in
this two-open case.  The ambient domain therefore implies both deck and
companion are nonzero and forces the companion to have only the `(c,c)`
coordinate.  Each of the four injective orientation pairs has a nonzero
off-diagonal coordinate, so the all-injective nonaxis branch is excluded.

## All pure-axis multiplicities

The new active-line quotient argument is sound.  Let P be the nonempty set
of pure-axis labels and U its complement, and let

```text
E_c={u in U : (k_u)_c is the zero polynomial}.
```

The E_c are pairwise disjoint by the injective nonaxis visibility lemma.  A
pure label p has an active full-row covector a_p, and every pair companion
whose pair meets P contains a_p as an explicit tensor factor (or is zero for
two same-type axes).  Therefore applying the quotient by F a_p in every pure
slot kills every source term whenever S contains P and has at most one U
label; a deck factor cannot evade a quotient acting on the separate companion
factor.

For |P|=1, the one-open double-cover already forces every E_c to be nonempty.
For |P|>=2, if some E_c were empty, the S=P equation has a nonempty weighted
coordinate diagonal on its right, while the active-line quotients kill its
entire source side.  The dual-functional construction in Lemma 7.2 shows
that any nonempty diagonal supported on at most three colours survives these
quotients: retain one chosen colour and assign each other colour to a distinct
pure slot.  The full variable covector has all coordinates nonzero, so the
required annihilating functionals exist.

Consequently all three E_c are nonempty.  They are disjoint, so |U|>=3 and
|P|<=3; with |U| equal to five, four, or three, some E_c is a singleton
{u}.  In the S=P union {u} equation the right side is the single nonzero pure
target tensor for colour c: the other colours have a nonaxis zero label
outside S.  Every retained pair meets P and is killed by the same active-line
quotients, while the target survives (for one pure slot by pi_p(e_(p,c)^*)
!=0, and for at least two by Lemma 7.2).  This contradiction excludes every
nonempty P.  Together with the nonaxis two-open contradiction, it excludes
the entire all-injective root-order-three branch.
