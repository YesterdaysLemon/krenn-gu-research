## Low-shore hostile audit

factor by `A` kills both the `a_s tensor Y_u(v)` term and `q`.  Fix any
nonzero `b_s`.  Every port slice then obeys

```text
pi(X_u(v)) tensor b_s
 in span{pi(e_i) tensor e_i:i=0,1,2}.
```

If `b_s(j)!=0`, comparison of the second-factor `e_j` coefficient forces

all residual--port and port--port columns span at most two dimensions.

For `H=1^perp`, write the nonzero off-diagonal vector of `q` as



most `2+1=3`, contradicting rank four.

This is a bound on the total complete labelled image.  It does not assert
that `sym(L,L)` itself is in `Delta+Kq`; physical membership can only shrink
its contribution.
