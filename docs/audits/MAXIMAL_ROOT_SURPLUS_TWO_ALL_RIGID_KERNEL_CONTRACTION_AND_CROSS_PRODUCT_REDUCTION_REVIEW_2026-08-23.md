## Boundary-kernel and uniformity audit

Fix a deficient label `n`, a nonzero `k in ker J_n`, and a colour `c` with
`k_c!=0`.  If every neighbour covector `W_(n,t)(k,-)` fails to be a nonzero
`c`-axis covector, the `GLS56` covector alternative supplies a vector at each
other label that kills that shore while retaining coordinate `c`.  Every
complete matching then dies: pairs containing `n` have zero joint-probe
companion, while every other complementary deck pairs `n` to one killed
neighbour.  The pure target coefficient remains nonzero.  This proves the
escape only on `supp(k)`; zero coordinates are deliberately excluded.

The initial hostile draft incorrectly identified the closure of

```text
{k:L_(nt)(k) in K^* e_c^*}
```

when that set can be empty.  Publication was rejected until the proof was
repaired.  The final proof discards empty sets, contains every remaining set
in the linear preimage `P_(t,c)`, and uses the finite-linear-subspace cover of
the nonempty open kernel slice.  A resulting `P_(t,c)=K_n` comes from a
nonempty original set, so its restricted shore is both pure and nonzero.
Finite hyperplane avoidance then gives simultaneous activation.  No closure
of an empty locally closed set is used.

For rigid deficient maps, the `GLS55` kernel classification gives exactly:

- rank one: a coordinate plane and two active colours;
- rank two: a projective kernel vector of support one or two;
- rank three: zero kernel, so the boundary theorem is silent.

The coordinate-row alignment/rank-raise dichotomy is ordinary annihilator
duality.  It does not imply nuisance absorption or projective
synchronization.

## One-kernel ten-deck audit

five companion terms containing that label.  The other ten pair companions

## Two-kernel graph and finite-theorem boundary

```text

D_uv=hW_uv+a_u tensor b_v+b_u tensor a_v.
```

Every original matching either uses `n--m` or sends `n,m` to two distinct
retained ports.  Expanding the unique retained-port edge in a six-vertex


## Cross-product and rank-fibre audit

For arbitrary probe vectors `z_0,z_1`, each polynomial vector

```text
k_t=X_t(z_0,-) cross Y_t(z_1,-)
```

kills both evaluated probe rows.  The complete identity and zero anchor give

```text
sum_c z_(0,c)z_(1,c) product_t(k_t)_c=0.
```

No cross product, rank minor, or coordinate factor is inverted, so every
rank-drop and cross-product divisor remains included.

On an injective joint map, all cross products vanish only on a pure-probe
axis.  A fixed cross coordinate vanishes identically exactly when one probe
rowspace lies on that coordinate axis and the other projects surjectively to
the complementary two-space.  The independent `F_5` audit exhausts all
`2,607` ordered injective rowspace pairs and all `7,821` coordinate cases.
Thus six injective labels have either a coordinate-axis shore covering each
colour product or at least two nonzero products participating in a genuine
polynomial cancellation.  Neither leaf is excluded.

The transverse all-rank-two result is a separate deficient profile, not a
subcase of the all-injective leaf.  For nonzero probe rowspaces spanning a
two-plane, every cross product is a nonzero scalar polynomial times its
fixed kernel vector.  The independent audit exhausts all
`31*43=1,333` such ordered `F_5` rowspace pairs and verifies the common kernel
line.  It also checks all `7^6=117,649` possible six-label kernel zero-mask
profiles: coefficientwise vanishing is exactly coverage of all three
coordinates by the six zero sets.
