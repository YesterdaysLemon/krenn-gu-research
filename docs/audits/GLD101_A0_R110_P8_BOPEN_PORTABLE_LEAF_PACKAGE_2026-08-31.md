## Two reconstruction routes

The primary route is
`claims/arbitrary-order/verify_four_root_torus_star_equal_leaf_h4_q6_a0_r110_p8_bopen_portable.py`.
It hash-pins and reuses the existing GLD101 quotient implementation, rebuilds
the nine actual minors from GLD71/GLD88, derives every R110 gate, compares the
entire deterministic certificate, and emits the canonical solver input.

The independent route is
`claims/arbitrary-order/audit_four_root_torus_star_equal_leaf_h4_q6_a0_r110_p8_bopen_portable.py`.
It imports only GLD71, locally transcribes the a=0 H4 chart, rebuilds the full
37 by 9 syndrome, computes the nine 7 by 7 minors directly, reduces their
coefficients modulo Q6 with an audit-local implementation, independently
derives R110 and its gates, and emits the same source hash.  It does not import
the primary verifier or its quotient arithmetic.
