   the full joint nuisance.**  Rejected.  GLS2 grades every companion of a
   surplus-two label `I` by `|I|=2+2p`.  Every label of order at least four
   has a root--root edge in every companion summand, so maximum-root
   evaluation kills every coefficient slice, not merely a chosen ledger.
   The surviving order-two labels are retained wholesale except for the
   desired label `S`.

2. **The residual-present desired label should survive the base evaluation.**
   Rejected.  Its deck label is `Q union S`, of order four, so its companion
   has exactly one root--root edge.  Equivalently GLS15 writes it as
   `Psi_C(R)`, and every summand contains `R_ij(x_i,x_j)=0`.

3. **`Psi_C(K^Q)` might differ from the complementary permanent by a factor

   eight respectively.

   The definition is the image of the complete joint nuisance.  All
   order-two labels `P!=S`, including `P=Q` and the `Q`--`U` cross labels,

   Rejected.  The theorem distinguishes the raw tensor `Pi_S(z_Q)` from its
