## 3. Two-residual atlas attack and repairs

The rank-factorization lemma is sound.  One complete cross block identifies
`g` on its first rank-two group and `rho(g)=Jg^(-T)J` on the second.  A third
mutually cross-observed rank-two group forces `rho(g)` to preserve `J`, hence
`g=rho(g) in O(J)`.  Pairing an extra port with one full-rank group then
identifies its two residual rows.

Hostile review found two genuine pre-repair countermodels:

1. an extra overlap port not observed against an identifying group could
   carry an undetected frame; and
2. a port shared by nonadjacent charts could disagree even when a chosen
   chart graph was a tree with vacuous holonomy tests.

The final theorem repairs both.  Every distinct pair of ports in an overlap
is observed, and the chart graph contains every nonempty pairwise overlap.
Thus trivial fundamental-cycle holonomies give path-independent chart gauges,
and agreement really defines one frame and covered edge family on the union.

The exact two-group control retains arbitrary `GL_2`/contragredient freedom.
The three-chart rational twist has nonidentity `O(J)` holonomy while every
chart is a complete physical response.  It is an abstract atlas boundary,
not the restriction of one graph and not a target detector.

## 5. Uniform bounded-window no-go

For each even `b`, the deck with empty coefficient one, coefficient `lambda`
at support `b+2`, and every other coefficient zero restricts to the zero-edge
physical deck on every window through `b`.  At a `(b+2)`-set the Euler--Wick
left side is nonzero and its right side is zero.  Cardinality makes the family
permutation invariant and restriction-natural.

The block-polarized response version uses three `b`-port charts with a common
`(b-1)`-port identifying core, common frame `[I_2 0]`, and identity
transitions.  The hidden mixed degree-`b+2` term vanishes on every subset of
size at most `b` but violates the physical degree-two/insertion recurrence.

This proves only ambient noncharacterization.  The mixed perturbation does
not assert nonzero/unit pure witness normalization, and the construction is
not on the full target locus.  A bounded certificate using target equations,
global generative equality, or a proved uniform structural-degree theorem
remains open.

boundary.  Its pair space and every four-mode product sensor have dimension

is one common permanent pullback, but not `P_6 -> Delta_3` and not graph data.
