## 1. Exact source and target

### Source locus

The physical source is an **actual hypothetical complex witness** in the scope
of M1/GLS2/GLS3:

- a ternary graph satisfying the complete GHZ coefficient equations;
- a maximum-cardinality fully supported torus root \(R\), where the maximum
  ranges over both vertex sets and torus vectors, with root size
  \(r\geq 3\);
- surplus two, so the outside set \(B\) has size \(r+2\);
- the resulting maximum-root incidence, blocker, local-concision, pure
  normalization, and complete uncontracted mixed deck data.

The presently committed physical reduction is over \(\mathbb C\).  GLS2 and
several later linear-algebraic implications are valid over characteristic-zero
fields after their incidence identities and nonzero hypotheses are supplied.
No package may silently upgrade the physical source quantifier from
\(\mathbb C\) to every characteristic-zero field.

### Required output interface

For every point of the source locus, on the same physical graph, prove one of:

1. **Useful-pair/attachment output.**  Produce a residual pair \(Q\) whose
   relevant order-two companion column survives modulo **all** relevant
   higher-order columns; a raw nonzero companion coefficient is not enough.
   If the named downstream theorem requires collective pair-observability or
   observability of the full fixed-\(Q\) coordinate family, prove that stronger
   condition as well.  On that same \(Q\), supply a
   legal nonzero target/response package in the full nuisance quotient.  The
   package must include exactly the hypotheses used by the downstream theorem
   being entered: constant-selector legality, nonzero response, augmented
   weights, synchronization, nuisance survival, target-pure anchoring, and,
   when the four-root detector is invoked, a legal \(l\) with
   \(l^{\mathsf T}Jp\neq0\) and
   \(l-\kappa p\in\ker U\).  All selectors and contractions must coexist on
   one graph and one \(Q\).

2. **Excluded failure output.**  Place the point in a proved exhaustive bad
   branch and derive a contradiction from complete mixed GHZ coefficients of
   that same graph.  Every declared nonzero coordinate, incidence weight,
   minor, amplitude, response, detector factor, or chart denominator must be
   handled by exact characteristic-zero saturation or by a separate divisor
   branch.

The node ends at this interface.  Permanent nonrestriction, downstream
extraction/gluing, and global-conjecture resolution are not part of it.

## 4. Minimal remaining branches

The all-pairs individual-supply failure branch is closed: the contracted
complete mixed target always produces at least one same pair with individual
order-two quotient survival and raw incidence.  The first missing edge is to
attach a complete legal response/target package to at least one such supplied
pair, or to exclude every attachment failure on the same graph.  If the
chosen downstream entry needs collective pair-observability or the full
fixed-\(Q\) coordinate family, those stronger ranks remain part of that edge.
A support-free proof must now cover:

1. for root order four, simultaneous pointwise failure of every target for
   every eligible \(Q\), expressed by the response-gated/pure-profile radical
   containments; the coarser R/A branches remain relevant only to the stronger
   all-seven GLD3 package, while C is separate only if a different downstream
   entry requires stronger pair-block observability;
2. all nuisance-rank-drop and observability-minor divisors, now expressible
   without rank-stratum enumeration by the radical--Fitting/incidence
   criteria;
3. response-identically-zero branches outside the already named conditional
   GLD subcells; at root order four the reviewed complete-fibre theorem
   excludes the literal all-seven/full-rank chart, while the reviewed
   determinant-divisor theorem reduces simultaneous six-pair zero to exact
   rank-two/rank-one cores and keeps the seventh quartic separate.  The
    reviewed follow-up excludes rank two and the singleton.  The reviewed
    two-port theorem routes Branch III to \(P_5\), while the reviewed
    contained/one-sided continuation routes Branches I/II to explicit pure
    compression, response-visible-selector, or augmented-face-defect
    interfaces.  Every weaker response-zero pattern remains;
4. joint selector ranks \(k=0,1,2\), common and unequal slopes, and all response
   visibility alternatives; GLS16 now sends every `k=0`, pure-`Z`, oblique,
   or unequal-line residue to an explicit swallowed base pair circuit, while
   GLS17 sends absence of pure `M` at any even target to all of its swallowed
   partial-root leading classes.  The common-line detector is denominator-free
   at arbitrary \(h\), so no residual-scalar divisor remains inside that
   conditional branch;
5. the detector's augmented-weight/alignment/anchor failures when that detector
   is the downstream entry;
6. at every root order, exclusion of the promoted response-zero/full-nuisance
   failure profiles, followed by the missing common-package/downstream-shape
   theorem satisfying every synchronization, alignment, activity, nuisance,
   and anchor hypothesis of a named committed downstream detector, especially
   for root order at least five.

The desired structural form is a companion-exchange/hafnian/Pluecker syzygy or
module identity which, after only legitimate saturation, places a nonzero pure
target coefficient in the module generated by complete mixed coefficients.
An exact countermodel instead revises this branch decomposition; it is not to
be hidden by adding support masks.

GLS15 now gives the exact class such a syzygy must control.  If target `T` has
absorbed physical direction `A_T=delta_T R-eta_T K^Q`, then in another
rank-one target `S`

```text
[Psi_(C_S)(A_T)]
 =(delta_T eta_S-eta_T delta_S)bar g_S.
```

Multiplying by an active selected diagonal value identifies this class with
the same projective determinant times the active pure GHZ class.  The next
step must prove foreign transport into `N_(C_S)^J` or contradict the resulting
nonzero pure class; generic raw injectivity of `Psi_C` does neither.

GLS16 adds the maximum-root base shadow.  If the base pair class of `S`
survives, then `k_S` is nonzero and a rank-one line is necessarily pure `M`.
Thus every rank-zero target and every oblique, pure-`Z`, or unequal-line
residue now carries a concrete order-two base-swallowing circuit.  A selector
for `T` also cross-annihilates both physical columns of every `S!=T`, but this
map has source `S-T` and target `T-S`; it is not the `S`-quotient membership
required by GLS15.  The next proof must use these exact circuits and cross
maps in the complete mixed equations, or force enough base classes to survive.

GLS17 supplies the adjacent-grade extension.  For `|S|=2t`, every partial-root
shadow leaving `t-1` roots open kills `g_S^Z` and sends `g_S^M` to an explicit
injection/permanent tensor.  Survival of any such class puts `(1,0)` in
`C_S`; absence of pure `M` forces all `binom(r,t-1)` shadows to be swallowed.
At `r=4`, this reduces the four-port synchronization failure to four exact
first-root absorptions, while the six pair failures remain the GLS16 base
circuits.  Complete mixed equations must now force one of these leading
classes or exclude their simultaneous absorption; activity is still separate.
