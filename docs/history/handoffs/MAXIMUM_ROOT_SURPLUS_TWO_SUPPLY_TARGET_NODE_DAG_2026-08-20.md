## 1. Exact source and target

### Source locus

The physical source is an **actual hypothetical complex witness** in the scope
of M1/GLS2/GLS3:

- a ternary graph satisfying the complete GHZ coefficient equations;
- a maximum-cardinality fully supported torus root \(R\), where the maximum
  ranges over both vertex sets and torus vectors, with root size
  \(r\geq 3\);
- surplus two, so the outside set \(B\) has size \(r+2\);
- the resulting maximum-root incidence, blocker, local-concision, pure
  normalization, and complete uncontracted mixed deck data.

The presently committed physical reduction is over \(\mathbb C\).  GLS2 and
several later linear-algebraic implications are valid over characteristic-zero
fields after their incidence identities and nonzero hypotheses are supplied.
No package may silently upgrade the physical source quantifier from
\(\mathbb C\) to every characteristic-zero field.

### Required output interface

For every point of the source locus, on the same physical graph, prove one of:

1. **Useful-pair/attachment output.**  Produce a residual pair \(Q\) whose
   relevant order-two companion column survives modulo **all** relevant
   higher-order columns; a raw nonzero companion coefficient is not enough.
   If the named downstream theorem requires collective pair-observability or
   observability of the full fixed-\(Q\) coordinate family, prove that stronger
   condition as well.  On that same \(Q\), supply a
   legal nonzero target/response package in the full nuisance quotient.  The
   package must include exactly the hypotheses used by the downstream theorem
   being entered: constant-selector legality, nonzero response, augmented
   weights, synchronization, nuisance survival, target-pure anchoring, and,
   when the four-root detector is invoked, a legal \(l\) with
   \(l^{\mathsf T}Jp\neq0\) and
   \(l-\kappa p\in\ker U\).  All selectors and contractions must coexist on
   one graph and one \(Q\).

2. **Excluded failure output.**  Place the point in a proved exhaustive bad
   branch and derive a contradiction from complete mixed GHZ coefficients of
   that same graph.  Every declared nonzero coordinate, incidence weight,
   minor, amplitude, response, detector factor, or chart denominator must be
   handled by exact characteristic-zero saturation or by a separate divisor
   branch.

The node ends at this interface.  Permanent nonrestriction, downstream
extraction/gluing, and global-conjecture resolution are not part of it.
